## Finding 1 — the Williams method is INHERENTLY WORST-CASE; the average-case regime is a SEPARATE (limited) step `[williams-lb-inherently-worst-case]` `[avg-case-needs-separate-amplification]`

Chen-Oliveira-Santhanam (CCC 2016/2017, "An Average-Case Lower Bound Against ACC⁰") directly address the worst-case↔average-case gap for the Williams method. Williams' original NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰ is a WORST-CASE lower bound (any poly-size ACC⁰ family fails on the hard function on a negligible fraction of inputs, infinitely often). **The crucial step that fails for average-case:** using the assumption NEXP ⊆ poly-size ACC⁰ to obtain SUCCINCT WITNESSES — this step "simply does not work if our assumption is that NEXP is in poly-size ACC⁰ ON AVERAGE rather than in the worst case." So the Williams algorithmic method's non-black-box-ness (Cycle 12) lives in the WORST-CASE CAPP→LB direction; it does NOT automatically extend to the average-case regime. Average-case requires a SEPARATE step: hardness amplification (via locally-list-decodable ECCs), starting from Williams' strengthened (NE∩coNE)/1 ⊄ ACC⁰ result (which preserves closure under amplification). `[avg-case-needs-separate-amplification]`: the worst-case→average-case transition is NOT a free extension — it is a distinct construction, and amplification itself has a black-box limitation (the search notes black-box MONOTONE hardness amplification cannot even amplify worst-case to 0.99 hardness, because monotone ECCs cannot have good distance — which is why the (NE∩coNE)/1 result is essential). So Chen-Tal-Wang's worst-case n^{2.5−ε} LB does NOT push to the average-case mining regime by a tweak; it needs the separate amplification step, which is itself limited. `[honest-ceiling]`: the "inherently worst-case / easy-witness step does not work for average-case" claim is from the search summary of Chen-Oliveira-Santhanam, search-level not PDF-line-verified; the monotone-amplification limitation is search-summary-level.

`[capp-implies-strong-avg-case]`: the ALTERNATE average-case route — Chen-Ren (STOC 2020, "Strong Average-Case Lower Bounds from Non-Trivial Derandomization"): a non-trivial CAPP algorithm for class C ⟹ strong (1/2 + 1/n^{ω(1)}) AVERAGE-CASE lower bounds for nondeterministic time classes against C, using techniques from Cryptography in NC⁰ (Applebaum-Ishai-Kushilevitz) and PCPs of Proximity with NC¹-computable proofs. This is the CAPP→average-case direction (the mining-relevant one, since the mining program IS a CAPP route `[majmaj-capp-route]`). Note: Chen-Tal-Wang's contribution IS a CAPP algorithm (for XOR-of-two THR∘THR) — so in principle Chen-Ren's CAPP→average-case theorem could apply, converting the Chen-Tal-Wang CAPP into a strong average-case LB. BUT the average-case class reached is a NONDETERMINISTIC time class (NQP / NEXP), with the hard function STRUCTURALLY DETERMINED (PCP-verifier witness, not NW-usable) — the same obstruction as the worst-case Williams LB (Finding 2 below). So even the CAPP→average-case route does not land in the NW-usable / general-MAJ∘MAJ mining regime; it lands in the "structurally-determined hard function, not a chosen explicit function" regime — the average-case analogue of the witness gap. `[honest-ceiling]`: Chen-Ren's CAPP→average-case implication is search-confirmed; the "lands in a nondeterministic-time / structurally-determined regime, not NW-usable" is a structural reading.

## Finding 2 — TWO SENSES of "non-black-box"; Cycle 12 conflated them; the Williams LB YIELDS a black-box-constructive distinguisher `[two-senses-of-non-black-box]` `[williams-lb-is-black-box-constructive]` `[cycle-12-walk-back]`

Cycle 12 (`[williams-method-is-non-black-box]`) claimed "the Williams algorithmic method IS non-black-box — it exploits internal ACC structure (SYM+), circumvents natural proofs via diagonalization, non-relativizing; black-box/oracle methods cannot see this structure — so Williams escapes the Fan-Li-Yang black-box barrier." Cycle 13 PARTIALLY WALKS THIS BACK by distinguishing two senses of "non-black-box":

- **Sense 1 — non-black-box PROOF METHOD:** the proof does not treat the circuit as a black box; it opens it up and exploits internal structure (SYM+ representation, diagonalization, non-relativizing). This is what the search summary meant by "black-box or oracle methods cannot see this structure." Cycle 12's claim is CORRECT on this sense: the Williams PROOF is non-black-box (and non-natural, by dropping largeness).
- **Sense 2 — black-box-constructive LB DISTINGUISHER:** the LOWER BOUND the method PRODUCES is EQUIVALENT to a BLACK-BOX CONSTRUCTIVE property — one decidable in randomized polylog(N) time WITH ORACLE ACCESS to the function (N=2ⁿ). This is Chen-Williams-Yang (ITCS 2023, `[cwy-2023-blackbox]` — ALREADY in the wiki from Cycles 3/8): "NEXP ⊄ C is equivalent to the existence of a black-box constructive property useful against C"; and "when the NEXP lower bound is proved via the ALGORITHMIC METHOD (CAPP algorithms), the advice can be removed, yielding coRTIME[polylog(N)]-constructive properties." So the Williams algorithmic LB specifically YIELDS a (randomized) black-box-constructive distinguisher.

These two senses are COMPATIBLE (the Williams method has BOTH: a non-black-box proof AND a black-box-constructive distinguisher) but they are on DIFFERENT axes. **Cycle 12 conflated them**: it inferred "the LB escapes Fan-Li-Yang" from "the proof is non-black-box (sense 1)." But Fan-Li-Yang's barrier is on the DISTINGUISHER axis (sense 2): Fan-Li-Yang's "tractable side" = "LB/derandomization techniques that imply efficient black-box algorithms distinguishing small circuits from random functions via ORACLE QUERERIES." That is precisely sense 2 — and Chen-Williams-Yang show the Williams LB yields a black-box (oracle-query) constructive property. So on the axis Fan-Li-Yang's barrier lives, the Williams LB is BLACK-BOX-constructive, NOT non-black-box. `[cycle-12-walk-back]`: the clean "Williams escapes Fan-Li-Yang because non-black-box" is QUALIFIED — the escape is from the natural-proofs LARGENESS axis (Williams is non-natural by dropping largeness), NOT from the black-box-DISTINGUISHER axis (where the LB is black-box-constructive per CWY2023).

`[honest-ceiling]`: the CWY2023 equivalence (NEXP ⊄ C ⟺ black-box constructive property; algorithmic-method LBs → coRTIME[polylog]-constructive) is search-confirmed and already recorded in the wiki as `[cwy-2023-blackbox]` (Cycles 3/8, on the S1.a recognizability axis: "black-box constructivity unavoidable for NEXP; Thm 1.6: no DETERMINISTIC polylog(N)-constructive property useful against AC⁰ — randomness essential"). So the walk-back is a RECONCILIATION with the wiki's own prior finding: CWY2023 was used on the S1.a recognizability axis (Cycles 3/8) to show constructivity is unavoidable; Cycle 13 re-uses it on the mining BLACK-BOX axis to show the Williams LB's distinguisher IS black-box-constructive. The two-senses distinction is a structural reading (my synthesis), not a theorem; whether sense 2 EXACTLY coincides with Fan-Li-Yang's "black-box LB technique" is the residual (Finding 3).

## Finding 3 — the residual open question: does a NON-LARGE black-box-constructive property trigger Fan-Li-Yang's PRF barrier? `[fly-largeness-residual-open]`

The walk-back is partial, not total, because a residual remains. Fan-Li-Yang's barrier combines (a) a black-box LB technique reaching the threshold wire count with (b) bootstrapping, and concludes the combination would DISPROVE PRFs (believed impossible). The Williams LB is black-box-constructive (sense 2, CWY2023) BUT NON-NATURAL (drops LARGENESS — sense 1's escape from RR). The residual question: **does Fan-Li-Yang's barrier require LARGENESS?** 
- If Fan-Li-Yang's barrier, like full Razborov-Rudich, requires LARGENESS (the distinguishing property holds for a large fraction of functions), then the Williams non-large black-box-constructive LB does NOT trigger it — the non-largeness is a genuine escape, and Cycle 12's "escape" stands (on the largeness sub-axis).
- If Fan-Li-Yang's barrier, like Ilango's recognizability barrier (Cycle 2, `[recognizable-stronger-than-natural]` — strictly stronger than RR, NO largeness required), does NOT require largeness, then the Williams non-large black-box-constructive LB DOES trigger it (a non-large black-box distinguisher at the threshold would disprove PRFs), and Cycle 12's "escape" fully fails.

PRF-breaking plausibly does NOT require largeness (a PRF distinguisher must distinguish the SPECIFIC PRF from random, not a large fraction of functions) — which weakly suggests Fan-Li-Yang's barrier may NOT require largeness (leaning toward the Williams LB triggering it). BUT this is a heuristic, not established; the search summaries do NOT clearly state whether Fan-Li-Yang's barrier requires largeness. `[fly-largeness-residual-open]`: this is an OPEN question, flagged, NOT resolved here — resolving it needs reading Fan-Li-Yang's barrier theorem precisely (does their "black-box LB technique" include non-large constructive properties, or only large natural-proof-style ones?), which is NOT PDF-line-verified in this cycle. This is the precise residual that determines whether the Williams/CWY black-box-constructive LB can cross the threshold or is Fan-Li-Yang-blocked.

`[honest-ceiling]`: the largeness-question framing is my structural reading; the "PRF-breaking plausibly doesn't require largeness" is a heuristic, flagged, not a theorem; whether Fan-Li-Yang's barrier requires largeness is NOT established by the search summaries — explicitly flagged as the residual requiring primary-source verification.

## Finding 4 — the within-quadrant push is NOT barrier-free (the net) `[within-quadrant-not-barrier-free]`

Combining Findings 1-3: pushing Chen-Tal-Wang (non-black-box proof, escapes n²-wall, worst-case, E^NP, XOR-of-two) toward the mining regime (average-case, threshold wire count, NW-usable, general-MAJ∘MAJ) is NOT barrier-free:

1. **The average-case axis (Finding 1):** the worst-case→average-case transition is a SEPARATE, limited step (Williams is inherently worst-case; the easy-witness step "does not work" for average-case; average-case needs hardness amplification, which has its own black-box-monotone limitation, OR the Chen-Ren CAPP→average-case route, which lands in a nondeterministic-time / structurally-determined regime, NOT NW-usable). So the average-case mining regime is NOT a free extension of Chen-Tal-Wang's worst-case technique.
2. **The black-box-distinguisher axis (Findings 2-3):** Cycle 12's "Williams escapes Fan-Li-Yang" is PARTIALLY WALKED BACK. The Williams/Chen-Tal-Wang LB yields a black-box-CONSTRUCTIVE distinguisher (CWY2023, sense 2), which is the axis Fan-Li-Yang's barrier is on. The two senses of "non-black-box" (proof method vs LB distinguisher) were conflated in Cycle 12. Whether the Williams non-large black-box-constructive LB triggers Fan-Li-Yang's PRF barrier is the OPEN residual (Finding 3) — possibly YES (if FLY needs no largeness, like Ilango's barrier), possibly NO (if FLY needs largeness, like full RR).

So the within-quadrant push reintroduces a barrier on the average-case axis (separate, limited amplification step) AND re-opens the black-box question on the distinguisher axis (Cycle 12 walked back). The honest net: the target quadrant is inhabited (Chen-Tal-Wang, Cycle 12), but the push from the inhabited corner to the mining regime is NOT a free slide — it crosses two further sub-barriers. **Refined genuine-open shape:** a method that (a) is non-black-box on the PROOF axis (escapes natural-proofs largeness, as Williams does) AND (b) reaches the AVERAGE-CASE mining regime (not via the limited worst-case→amplification step, and into an NW-usable / general-MAJ∘MAJ function, not a structurally-determined NEXP witness) AND (c) either is non-black-box on the DISTINGUISHER axis OR is a black-box-constructive property that does NOT trigger Fan-Li-Yang (the largeness residual). This is STRICTER than Cycle 12's "non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall."

`[honest-ceiling]`: the within-quadrant-not-barrier-free synthesis is a structural reading; the two sub-barriers (average-case amplification; distinguisher black-box-constructive) are each search-grounded but their COMBINATION as "the push is not free" is my synthesis. No breakthrough; no impossibility claimed (the push is hard and barrier-guarded, not proven impossible — the largeness residual is genuinely open either way).

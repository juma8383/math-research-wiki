## The bridge, precisely `[chen-2018-maxip]` (Chen, ITCS 2019, arXiv:1805.10698)
An `n²·poly(d) / log^{ω(1)} n` algorithm for **ℓ₂-Furthest-Pair** (or Z-Max-IP / Z-OV=Hopcroft / Bichromatic-ℓ₂-Closest-Pair / Weighted-Max-IP) for **polylogarithmic d** ⟹ **NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR**. Equivalently, an `n² / log^{ω(1)} n` algorithm for **Max-IP** with dimension **d = n^ε** (small constant ε) ⟹ same. The mechanism: Chen's two **structure lemmas** decompose every poly-size THR∘THR into Gap-OR∘THR∘MAJ (poly fan-in) / DOR∘MAJ∘MAJ (2^{O(εn)} fan-in), reducing satisfiability of these to Max-IP / Z-OV instances; a fast enough instance algorithm feeds the Williams easy-witness lemma. **The key structural reason this is the highest-leverage target:** the bridge needs only a **polylog shave** (`/log^{ω(1)} n`), while SETH-based fine-grained hardness rules out only **polynomial** improvements (`n^{2−ε}`) — the bridge sits *exactly* in the regime hardness leaves open.

## Verified state of the art
- **Max-IP / Min-IP at d = c log n:** `n^{2 − 1/Õ(√c)}` `[acw-maxip]` (Alman-Chan-Williams, polynomial method — low-degree probabilistic polynomials over F₂); OV: `n^{2 − 1/O(log c)}` (Abboud-Williams-Yu / Chan-Williams). For **fixed c** these are *constant-exponent* (polynomial) improvements — yet the bridge remains **unproven**, so the exponent `1/Õ(√c)` is too small to survive the Williams/Chen reduction blowups. (The exact mechanism of the failure is inferred, not verified against the reduction; marked plausible.)
- **At polylog d = log^k n (k≥2):** the best-known `n^{2 − 1/O(d)}` collapses to **≈ n²** (the improvement exponent `1/O(log^k n)` gives `n² / 2^{(log n)^{1−k}/O(1)}` → n²); at d = log n it is n²/constant. Word-RAM bit-packing gives only **O(1)** log factors (one log, via packing d/log n bits/word). **The bridge needs an unbounded polylog shave; known techniques give a constant number of logs at best.**
- **At d = n^ε:** "naive" `n²·polylog(n)` via fast rectangular matrix multiplication — on the *wrong* side (slower than n² by a polylog factor).
- **True-subquadratic equivalence** `[chen-williams-ov-equiv]` (Chen-Williams, SODA 2019): OV / Min-IP / Max-IP / Exact-IP / Apx-Min/Max-IP / approximate ℓ_p closest & furthest pair are all `n^{2−ε}`-equivalent. **But this equivalence concerns *polynomial* improvements; a polylog shave is below its radar** — being "truly subquadratic equivalent" does not transfer a polylog shave.
- **Tight approximation lower bound** `[chen-2020-maxip-tight]` (Chen, ToC 2020 / CCC 2018): under SETH, an `n^{2−Ω(1)}`-time multiplicative t-approx of Max-IP exists **iff** `t = (d/log n)^{Ω(1)}`, and an additive t-approx exists **iff** `t = Ω(d)`. **An additive o(d) approximation in subquadratic time refutes SETH.** Exact Z-Max-IP is hard in dimension as low as `2^{O(log* n)}` (recursive CRT dimensionality self-reduction).

## The shave attempt — five routes, each blocked for a specific reason `[maxip-shave-obstruction]`
1. **Word-RAM bit-packing** → O(1) log factors (one log via d/log n bits/word). Needs ω(1) logs. **Blocked: gives a constant, not unbounded, number of logs.**
2. **Polynomial method** `[acw-maxip]` → `n^{2−1/Õ(√c)}` at d = c log n, but the exponent collapses to ~0 at polylog/n^ε d and is (inferred) too small to drive the LB reduction even where nonzero. **Blocked: exponent too small / collapses in the bridge's target regime.**
3. **Rectangular matrix multiplication** → `n²·polylog(n)` at d = n^ε, on the wrong side. **Blocked: slower than n².**
4. **Approximate-then-round-to-exact** — get an additive-Ω(d) approx in subquadratic time (achievable) then round the last "factor d" to exact. **Blocked by SETH itself** `[chen-2020-maxip-tight]`: an additive o(d) approx in subquadratic time *refutes SETH*. The exact shave would require exactly the SETH-forbidden additive-o(d) rounding step. (Gap amplification / coordinate sampling to close a factor-d gap needs > d² samples per pair → no gain; tensoring vectors scales d with the IP — ratio unchanged.)
5. **CRT dimensionality reduction** (Chen 2020's recursive CRT) → powers the *lower bound* (hardness down to dim 2^{O(log* n)}), but max is not a modular-friendly operation, so CRT does not recombine to an *algorithm*. **Blocked: asymmetric to algorithms.**

**Net honest finding:** the exact-Max-IP / ℓ₂-Furthest-Pair polylog shave is **not merely open** — the two most natural routes (approximation-rounding and the polynomial method) are blocked for the specific reasons above, and the approximation-rounding route is *provably* SETH-hard. The wiki's prior framing "only the algorithm is missing" is **true but optimistic**; the corrected framing is "the natural SAT-side routes are blocked; the live opening is on the *derandomization* side."

## The reframed live target `[majmaj-capp-route]` (Chen-Williams, CCC 2019) `[chen-williams-2019]`
Chen-Williams ("Stronger Connections Between Circuit Analysis and Circuit Lower Bounds, via PCPs of Proximity," CCC 2019) **tightened the bridge to derandomization**: **NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR** also follows from a non-trivial **CAPP** (or SAT) algorithm for poly-size **THR∘MAJ**, **or** a `1/poly(n)`-error CAPP for poly-size **MAJ∘MAJ** — i.e., a *derandomization* (estimating acceptance probability) suffices, not a SAT algorithm. PCPs of Proximity let the requirement drop from `OR_{poly(n)}∘AND3∘C` to `AND2∘C / OR2∘C / ⊕2∘C`.

**Why this reframing is the real opening:** the exact-Max-IP approximation lower bounds (`[chen-2020-maxip-tight]`) bound *search/approximation* of Max-IP; they do **not** bound a *derandomization* (CAPP/PRG) for MAJ∘MAJ. MAJ∘MAJ is a **restriction** of THR∘THR (majorities, not general thresholds), and the question "does MAJ∘MAJ admit a non-trivial CAPP / a PRG that fools it with seed < N" is **strictly weaker and not covered by the SETH Max-IP results**. Concretely:
- The `[n2-wall]` obstruction (THR∘THR composed approximate degree Θ√(mt), forcing ≥2ⁿ monomials at n² wires) applies to **single-polynomial representations of THR∘THR** and to the SAT side. MAJ gates, while still Θ√t approximate degree, carry extra **symmetry / low-weight structure** that general thresholds lack; whether this admits a PRG/derandomization the general class doesn't is **open** and is the sharpened target.
- A positive CAPP for MAJ∘MAJ (1/poly error, non-trivial) ⟹ NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR by `[chen-williams-2019]` — a *derandomization*, the empirically fruitful side (cf. the de-conditioning trajectory on the MCSP page).

**The concrete open question (the next push):** *Does MAJ∘MAJ admit a non-trivial PRG / CAPP derandomization exploiting its majority-symmetry, in a way general THR∘THR does not?* Resolve positively ⟹ NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR (the first lower bound past the n² wall). Resolve negatively (a PRG lower bound for MAJ∘MAJ) ⟹ a new hardness-of-derandomization result. **Either outcome is publishable** — the target is genuinely falsifiable in both directions, like the GCT n=4,5 experiment.

**Adjacent lever** `[chen-tell-2019]` (Chen-Tell, STOC 2019, "Bootstrapping... Just Beyond Known Lower Bounds"): even **mild** quantitative improvements of *known* TC⁰ lower bounds or derandomization results imply super-polynomial TC⁰ lower bounds; the gap reduces to the precise constant c > 1 in the wire bound `n^{1 + c^{-d}}`. So even a *constant-factor* improvement to the right derandomization quantity bootstraps — lowering the bar on the MAJ∘MAJ CAPP route yet further.

## Critic (honest-ceiling, researcher-applied)
- **What the breakthrough is NOT:** this push produced **no** lower bound, **no** NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR proof, **no** polylog shave algorithm. The exact-Max-IP shave remains open; we only *sharpened* why the natural routes fail and *redirected* the target.
- **Overclaim corrected:** the prior wiki framing "only the algorithm is missing" is downgraded to "the natural SAT-side routes are blocked (one provably, by SETH); the live opening is the strictly-weaker MAJ∘MAJ derandomization route." The optimism was real (the regime is SETH-open), but the obstruction analysis shows the SAT side is harder than "missing" suggests.
- **Unverified attributions carried forward:** the *mechanism* by which the existing `n^{2−1/Õ(√c)}` algorithm fails the LB reduction is inferred (plausible), not verified against Chen's structure-lemma blowups — flagged. The MAJ-vs-THR "symmetry admits a PRG" is a *proposed open question*, not a result.
- **Transferable progress:** (1) the precise obstruction map (5 routes, each with a specific block, one provably SETH-hard); (2) the reframing of the target from exact-Max-IP shave (SAT side, partly SETH-blocked) to MAJ∘MAJ CAPP derandomization (weaker, uncovered by the SETH Max-IP bounds); (3) the falsifiable-in-both-directions question, adjacent to the Chen-Tell bootstrap lever. This is a genuine sharpening of the highest-leverage concrete target — honest progress, not a breakthrough.

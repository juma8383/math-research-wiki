## Findings

### F1. The lever — Gurevich's conjecture ⟹ P ≠ NP `[logic-for-p-implies-pneqnp]`
Gurevich (1988) asked whether a **logic captures P** on the class of *all* finite structures (isomorphism-closed, including **unordered** — the hard case, since on ordered structures FO+LFP captures P by Immerman-Vardi). He conjectured the **negative**: no such logic exists. **The implication:** Gurevich's conjecture (no logic for P) ⟹ P ≠ NP. Mechanism (per Dahan-Dawar 2020, who stress it is NOT a trivial corollary of Fagin): if P = NP then the polynomial hierarchy collapses and in particular there is a **poly-time canonical labelling** of graphs; poly-time canonization ⟹ a logic for P (Gurevich 1997: PTIME-computable complete invariants ⟹ PTIME canonization ⟹ a logic effectively capturing PTIME). Contrapositive: **no logic for P ⟹ P ≠ NP.** The **converse is open** — it is consistent with current knowledge that P ≠ NP yet a logic for P exists. (Equivalent formulation, Dawar 1995: a logic for P exists ⟺ a complete problem for P under first-order reductions exists.) This is a **genuinely different conclusion shape**: the statement is "no recursive presentation of the P-computable isomorphism-invariant classes exists" — a meta/descriptive non-existence statement, NOT "exhibit a hard function" — so, like the measure pivot, it **sidesteps the `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` explicit-construction lock in its CONCLUSION.**

### F2. The genuinely new structural fact — relativization escape (FIRST in 24 cycles) `[descriptive-escapes-relativization]`
**Dahan-Dawar 2020** construct an oracle A such that **there is a logic for P^A but P^A ≠ NP^A.** This shows the implication "logic-for-P ⟹ P=NP" (the converse of the lever) is **FALSE in relativized worlds** — the two statements (logic-for-P, P=NP) are independent under relativization. Equivalently: the lever "no-logic-for-P ⟹ P≠NP" is a **non-relativizing** theorem (its relativized analog fails). Consequence: **any P≠NP proof that goes through "no logic for P" is automatically non-relativizing** — the relativization barrier `[three-barriers-required]` is circumvented *for free* by the surface, not by a clever extra trick. **This is the first surface in 24 absolute cycles to escape the relativization barrier.** Every prior surface was explicitly relativization-blocked: (A)/S1.a needs a non-relativizing proof (open requirement); the mining face needs one; the measure surface inherits relativization (oracles both ways, `[measure-relativization-barrier]`); the disjoint-pairs target is relativization-blocked (`[disjoint-pairs-relativize]`). The descriptive/logic-for-P surface is the sole exception. Caveat: it escapes **one** of the three barriers (relativization); natural-proofs and algebrization still apply to the *proof* (see F5).

### F3. The catch — the active frontier targets a WEAKER question `[cpt-separator-not-pneqnp]`
The active descriptive-complexity frontier is the **separator program**: find a query in P not definable in the strongest candidate logic, separating that candidate from P. State of play:
- **FP+C** (fixed-point logic with counting): ruled OUT by **CFI 1992** (Cai-Fürer-Immerman) — a P-query (the CFI gadget query over Z₂) not definable in C^k for any fixed k ⟹ FP+C ≠ P. The CFI pairs (G_n,H_n) are non-isomorphic yet C^k-equivalent for k=o(|V|), distinguishable in poly-time.
- **Rank logic (FP+rk)**: ruled OUT by **Lichter 2022** (generalized CFI over rings Z_{2^i}) — the linear-algebraic approach to capturing P is closed.
- **CPT** (Choiceless Polynomial Time, Blass-Gurevich-Shelah 1999): the leading OPEN candidate; **CPT = P is open**. Dawar-Richerby-Rossman 2008: CPT solves CFI on *ordered* base graphs; Pakusa et al. 2018 extended to preordered/linear-degree; **CFI on fully *unordered* graphs is OPEN** — the key separator candidate.
- **CPT+WSC** (witnessed symmetric choice, Lichter-Schweitzer 2022): open; captures P on a class iff it defines isomorphism on that class (reduces capture to defining isomorphism).
- **Pago (2021/2023)**: CPT *cannot* define the needed fine preorders in hypercubes (CSL 2021) — the known preorder-based CPT algorithms fail on unordered CFI over hypercubes; reduced the separator to symmetric-circuit / proof-complexity lower bounds (F4/F5).

**The crucial honest point:** separating **one** candidate (CPT, or CPT+WSC) from P is a major result, but it is **NOT** the Gurevich conjecture and **does NOT imply P ≠ NP** — Gurevich needs *every* logic to fail, a universal negative. The active frontier (CFI/CPT/Pago) attacks CPT≠P, a strictly weaker target than the P≠NP lever. **The P≠NP-relevant version (the full Gurevich conjecture) is not what the field is currently attacking head-on** `[logic-for-p-harder-not-easier]`: it is a universal negative — prove no recursive presentation of P exists — plausibly **harder** than (A)'s explicit-construction problem, not easier. So the descriptive surface sidesteps the construction lock in its *conclusion* but the P≠NP-relevant lever is, if anything, harder.

### F4. A SECOND independent bridge to the proof-complexity face `[descriptive-bridges-to-proof-complexity]`
**Pago CSL 2023:** if CPT distinguishes all non-isomorphic graphs in a class K, then so does the **degree-3 extended polynomial calculus (EPC3)** with polynomial-size refutations. Therefore a **super-polynomial EPC3 lower bound for graph isomorphism** on CFI-graphs or multipedes (both **in P**, explicitly constructible) would **separate CPT from P.** This is a direct **bridge from the descriptive-complexity surface to propositional proof complexity** — the **second** independent bridge to that face, after Cycle 24's measure→disjoint-pairs→canonical-pairs (Razborov) bridge. Two genuinely different surfaces (measure/dimension and descriptive/logic-for-P) now both converge on the proof-complexity face as their lower-bound far end. This re-confirms the Cycle-24 finding that proof complexity is a *convergence point* of multiple surfaces, and strengthens the structural reading that the proof-complexity face is a natural hub — while also re-confirming `[proof-complexity-face-barriered]` (the far end is itself open/blocked: EPC3 / Frege / Extended-Frege lower bounds for GI are open).

### F5. The re-collapse — to weak-model lower bounds, in a DIFFERENT (instance-free) form `[descriptive-re-collapse-weak-model-lb]`
The descriptive surface's *proof* re-collapses to lower bounds against **weak models**, not to (A)'s explicit-balanced-point construction:
- **Symmetric XOR-circuit lower bounds** (Pago MFCS 2023): if a CFI-symmetric CPT algorithm solves CFI on a base-graph family, then certain polynomial-size **symmetric Boolean XOR-circuit** families must exist; Pago proved *near-nonexistence* for fully-symmetric circuits over hypercubes with bounded orbit-wise fan-in/fan-out (a gap remains).
- **EPC3 lower bounds** for the explicitly-given CFI/multipede families (F4).

**The shape is genuinely different from `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`:** the hard instance is **EXPLICIT and freely given** — the CFI graphs and multipedes are in P, constructible by a known polynomial-time procedure (CFI gadget construction over Z₂). So the **construction lock is DROPPED** — this is the first surface where the "exhibit a hard instance" half of the witness barrier is satisfied for free. The open part is the **lower bound against the weak model** (symmetric XOR-circuits / EPC3), for an instance that is already in hand. This is closer to the *mining* face's shape (convert an existing structure into a lower bound) but for a much weaker, more structured model — and the lower bound is the sole open task, not paired with a construction task.

**Whether natural-proofs / locality applies here is itself open** — the natural-proofs barrier is formulated for *general* circuit classes; **symmetric circuits** (invariant under an automorphism group, e.g. Sym_n for hypercubes) are a restricted, highly-structured class, and whether a natural-proofs-style barrier extends to symmetric-circuit lower bounds is not established in the literature the search surfaced. The `[locality-barrier]` (CHOPRS) re-surfaces via the 4th search but in the hardness-magnification/MCSP context (a different route), not directly as a barrier on the symmetric-circuit LB. So this is a genuine open question, not a known block — a sliver of light, honestly flagged as unmapped, not as an escape.

## Honest scope
NO BREAKTHROUGH. The descriptive/logic-for-P surface is the most genuinely *different* surface encountered in the sixth loop: it is the **first to escape the relativization barrier** (F2), and its *conclusion* sidesteps the explicit-construction lock (F1). But: (a) the P≠NP-relevant lever (full Gurevich conjecture) is a universal negative *harder* than the active separator frontier and plausibly harder than (A) (F3); (b) the *proof* re-collapses to weak-model lower bounds (symmetric circuits / EPC3) that **bridge to proof complexity** (F4, the second such bridge) and to restricted-circuit LBs (F5); (c) the construction lock is dropped (instance is free) — a real shape difference — but the weak-model LB is the open part, and whether natural-proofs/locality bars symmetric-circuit LBs is itself open (F5). Re-confirms `[no-missed-connection-moved-wall]` from a FOURTH sub-surface (structural → measure → disjoint-pairs/proof-complexity → descriptive/symmetric-circuits+EPC3), but with a genuinely new structural product: the **relativization escape** and the **instance-free weak-model-LB shape**, neither of which cleanly re-collapses to `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` in its standard (A)-form. (A) remains the single live thread (OPEN construction); the descriptive surface is a genuinely-different-shape FIFTH route that escapes one barrier and bridges to the proof-complexity convergence point, but does not solve P≠NP. `[honest-ceiling]` upheld.

## Net
The descriptive-complexity pivot's load-bearing new facts: (1) **relativization escape** — the logic-for-P lever is non-relativizing (Dahan-Dawar 2020 oracle), the first surface in 24 cycles to circumvent the relativization barrier for free; (2) **instance-free weak-model-LB shape** — the hard instance (CFI/multipedes) is explicit and in P, so the construction lock is dropped, leaving only a lower bound against a weak model (symmetric XOR-circuits / EPC3); (3) **second bridge to proof complexity** (Pago EPC3, alongside Cycle-24's Razborov canonical-pairs bridge) — proof complexity is a multi-surface convergence hub. Tempered by: the P≠NP-relevant lever (full Gurevich conjecture) is a universal negative harder than the active CPT-separator frontier (which reaches only CPT≠P, not P≠NP), and the weak-model LBs are themselves open (whether natural-proofs/locality bars symmetric-circuit LBs is open).

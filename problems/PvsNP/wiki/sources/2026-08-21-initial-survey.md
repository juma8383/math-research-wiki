# Source: Initial P vs NP survey (pre-workflow)

**Provenance:** The framing survey produced in-conversation before the fan-out workflow — the barriers primer, the two barrier-immune programs, the genuine attempt on Williams' door, the "barrier-treadmill" meta-observation, and the honest recommendation. Superseded in detail by [[2026-08-21-pnp-workflow]] but kept as the source of the framing concepts.

## Extracted claims

- `[three-barriers-required]` — Any proof of P≠NP must be non-relativizing `[bgs-1975]`, non-natural `[rr-1997]`, AND non-algebrizing `[aw-2008]`. The set of known techniques satisfying all three is tiny.
- `[barrier-treadmill]` — Meta-observation: each breakthrough technique spawns a new barrier (diagonalization→relativization; arithmetization→algebrization; combinatorial bounds→natural proofs). Reframes "prove P≠NP" as "characterize the boundary of constructive proof."
- `[two-live-programs]` — Only two programs are designed to dodge all three barriers: the Williams algorithmic method `[williams-2011]` and Geometric Complexity Theory (see [[gct]]). Both slow, neither killed.
- `[honest-ceiling]` — No brainstorm/session can produce a correct proof; the honest deliverable is a map + recommendation, not a theorem. (Carried forward as the working contract for this wiki.)

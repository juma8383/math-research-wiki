# Cycle 11 — the mining face's lever is real and quantified, but guarded by the black-box natural proof barrier; the two faces have different geometry

## The question Cycle 11 set out to answer

The third loop's synthesis (sources/2026-08-23-third-loop-synthesis.md) compressed the S1.a (meta-complexity / DNF) face to a 1-D "balanced point of two tensioned lifting-intrinsic conditions" barrier and named a forward pointer `[mining-face-forward-pointer]`: the symmetric Williams/mining (MAJ∘MAJ) face was NOT revisited in the third loop; is ITS wall compressible to a one-linchpin structure, and does its near-term relaxation seam (Tell's quantified derandomization = the CSS16 wire frontier) offer a lever the S1.a face lacks? Cycle 11 tests both halves of that pointer. Sources: the prior mining-face work (novel-diagnoses §8/§10/§11; williams-algorithmic page, the four-pass mining investigation) + web-verified primary literature (Goldreich-Wigderson STOC 2014; Tell STOC 2018 / CCC 2017; Chen-Tell STOC 2019; Chen-Jin-Williams STOC 2020; Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022). `[honest-ceiling]`: the literature findings are web-confirmed (search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified); the structural comparison is a reading of the prior wiki sources + the search summaries.

## Finding 1 — the S1.a compression lens transfers only PARTIALLY: the mining wall is structurally different `[mining-face-structurally-different]`

Applying the third-loop's "balanced point of two tensioned conditions" lens to the mining face: the mining wall (novel-diagnoses §11, the fifth pass) is a **two-dimensional gap** `[mining-gap-two-dimensional]` — correlation-strength (need exponential 2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) × wire-regime (need full poly-size) — blocked by a **single mechanism** (boosting/aggregation: the top majority aggregates weak per-gate correlations into a strong learner, `[threshold-aggregation-obstruction]`; low per-gate correlation does NOT bound MAJ∘C correlation). The known data points fall short on BOTH dimensions: CSS16 Parity is wire-tight at n^{1.5} (the wrong function past n^{1.5}); CSS16 Generalized Andreev is subexponential (exp(−n^{ε_d})) at n^{1+ε_d} wires.

- **The two conditions ARE two-dimensional** (strength × regime), like S1.a's (expensive ∧ small-gap). So at the coarse level both faces are "an explicit function satisfying two conditions simultaneously."
- **BUT the internal geometry differs.** S1.a's two conditions are in a **non-trivial, lifting-intrinsic tension** (Ilango §1.3, verbatim: "as g gets more expensive [the gap] also gets larger") — a property of the Lupanov construction, and the balanced point (Lupanov's near-optimal CNF yielding small-gap AND random-like hardness from ONE structural feature) is the non-trivial content. The mining face's two conditions are in a **banal monotonicity** (more wires ⟹ more computing power ⟹ the function gets easier ⟹ correlation grows) — true for ANY function — PLUS the non-trivial **boosting mechanism** (the single mechanism, not a tension between two conditions). The mining wall is "a 2-D gap blocked by one mechanism," not "a balanced point of two tensioned conditions." `[mining-face-structurally-different]`: the S1.a compression lens (2-D balanced point with tension) transfers only at the coarse "two conditions" level; the internal obstruction geometry is different (tension vs boosting-mechanism). The unification `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` holds at the "explicit witness construction" level, NOT at the internal-structure level — the two faces are NOT mirror images.

## Finding 2 — the Tell lever IS real: TC⁰/MAJ∘MAJ is on the STRONG side of the Goldreich-Wigderson dichotomy; quantified derand at threshold ⟹ standard derand ⟹ NEXP ⊄ TC⁰ `[tell-quantified-derand-is-a-real-lever]` `[tc0-extractor-enables-error-reduction]`

The williams-algorithmic page recorded that Tell's quantified derandomization is "the tractable seam — it lowers the witness requirement rather than meeting it," but did NOT settle whether the quantified (promise) derandomization, even if achieved, yields the mining payoff (NEXP ⊄ TC⁰ / THR∘THR), which Chen-Ren Thm 1.13 obtains only from a FULL CAPP. Cycle 11 resolves this: **the quantified derandomization of TC⁰/MAJ∘MAJ IS a real lever — it DOES yield the lower bound — but only at or beyond a threshold wire count.**

- **The Goldreich-Wigderson dichotomy** `[goldreich-wigderson-dichotomy]` (Goldreich-Wigderson STOC 2014): quantified derandomization (parameterized by a "badness" B(n) — derandomize circuits that err on ≤ B(n) inputs) relates to standard derandomization by a CLASS-DEPENDENT dichotomy. For SUFFICIENTLY STRONG classes (e.g. AC⁰[2]) that can compute extractors + approximate majority for error reduction, quantified derandomization at subexponential B(n) is **AS HARD AS standard derandomization** — it does NOT bypass the need for circuit lower bounds; it is equivalent in difficulty to standard derandomization, which itself requires LB breakthroughs. For WEAK classes (AC⁰), quantified derandomization is strictly easier than standard (solvable for all subexponential B).
- **TC⁰/MAJ∘MAJ is on the STRONG side — Tell constructs the enabling extractor.** `[tc0-extractor-enables-error-reduction]` Tell (STOC 2018, "Quantified Derandomization of Linear Threshold Circuits") proves the reduction himself: he constructs a **seeded extractor computable by a TC⁰ circuit with super-linear wires (n^{1+O(1/d)})** — the FIRST seeded extractor specific to TC⁰ (via weak combinatorial designs a la Raz-Reingold-Vadhan reducing Trevisan's extractor seed to ~1.01·log n, ε-balanced error-correcting codes in sparse TC⁰, batch computation). This extractor is the error-reduction bridge. **Tell's headline (his own theorem): a modest improvement in the quantified-derandomization wire threshold — from n^{1+exp(−d)} (his algorithm) to n^{1+O(1/d)} wires — would yield a non-trivial algorithm for STANDARD derandomization of all of TC⁰, implying NEXP ⊄ TC⁰.** So MAJ∘MAJ (depth-2 threshold ⊆ TC⁰) falls on the strong side of the dichotomy BECAUSE Tell supplies the TC⁰ extractor; quantified derand at threshold ⟹ standard derand ⟹ (via Williams) NEXP ⊄ TC⁰ (and via the CAPP-equivalence `[majmaj-symmetry-deflated]`, NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR). `[tell-quantified-derand-is-a-real-lever]`: the third-loop forward pointer is CONFIRMED — the mining face has a lever the S1.a face lacks.
- **The lever is QUANTITATIVE, not qualitative** `[wire-frontier-quantitative-gap]`. Tell HAS the quantified-derand algorithm at n^{1+exp(−d)} wires (below threshold, time n^{O(log log n)²}); the breakthrough is to push the wire count to n^{1+O(1/d)}. This is a MEASURABLE, INCREMENTAL gap with a known algorithm already in hand below the threshold — fundamentally different from S1.a's (A), a qualitative all-or-nothing construction (an explicit balanced-point function) with no partial-progress metric. This is the genuine structural advantage of the mining face: it admits a quantitative frontier with an algorithm on one side and a precise target on the other.

## Finding 3 — but the crossing route is guarded by a NEW barrier: the black-box natural proof barrier (Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022) `[black-box-natural-proof-barrier]` `[sharp-threshold-at-phi]`

The third-loop forward pointer hoped the Tell lever "offers a lever the S1.a face lacks." Cycle 11 confirms the lever is real — and discovers it is guarded by a barrier NOT present in the S1.a analysis:

- **Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022** ("The Exact Complexity of Pseudorandom Functions and the Black-Box Natural Proof Barrier for Bootstrapping Results") resolve the EXACT PRF complexity for TC⁰ (depth d): PRFs need n^{1+O(φ^{−d})} wires (upper, φ ≈ 1.618 the golden ratio) and n^{1+Ω(c^{−d})} wires (lower, c > φ, unconditional). `[sharp-threshold-at-phi]`: the breakthrough threshold constant IS the golden ratio φ ≈ 1.618 — the Chen-Tell threshold n^{1+(1.61)^{−d}} matches the PRF complexity. (The search summary reports Chen-Tell's threshold constant as 1.61 vs. the known ~30; Fan-Li-Yang pin it to φ.)
- **The black-box natural proof barrier** `[black-box-natural-proof-barrier]` is a FINE-GRAINED variant of Razborov-Rudich, specific to BOOTSTRAPPING (hardness magnification + quantified derandomization). The conflict: (a) **tractable side** — LB techniques that yield black-box distinguishers (via oracle queries) for small circuits yield unconditional PRF lower bounds of the SAME complexity; (b) **bootstrapping side** — bootstrapping techniques (error-correcting codes / hash functions) construct PRFs at roughly the bootstrapping-threshold complexity (assuming PRFs exist); (c) **conflict** — combining both to close the gap (the LB reaching/exceeding the bootstrapping threshold) would DISPROVE the existence of PRFs, believed impossible. So the barrier says: **no BLACK-BOX (natural-proof-style, distinguisher-based) LB technique can cross the wire threshold** (from n^{1+exp(−d)} to n^{1+φ^{−d}}), because doing so with a black-box method would disprove PRFs.
- **This is the obstacle on exactly the Tell lever route.** The breakthrough (push the wire count to threshold) requires an LB at the threshold wire count; the black-box natural proof barrier says any black-box LB technique that reaches the threshold disproves PRFs. So crossing requires a NON-black-box LB method — a method whose distinguisher is NOT a black-box oracle query (i.e., not a natural-proof-style property), parallel to how the S1.a face requires a non-(P/poly)-recognizable method. `[honest-ceiling]`: Fan-Li-Yang / Chen-Tell / Chen-Jin-Williams are web-confirmed (search/arXiv-summary-level, not PDF-line-verified); the exact threshold constant (φ ≈ 1.618) and the "known ~30" base are from the search summary; the barrier's precise scope (which techniques count as "black-box") is summary-level.

## Finding 4 — the higher-level unification: both faces guarded by a natural-proofs-VARIANT, but different variants `[two-faces-two-np-variants]`

The genuinely novel structural insight of Cycle 11: BOTH faces of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` are guarded by a **natural-proofs-variant barrier**, but the variants target DIFFERENT axes:

- **S1.a face:** Ilango's (P/poly)-recognizability barrier (Diagnosis 13) — strictly stronger than RR (no largeness condition); the gate is the **constructivity / recognizability of the LB COLLECTION** (the witness object); the escape requires a non-recognizable method.
- **Mining face:** Fan-Li-Yang's black-box natural proof barrier — a fine-grained RR variant on bootstrapping; the gate is the **black-box-ness of the LB TECHNIQUE** (the proof method); the escape requires a non-black-box method.

These are the two RR axes — **constructivity/recognizability** (the S1.a face, the witness object) vs **the black-box/natural distinction** (the mining face, the proof method) — showing up as the guarding barriers on the two faces. `[two-faces-two-np-variants]`: the unification is HIGHER than "explicit witness construction"; it is that the natural-proofs barrier, in two different fine-grained forms, guards BOTH live routes, on two different axes. A single idea that defeats BOTH variants (a non-recognizable AND non-black-box LB method) would advance both faces; but the two variants are distinct, so defeating one does not defeat the other (mirroring the second-loop's `[locality-vs-witness-orthogonal]` lesson: escaping one barrier ≠ escaping the other).

## What this changes in the map

1. **The third-loop forward pointer is resolved (positively on the lever, with a discovered barrier).** The mining face DOES have a near-term lever (Tell's quantified-derand wire frontier) the S1.a face lacks — a quantitative, incremental gap with a known algorithm below threshold and a precise target (n^{1+φ^{−d}}) above it. This is genuinely more optimistic than S1.a's qualitative (A).
2. **A NEW barrier enters the map** `[black-box-natural-proof-barrier]` — not present in the S1.a (third-loop) analysis. The crossing route is guarded by a fine-grained RR variant (Fan-Li-Yang 2022) that blocks black-box LB techniques from reaching the threshold. So the mining face is MORE optimistic (quantitative lever) AND guarded by a NEW barrier — the honest net is "different geometry, different barrier, no free lunch."
3. **The two faces are structurally different, not mirror images.** `[mining-face-structurally-different]` S1.a = balanced-point/tension/non-compositional; mining = sharp-threshold/black-box-NP-barrier. The `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` unification holds at the "explicit witness" level and now also at the higher "both guarded by a natural-proofs variant" level, but the internal geometry and the specific variant differ.
4. **No bypass, no breakthrough.** The Tell lever is real but its crossing is barrier-guarded; no derandomization built, no LB proved, no PRF disproved. The mining payoff (NEXP ⊄ TC⁰ / THR∘THR) remains open, now precisely framed as "cross the wire threshold (n^{1+exp(−d)} → n^{1+φ^{−d}}) with a NON-black-box LB method."

## Net

No breakthrough. The third-loop forward pointer is resolved: the mining (MAJ∘MAJ) face DOES have a real near-term lever the S1.a face lacks — Tell's quantified derandomization, a QUANTITATIVE wire frontier (push n^{1+exp(−d)} → n^{1+φ^{−d}}) with a known algorithm below threshold and the lower bound (NEXP ⊄ TC⁰ / THR∘THR) above it, because TC⁰ is on the strong side of the Goldreich-Wigderson dichotomy (Tell's TC⁰ extractor enables the quantified→standard passage). But the crossing route is guarded by a NEW barrier, the black-box natural proof barrier (Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022), a fine-grained Razborov-Rudich variant specific to bootstrapping, which blocks black-box LB techniques from reaching the threshold (they would disprove PRFs). The two faces of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` are structurally DIFFERENT (S1.a = balanced-point/tension; mining = sharp-threshold/black-box-NP-barrier) and guarded by DIFFERENT natural-proofs variants (S1.a = recognizability of the witness object; mining = black-box-ness of the proof method) — the unification is higher (both guarded by an RR variant, on two axes), not a mirroring. More optimistic than S1.a (a quantitative lever with an algorithm in hand), with a new barrier (black-box natural proofs) the S1.a analysis lacked. Equally honest in difficulty (the crossing needs a non-black-box LB method, and the barrier says black-box methods can't). `[honest-ceiling]` upheld — web-confirmed not PDF-line-verified; structural comparison is a reading; the load-bearing "real lever" fact is Tell's own stated implication; no breakthrough.

## What this means for the remaining cycles

The natural next attack (Cycle 12): the black-box natural proof barrier blocks BLACK-BOX methods from crossing the wire threshold. The question is whether a NON-black-box LB method exists (or is conceivable) for MAJ∘MAJ at the threshold wire count — the mining face's analogue of the S1.a face's "non-recognizable method" question. Candidate non-black-box methods: the Williams algorithmic method itself (NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰ is non-natural by dropping largeness, but is it black-box? — Diagnoses 13/14 analyzed its recognizability, not its black-box-ness); diagonalization; the Chen-Tal-Wang STOC 2025 worst-case THR∘THR LB (E^NP, via the Williams method in the CAPP→LB direction — is it black-box?). Whether ANY known non-black-box method can reach the threshold wire count, or whether the barrier is total (like a full natural-proofs barrier), is the open question for the next cycle.

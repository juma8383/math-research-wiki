## Findings

### F1. CORRECTION — GCT escapes all THREE barriers, not two `[gct-escapes-three-barriers]`
The wiki's `gct.md` / `barriers.md` recorded GCT as escaping natural-proofs and algebrization only. The search surfaces Mulmuley's explicit claim that GCT bypasses **relativization as well**, because it works on the **algebrized form** of computation (in the spirit of IP=PSPACE) — the relativization barrier does not apply. **Theorem 8.3 (Mulmuley, overview arXiv:0908.1932):** the #P≠NC result (no generalized permanent expressible as a trace-form polynomial in the relevant sense) bypasses natural proofs, relativization, and algebraic-degree barriers **simultaneously**, with **strongly explicit** obstructions of O(nk) bitlength constructible in O(nk) time. Mechanism for each barrier:
- **Relativization**: algebrized form (IP=PSPACE spirit) → the barrier does not apply.
- **Natural proofs**: the proof technique exploits the **characterization by symmetries** of det (Property D: unique up to det(A)det(B)=1 invariance) and perm (Property P: diagonal/permutation invariance). A technique that nontrivially uses these symmetries is **extremely rigid** — it applies to very few functions. Mulmuley's **Rigidity Hypothesis** (Hyp 2.4): |UP_n| ≤ 2^{poly(n)}, far stronger than RR's mild largeness requirement; this **automatically bypasses the RR largeness criterion** (Prop 7.3). This is the *legitimate* non-large/rigid escape from natural-proofs — the obstructions are NOT constructible as a truth-table predicate in the RR sense, which is the *same* property that makes them hard to find.
- **Algebrization/degree**: perm and det have the **same degree**, so degree-based reasoning cannot distinguish them; GCT reasons on the deeper **representation-theoretic** structure of the coordinate rings (which Weyl modules occur, with what multiplicity).

**This is the FIRST surface in 25 cycles to (claim to) escape all three local barriers** — every prior surface was blocked by at least one (S1.a/mining/descriptive needed a non-relativizing proof; measure/disjoint-pairs relativization-blocked; descriptive escapes only relativization, not natural-proofs/algebrization on its proof). GCT is the sole candidate with a clean barrier profile. **Caveat (honestly flagged):** this is Mulmuley's *claim* at search/arXiv-summary level, NOT PDF-line-verified; the natural-proofs escape in particular rests on the rigidity argument, which is accepted in the GCT literature but not a theorem in the RR framework. The criterion-B evidence (Thm 8.3 bypassing all three with explicit obstructions) is the strongest available.

### F2. The flip IS the construction lock `[gct-flip-is-construction-lock]`
The **flip** is GCT's defining abstract strategy, and it is — almost verbatim — the wiki's `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` relocated to algebraic geometry. The lower-bound problem (perm ⊄ det, i.e. nonexistence of a small representation) is **converted into an existence/construction problem**: explicitly exhibit **geometric obstructions** (Weyl modules V_λ living on the padded-permanent class variety but not the determinant class variety) that are:
1. **Short** — poly(n) bitlength,
2. **Easy to verify** — recognizable in poly(n) time,
3. **Easy to discover** — constructible in poly(n) time.

This mirrors NP's own definition: NP problems have short efficiently-verifiable witnesses for *membership*; the flip seeks short efficiently-verifiable witnesses for **nonmembership in P** (hardness). The **trivial obstruction** — an exponential-size table listing, for every small circuit C, a counterexample X with f(X)≠f_C(X) — has **"no short witnesses"** (Mulmuley's phrase). GCT proposes geometric obstructions as a *new notion* of short witness. **This is precisely `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`:** the route to a lower bound runs through an explicitly-constructed, short, verifiable witness whose explicit construction IS the lower-bound problem. The construction lock is not avoided by GCT; it is the **entire point** of the flip.

### F3. Mulmuley's "universal complexity barrier" = the wiki's witness barrier `[mulmuley-complexity-barrier-equals-witness-barrier]`
Mulmuley distinguishes the three (now four) barriers as **"local and mathematical"** — they only rule out restricted subareas of mathematics (diagonalization, arithmetization, combinatorial bounds, degree reasoning). He posits a deeper **universal, complexity-theoretic "complexity barrier"** that applies regardless of which area of mathematics an approach goes through: the self-referential fact that P≠NP is a universal statement that "discovery is hard," which seems to imply that "the discovery of its own proof is hard." Formalizing this barrier (GCT6) is, per Mulmuley, itself part of the challenge — analogous to the computability barrier being formalized by Church-Turing before Gödel's incompleteness theorem. **The formalization IS the short-witness / explicit-construction problem:** Step I formulates an Obstruction Hypothesis (OH) implying the trivial infeasible one; Step II trades "hard complexity theory (IOH)" for "hard mathematics (positivity hypotheses MH1..MHr + OH)" with the gain that P≠NP is not expected to obstruct the *mathematics*; Step III justifies that P≠NP shouldn't stand in the way of proving MH1..MHr. **The wiki's independent meta-finding — reached across six loops from four sub-surfaces (structural→measure→disjoint-pairs/proof-complexity→descriptive) that "the wall's lock is a CONSTRUCTION not a CONNECTION/BARRIER" — converges with Mulmuley's formalization.** This is not a missed connection (Mulmuley knew it); it is an **independent convergence** of two separate lines of reasoning on the same obstruction, which strengthens the diagnosis: the construction lock is not a wiki artifact but the object Mulmuley names the universal complexity barrier.

### F4. THE SHARPENING — barriers and the construction lock are COMPLEMENTARY, not alternatives `[barriers-and-construction-are-complementary]`
This is Cycle 26's load-bearing structural finding. GCT is the **existence proof** that escaping the three (local) barriers does **not** remove the construction lock — it **isolates** it. GCT pays the price to escape all three barriers (rigidity, algebrized form, representation-theoretic structure) and is left *exactly* with the flip = the full explicit short-witness construction. Triangulated with Cycle 25's descriptive surface (which escaped *one* barrier — relativization — and dropped the construction lock on the *instance* side because CFI/multipedes are explicit and in P, but retained the weak-model-LB construction), the two surfaces together pin down the wall:
- The **three barriers** are **local** — they block specific technique families.
- The **construction lock** is **universal** — it remains after every local barrier is escaped.
- Escaping barriers and constructing the witness are **orthogonal axes**: a surface can be clean on barriers (GCT: 3/3 escaped) yet fully locked on construction (the flip); or clean on the instance-construction half (descriptive: CFI free) yet locked on the LB half. No surface is clean on both. **The real obstruction is the construction lock, and no barrier-escape removes it.** This is the strongest form of `[no-missed-connection-moved-wall]` and the sharpest expression of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`: the wall is *not* the three barriers at all (those are local symptoms); the wall *is* the universal short-witness construction problem that Mulmuley names the complexity barrier.

### F5. The construction lock bites TWICE — the flip's own objects are NP-hard `[flip-construction-objects-are-nphard]`
The flip requires obstructions that are *poly-time constructible* (criterion 3 above). But the objects it must construct — Kronecker and plethysm coefficients / their positivity — are themselves **NP-hard / #P-hard** to decide/compute:
- **Ikenmeyer-Mulmuley-Walter 2017:** deciding **Kronecker positivity** is **NP-hard** (Theorem 4.1 in the survey) — a setback, since Mulmuley had conjectured poly-time decidability.
- **Fischer-Ikenmeyer 2020:** deciding **plethysm positivity** is **NP-hard** (even for fixed inner parameter m≥3); computing plethysm coefficients is **#P-hard** and **GapP-complete** (Thms 3.5/3.6/3.8). (Only fixing the *outer* parameter keeps it in P — the wiki's existing `[fi-2020-asymmetry]`.)
So the "construct/discover in poly time" half of the flip collides with the **very P≠NP-style hardness it is trying to prove**: the obstruction labels live in #P-hard territory. This is the **self-referential core** Mulmuley flags as the complexity barrier, now concrete: the construction lock bites once (you must construct the witness) and again (the witness's components are themselves hard to construct). The wiki's existing `gct.md` frames `[fi-2020-asymmetry]` as an *asymmetry lever* (permanent-side plethysm tractable for fixed outer d); the **new** framing is that this same hardness is a **collision with the flip's poly-time constructibility requirement** — a second face of the construction lock, not just a lever.

### F6. The flip's positivity is deep OPEN mathematics `[gct-flip-needs-deep-open-math]`
Even setting aside the complexity-theoretic hardness (F5), the flip's mathematical core is open. Theorem 10.2 (Mulmuley): an explicit obstruction family exists (for m=2^{log^a n}, a>1) assuming **PH1** (the multiplicity functions have #P-formulae with *positive* form — no alternating signs — characterized by explicit parametrized polytopes) and **OH** (a complexity-theoretic obstruction hypothesis for small m). Proving PH1 requires extending **LR PH0** (positivity of canonical-basis structure coefficients for standard quantum groups, proved via the **Riemann Hypothesis over finite fields** by Deligne/Lusztig) to **Kronecker PH0** (analogous positivity for the **nonstandard quantum groups** constructed in GCT4/7/8). The top-right corner of Mulmuley's commutative diagram — a nonstandard extension of the RH over finite fields — is **unknown**. This is the "law of conservation of difficulty": the approach trades the complexity-theoretic wall for a representation-theoretic wall of comparable depth. The construction lock, in GCT, is not merely open — it is open at the depth of extending Deligne's Weil conjectures.

### F7. CORRECTION — VP≠VNP does not imply P≠NP `[vnp-separation-not-pneqnp]`
The wiki's `gct.md` states "Transfers to P≠NP via Valiant's #P-completeness of the permanent." This is **overstated** and this cycle corrects it. Per Bürgisser 2000, Koiran-Perifel, and the Bürgisser 2024 survey:
- **VP≠VNP ⟹ P≠NP is NOT known.** There is no direct implication.
- **VP≠VNP ⟹ P/poly ≠ NP/poly** (over F₂, Valiant 1992, by direct Boolean simulation of polynomial computation; over general fields, Bürgisser 2000). This is a **nonuniform** separation.
- **VP=VNP ⟹ the polynomial hierarchy collapses** (Bürgisser 2000, **under GRH**); over characteristic 0, NC³/poly=P/poly=PH/poly; over finite characteristic p, NC²/poly=P/poly=PH/poly.
- Intuitively, VP (via depth reduction, Valiant-Skyum-Berkowitz-Rackoff 1983) is an algebraic analogue of **NC²**, so VP≠VNP ≈ algebraic nonuniform **NC²≠#P** — which does not directly rule out P=NP.

So GCT's target (VP≠VNP, equivalently perm≠det-as-p-projection) is a **necessary** step toward P≠NP (at least nonuniformly and under GRH) but **not sufficient**: even a complete GCT success would separate the *algebraic* classes, leaving a further **algebraic→Boolean, nonuniform→uniform** transfer that is itself open. This is the same "necessary-not-sufficient / transfer-gap" shape as the descriptive surface (CPT≠P doesn't reach P≠NP) and the Williams mining face (NEXP LB doesn't reach P≠NP) — a recurring pattern that the P≠NP-relevant lever is always *stronger/harder* than the active frontier's target.

# Cycle 12 — the mining face has TWO barriers on TWO axes; the known methods each escape one; the target quadrant is inhabited but not at the mining regime

## The question (Cycle 11's directive)

Cycle 11 (`sources/2026-08-23-mining-face-lever-and-bbnp-barrier.md`) identified the black-box natural proof barrier (`[black-box-natural-proof-barrier]`, Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022) guarding the mining face's Tell lever, and named the natural next attack (Cycle 12): **does a NON-black-box LB method exist (or is conceivable) for MAJ∘MAJ at the threshold wire count (n^{1+φ^{−d}}) — the mining analogue of the S1.a face's "non-recognizable method" question?** This cycle answers: the barrier's scope is precise (it blocks BLACK-BOX techniques only), the Williams method IS non-black-box (escapes it), BUT the mining face has a SECOND, stacked barrier (the n²-wall) that the Williams method does NOT escape — and the two barriers sit on two orthogonal axes, with the known methods each escaping one. The target quadrant (non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall) is NOT empty — Chen-Tal-Wang inhabit it — but NOT at the mining regime. `[honest-ceiling]`: all three load-bearing facts are web-confirmed (search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified); the two-axes/two-barriers synthesis, the SYM+ coupling reading, and the quadrant classification are STRUCTURAL READINGS, not theorems.

## Finding 1 — the Williams algorithmic method IS non-black-box (escapes Fan-Li-Yang) `[williams-method-is-non-black-box]`

Williams' NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰ method (STOC 2011 / J. ACM 2014) is, per the method's own description, **NON-BLACK-BOX**: "The method fundamentally exploits the internal structure of ACC circuits; black-box or oracle methods cannot see this structure." It circumvents natural proofs via **diagonalization** (completeness of SUCCINCT 3SAT; no efficient natural property is extracted) and is **non-relativizing / non-algebrizing** (oracles exist relative to which NEXP^A ⊆ ACC^A; the break uses the SYM+ representation's non-relativizing structure). The meta-theorem: a Circuit-SAT algorithm for class C running in 2ⁿ/n^{ω(1)} ⟹ NTIME[2ⁿ] ⊄ C; the ACC SAT algorithm comes from the Yao–Beigel–Tarui SYM+ depth reduction + an Evaluation Lemma (zeta transform / rectangular matrix multiplication). `[honest-ceiling]`: "non-black-box exploits internal structure / diagonalization circumvents natural proofs" is from the search summary of the method, search-level not PDF-line-verified; the classification of the method as non-black-box is standard and corroborated by the search, but the precise boundary of "black-box" in Fan-Li-Yang's sense (see Finding 2) is summary-level.

This is the mining-axis analogue of what the S1.a analysis (Diagnoses 13/14, Cycles 6-8) established on the RECOGNIZABILITY axis: there, Williams' (⇒) gives a recognizable property that is NON-TARGETABLE onto the specific witness g. Here, on the BLACK-BOX axis, the Williams method is NON-BLACK-BOX. **The two axes are distinct** (`[two-faces-two-np-variants]`, Cycle 11): recognizability (the witness OBJECT's constructivity) vs black-box-ness (the PROOF METHOD's structure-exploitation). The Williams method's status differs across the two axes — non-targetable on the recognizability axis (S1.a face), non-black-box on the black-box axis (mining face) — confirming the two axes are independent dimensions of "natural-ness," not redundant. `[honest-ceiling]`: the cross-axis reading is structural, not a theorem.

## Finding 2 — BUT the mining face has a SECOND barrier (the n²-wall) the Williams method does NOT escape; the two barriers sit on TWO AXES `[n2-wall-and-bbnp-are-two-axes]` `[two-barriers-two-axes-mining-face]`

The Williams method, though non-black-box (escapes Fan-Li-Yang), **cannot reach the mining target** because it needs a fast SAT algorithm for the target class, and for TC⁰/THR∘THR at the threshold wire count the **n²-wall** (`[n2-wall]`, established Cycles 1-5, `pages/williams-algorithmic.md`) kills the SYM+ route: threshold gates have F_p-approximate degree Θ√t (Alman-Williams 2015, tight), not polylog, so Beigel-Tarui modulus amplification is useless for real-valued thresholds; the composed degree Θ√(mt) forces ≥2ⁿ monomials at n² wires. **No fast SAT algorithm ⟹ the Williams meta-theorem cannot fire for TC⁰ at threshold wires.**

So the mining face has TWO STACKED barriers on TWO ORTHOGONAL axes:

| | escapes n²-wall (algorithmic axis) | blocked by n²-wall |
|---|---|---|
| **black-box (Fan-Li-Yang axis)** | CSS16/Tell CAPP route escapes the SAT requirement (SAT→CAPP escapes SETH) but is BLACK-BOX → blocked by Fan-Li-Yang | (empty / irrelevant) |
| **non-black-box** | **Chen-Tal-Wang** (Finding 4) — inhabited, but not at the mining regime | **Williams algorithmic** — non-black-box but needs the SYM+ SAT algorithm the n²-wall denies |

`[two-barriers-two-axes-mining-face]`: barrier #1 = the n²-wall (ALGORITHMIC axis — whether the class admits a fast SAT/CAPP algorithm via structure exploitation); barrier #2 = the black-box NP barrier (PROOF-THEORETIC axis — whether the LB technique is black-box/natural-proof-style). The known methods each escape ONE barrier and fall to the OTHER:
- **Williams algorithmic**: non-black-box (escapes #2) BUT n²-wall-blocked (falls to #1) — no fast SAT algorithm for TC⁰ at threshold wires.
- **CSS16/Tell CAPP route** (the mining program's known anchor, the Tell lever of Cycle 11): escapes #1 (the CAPP/derandomization route bypasses the fast-SAT-algorithm requirement — SAT→CAPP escapes SETH) BUT black-box (the CSS16 Chen-Santhanak-Srinivasan random-restriction LB is, per Fan-Li-Yang, "extracting a black-box natural property from a white-box restriction lemma") → blocked by #2. `[capp-route-is-black-box]`.

`[honest-ceiling]`: the 2×2 classification is a STRUCTURAL READING of the methods' known properties (web-confirmed, search-level); "the CSS16/CAPP route is black-box" is from the Fan-Li-Yang search summary (their TC⁰ LB "extracts a black-box natural property"); the orthogonality of the two axes is a reading (an LB technique's black-box-ness and its dependence on a fast SAT algorithm are logically independent properties — no theorem states they must align — but in the KNOWN methods they happen to anti-correlate).

## Finding 3 — the Williams method's non-black-box-ness and its n²-wall-blockage are the SAME COIN (SYM+ coupling) `[williams-sym-plus-coupling]`

A subtle but load-bearing reading: for the Williams method specifically, escaping Fan-Li-Yang (non-black-box-ness) and falling to the n²-wall are NOT independent — they couple through the **SYM+ representation**. The method's non-black-box-ness IS its exploitation of internal ACC structure (the Yao–Beigel–Tarui SYM+ depth reduction + Evaluation Lemma); that same SYM+ structure is exactly what the n²-wall denies for TC⁰ (threshold approximate degree Θ√t wrecks the SYM+ representation). So **the Williams method escapes Fan-Li-Yang PRECISELY in classes (ACC⁰) where the SYM+ structure is available, which is exactly where the n²-wall does NOT apply; for TC⁰, both barriers fire simultaneously through the same mechanism.** You cannot "take Williams, make it non-black-box AND escape the n²-wall" by a tweak — its non-black-box-ness is co-extensive with its SYM+ dependence. Escaping the n²-wall while staying non-black-box requires a DIFFERENT structure-exploitation than SYM+.

`[honest-ceiling]`: this SYM+ coupling is a STRUCTURAL READING (not a theorem — no paper states "Williams' non-black-box-ness = SYM+ = n²-wall-blocked" as a formal equivalence); it is sound as a mechanism description (the method's non-black-box content IS the SYM+ representation; the n²-wall IS the failure of SYM+ for thresholds) but the "same coin" framing is my synthesis, flagged. It is the mining-face analogue of how, on the S1.a face, the two gates (recognizability + tight-window) coupled through ONE linchpin (the tight-window upper bound, Cycle 8) — here the two barriers (black-box + n²-wall) couple through ONE mechanism (SYM+) for the Williams method specifically. The coupling is method-specific (the CAPP route is NOT coupled this way — it escapes the n²-wall via derandomization, a different mechanism, at the cost of being black-box).

## Finding 4 — the target quadrant (non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall) is INHABITED — by Chen-Tal-Wang — but NOT at the mining regime `[chen-tal-wang-inhabits-target-quadrant]` `[target-quadrant-not-at-mining-regime]`

**Chen-Tal-Wang** ("Superquadratic Lower Bounds for Depth-2 Linear Threshold Circuits," STOC 2026, ECCC TR26-039; `[chen-tal-wang-2025]` — note: previously recorded in the wiki as "STOC 2025"; the search confirms STOC 2026 / ECCC TR26-039, a minor dating correction recorded additively here) occupy the (non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall) quadrant:
- **Non-black-box**: they apply the Williams algorithmic method (the algorithm-to-LB framework) — diagonalization-based, structure-exploiting, not an oracle-query natural property.
- **Escapes the n²-wall**: their key technical contribution is a NEW 2^{n−n^{Ω(ε)}}-time CAPP algorithm for estimating the acceptance probability of an **XOR of two** n^{2.5−ε}-size THR∘THR circuits — a DIFFERENT algorithmic route than the single-polynomial SYM+ SAT algorithm the n²-wall kills. The XOR-of-two-specialized-estimator structure bypasses the n²-wall's specific mechanism (it does not require a low-degree polynomial representation of a single threshold circuit). Result: a function f ∈ E^NP requiring n^{2.5−ε}-size THR∘THR — the first **superquadratic** depth-2 threshold LB, breaking the decade-old sub-quadratic gate barrier (Tamaki 2016 / Alman-Chan-Williams 2016).

So the target quadrant is NOT empty: a non-black-box method that escapes the n²-wall and yields a real THR∘THR lower bound EXISTS. `[honest-ceiling]`: Chen-Tal-Wang details are from the search abstract (search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified); the classification as "non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall" is a structural reading of the method (Williams-method-based ⇒ non-black-box; XOR-of-two CAPP ≠ SYM+ SAT ⇒ escapes the n²-wall's specific mechanism) — sound as mechanism description, not a theorem.

**BUT it does not reach the mining payoff** `[target-quadrant-not-at-mining-regime]`:
1. **Worst-case**, not average-case/correlation — the mining program (`[mining-program]`) needs an average-case (correlation) LB to feed the NW reconstruction; Chen-Tal-Wang is a worst-case size LB.
2. **n^{2.5−ε} SIZE** regime, not the **threshold wire count n^{1+φ^{−d}}** — the mining payoff (via Tell's quantified-derand lever, Cycle 11) is a wire-count LB at n^{1+φ^{−d}} ⟹ NEXP ⊄ TC⁰; Chen-Tal-Wang's n^{2.5−ε} is a depth-2 SIZE (gate) bound in a different (worse) regime.
3. f ∈ **E^NP**, not NW-usable — the mining reconstruction needs a well-structured average-case-hard function for a restricted class; E^NP witnesses from the Williams method are not downward-self-reducible / sample-aided-reducible in the sense CRTY (JACM 2023) requires (`[crty-2023]`).
4. **CAPP for XOR-of-two** specialized THR∘THR circuits, not a general CAPP for MAJ∘MAJ — the mining program needs the latter (`[majmaj-capp-route]`).

So Chen-Tal-Wang prove the target quadrant is REACHABLE (a non-black-box, n²-wall-escaping LB for THR∘THR exists and is non-trivial — superquadratic), but the GAP between their n^{2.5−ε} worst-case E^NP LB and the n^{1+φ^{−d}} average-case threshold LB is itself the open frontier. The honest net: the quadrant is inhabited at a weaker regime; pushing Chen-Tal-Wang's XOR-of-two-CAPP technique (or a descendant) from (worst-case, n^{2.5−ε}, E^NP, XOR-of-two) to (average-case, n^{1+φ^{−d}}, NW-usable, general-MAJ∘MAJ) is a concrete — if very hard — research program, not a proven impossibility. `[honest-ceiling]`: the "gap = open frontier" framing is a reading; whether the XOR-of-two technique can be pushed to the mining regime is an OPEN question, not resolved here.

## What changes vs Cycle 11

Cycle 11 framed the mining face as "cross the wire threshold with a non-black-box LB method" — ONE barrier (Fan-Li-Yang black-box NP). Cycle 12 ADDS the second, stacked barrier (the n²-wall) and shows:
- The two barriers are on TWO ORTHOGONAL axes (algorithmic n²-wall vs proof-theoretic black-box NP).
- The known methods each escape ONE barrier and fall to the OTHER (Williams: non-black-box but n²-wall-blocked; CSS16/Tell: escapes n²-wall but black-box).
- For the Williams method, the two barriers COUPLE through SYM+ (the escape and the blockage are the same coin).
- The target quadrant (non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall) is INHABITED (Chen-Tal-Wang) but NOT at the mining regime (worst-case / n^{2.5−ε} / E^NP / XOR-of-two, vs the needed average-case / n^{1+φ^{−d}} / NW-usable / general-MAJ∘MAJ).
- The genuine-open shape is now PRECISELY: a non-black-box LB technique that escapes the n²-wall AND reaches the mining regime (average-case, threshold wire count, NW-usable) — Chen-Tal-Wang show the first two conditions are jointly satisfiable (at a weaker regime), so the question reduces to whether their (or a descendant) technique can be pushed to the regime. This is a STRICT refinement of Cycle 11's single-barrier framing.

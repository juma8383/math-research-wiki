# Cycle 7 (3rd loop, Cycle 2) — The two-gate wall is overcounted

This is Cycle 7 (the second cycle of the user's THIRD 5-cycle mandate: "continue the next best options on loop for 5 cycles to make a true breakthrough or solve the problem"). Cycle 6 resolved the second loop's named next verification ((Q-targetability)) and resolved **Gate 1 (recognizability) as soft** — the breakthrough shape (a non-recognizable explicit-f tight-window AC⁰ LB) is UN-conditioned, blocked only by **Gate 2 (tight-window)** + the empirical non-recognizable-method gap. The natural next lever (the cycle's mandate) is therefore Gate 2 directly: *is the tight-window itself theorem-blocked, or merely empirically unmet — and is it an independent gate from Gate 1 at all?* This cycle attacks that and finds that **the Cycle-1 "two independent gates" framing overcounts the binding constraints**: Gate 1 is not a separate gate to cross, it is an *automatic structural consequence* of any successful deterministic many-one reduction (Ilango's own theorem), and the *same method* that achieves the tight window (Gate 2) is the one Ilango himself flags as plausibly non-natural (Gate 1). `[honest-ceiling]` upheld: no breakthrough; the product is a primary-source-grounded structural sharpening that collapses the two-gate wall into a single derandomization barrier plus one naturalization question.

## The question Cycle 7 set out to answer

Gate 2 (the tight-window / two-sided-precision gate `[ac0-tight-window]`): the S1.a witness g must have its AC⁰_{d−1} (DNF) complexity **pinned** in [(1−4δ)T,(1+4δ)T] for each T=t·n², t∈[n^{8/δ},2^n] — a multiplicative (1±4δ) cliff at a tunable size: g **easy** at size (1+4δ)T and **hard** below (1−4δ)T. Sipser/RST (the existing explicit exponential AC⁰ LBs) are "all-hard" and fail the upper-bound side. So Gate 2 asks: (i) is an explicit tight-window AC⁰ LB theorem-blocked, or just unmet? and (ii) is Gate 2 genuinely *independent* of Gate 1, or do they interact — e.g., does achieving the tight window force recognizability (merging the two gates into one)?

The cycle's load-bearing observation came from re-reading the **primary source** (Ilango, ECCC TR20-183, §1.4, "When can lower bounds be used to prove hardness?") with this question in mind — and finding that Ilango's *own* discussion already answers (ii).

## Finding 1 — Gate 1 is automatic for any successful deterministic many-one reduction `[gate-1-automatic]`

**Verified verbatim** (`sources/_tr20-183.txt`, lines 559-564):

> "Hence, we can conclude that the reduction R actually also implies that **recognizing elements of S is coNP-hard**! In fact, it shows that even the promise problem of distinguishing the lower bounds contained in S from strings in the set of YES instances of (C)-MCSP … is coNP-hard. Thus, if one believes that, say, coNP ⊄ P/poly, it better not be the case that the language S can be computed in P/poly."

and the definition (lines 565-572):

> "a collection of lower bound statements S against a circuit class C is **(P/poly)-recognizable** if there exists a family of polynomial-sized circuits that accepts all elements of S and rejects all the YES instances of (C)-MCSP. … one should not be able to prove hardness for (C)-MCSP using (P/poly)-recognizable collections of lower bound statements (at least under the usual type of reductions: many-one, deterministic, polynomial-time). This is interesting because many lower bound methods we know, like **Håstad's switching lemma, yield collections of lower bound statements that are (P/poly)-recognizable**."

`[gate-1-automatic]`: **Gate 1 (non-recognizability) is not a constraint separately imposed on the witness g; it is an automatic structural consequence of any successful deterministic many-one (C)-MCSP reduction.** Ilango's theorem says: if R is a deterministic many-one reduction using LB collection S, then recognizing S is coNP-hard ⇒ (under coNP⊄P/poly) S is **not** (P/poly)-recognizable. So a reduction that *works* *automatically* uses a non-recognizable LB collection; there is no separate "and also make it non-recognizable" hurdle. The real content of Gate 1 is a constraint on the **proof method** — *don't use recognizable LB methods (switching lemma), use non-recognizable ones* — not a third property the witness must independently satisfy on top of {explicit, tight-window}. This is the precise sense in which the Cycle-1 framing of Gate 1 as a peer of Gate 2 overcounts: Gate 1 is a *method-selection filter* (rule out switching-lemma-style recognizable LBs), automatically discharged once a non-recognizable method is used.

**Honest scope on the automaticity.** Ilango notes (lines 630-631) "our logic above assumes that the reduction from SAT to (C)-MCSP is a **deterministic many-one** reduction." The *existing* `(AC⁰_d)`-MCSP reduction is randomized + quasipoly + Turing (`[route-a-variant-gap]`), so the automaticity applies to the *target* deterministic many-one reduction (Route A's goal), not to the reduction that currently exists. Gate 1 is therefore a binding constraint on *which method may be used in the eventual deterministic many-one reduction*, not a property to verify of an already-constructed witness. Conditional on coNP⊄P/poly (widely believed) and on the target reduction existing.

## Finding 2 — Ilango's own Lupanov/direct-sum method is the candidate non-recognizable method, AND it achieves the tight window `[lupanov-plausibly-nonnatural]` `[lupanov-largeness-violated]`

The decisive passage — Ilango discussing **his own** `(AC⁰_d)`-MCSP reduction's lower bound (Theorem 2, the "large gaps in formula complexity between depths" result, proved via the direct-sum/lifting Theorem 4/5 with Lupanov-sampled g — i.e., the S1.a witness method). Lines 601-619:

> "if we view Theorem 2 as separating the class of size-s depth-(d+1) formulas from size-(s+2^{O_d(n)}) depth-d formulas … it is not clear to what extent this circuit class separation naturalizes in the sense of Razborov and Rudich's Natural Proofs Barrier. For one, **our method only proves a lower bound on a specific class of functions obtained via a direct sum. This seems to violate the largeness condition** of a natural proof … it is open whether uniformly random functions f have a gap as large as L_d(f) − L_{d+1}(f) = Ω(2^n) with high probability. **Lupanov showed that L_d(f) = (1+o(1))L_{d+1}(f) when d ≥ 3 with high probability.** Second, **it is not clear how to recognize the functions witnessing this lower bound in polynomial time given a truth table. This seems to violate the constructivity condition** of a Natural Proof. Of course, this does not mean that this separation does not naturalize, just that it does not obviously naturalize. … it would be interesting to explore whether one can put this result in the framework of Natural Proofs."

`[lupanov-plausibly-nonnatural]` `[lupanov-largeness-violated]`: **the Lupanov/direct-sum method — the very method that achieves the tight window (Gate 2, Lemma 26's g with DNF complexity in [(1−4δ)t·n²,(1+4δ)t·n²]) — is the method Ilango himself flags as plausibly non-natural**, on *both* natural-proofs axes:
- **Largeness violated.** Lupanov's theorem (cited by Ilango): for d ≥ 3, a uniformly random function has L_d(f) = (1+o(1))L_{d+1}(f) — **no** depth-gap w.h.p. So the large-gap/tight-window property holds for a *negligible* fraction of uniform functions (a random function sits at near-maximal complexity with no cliff). Equivalently for the S1.a window: a uniform function has DNF complexity near-max (~2^N/N), not the low ~t·n² the window requires — so "DNF complexity ∈ [(1−4δ)t·n²,(1+4δ)t·n²]" is a *small-fraction* property over uniform functions. Largeness fails.
- **Constructivity (recognizability) plausibly violated.** "It is not clear how to recognize the functions witnessing this lower bound in polynomial time given a truth table." The lower-bound half (Claim 32: no small DNF ε-approximates g) is a **union bound over an exponentially-large formula family** — its certificate is the enumeration itself (size 2^{O(t·n²·log n)}), not a poly-size object. So the LB collection is not *obviously* (P/poly)-recognizable. (The upper-bound half, Claims 30-31, *is* constructive — an explicit DNF of size (1+4δ)t·n² — but recognizability of the LB collection is about the *hardness* certificate, not the upper bound.)

So a **single method** (Lupanov/direct-sum) **plausibly crosses both gates simultaneously** — it achieves the tight window (Gate 2, existentially, w.h.p. over the Lupanov seed) *and* is plausibly non-recognizable (Gate 1, per Ilango's own constructivity flag + the Finding-1 automaticity for a deterministic many-one reduction).

## Finding 3 — the two-gate wall collapses to derandomization + one naturalization question `[two-gates-overcounted]` `[wall-collapses-to-derandomization]`

Combining Findings 1–2 with the first-loop antiprox analysis `[s1a-antiprox-obstruction]`:

`[two-gates-overcounted]`: **the Cycle-1 framing of the AC⁰ face as two *independent* gates (Gate 1 recognizability ⊥ Gate 2 tight-window, "both must be crossed") overcounts the binding constraints.** The two gates are not independently binding because they are discharged by *the same method*:
- Gate 2 (tight-window) is **achieved existentially** by the Lupanov method (Lemma 26: g in the window w.h.p. over the seed).
- Gate 1 (recognizability) is **automatic** for any successful deterministic many-one reduction using that method (Finding 1), and the method is **plausibly non-recognizable** in its own right (Finding 2).

So the AC⁰ wall is **not** "find a function that independently satisfies {non-recognizable AND tight-window}" (a conjunction of two gates to cross from scratch). It is a **single barrier**:

> **(A) Derandomize the Lupanov sampler** — find an explicit seed (a deterministic choice of the Z_y's) such that g is good. This is exactly the antiprox obstruction `[s1a-antiprox-obstruction]`: the lower-bound half of goodness is a derandomized union bound over a 2^{O(t·n²·log n)}-sized formula family, infeasible to enumerate; equivalently, a **fine-grained DNF lower bound for an explicit function in the tight window** [(1−4δ)t·n²,(1+4δ)t·n²] — the circuit-LB frontier (S1.b′, the genuine open core of S1).

with Gate 1 auto-satisfied by the Lupanov method's non-recognizability (Finding 1 + 2). `[wall-collapses-to-derandomization]`: **the AC⁰ face's binding barrier is the derandomization (A) — the antiprox obstruction — not a separate recognizability gate on top of it.**

## The ONE residual Gate-1 concern — the naturalization question (B) `[gate-1-residual-is-naturalization]` `[naturalization-open-question]`

Gate 1 is *plausibly* (not *provably*) discharged. Ilango's own caveat (lines 615, 620-625):

> "this does not mean that this separation does not naturalize, just that **it does not obviously naturalize**. Since results can naturalize in highly non-trivial ways (we mention an example in the next paragraph), it would be interesting to explore whether one can put this result in the framework of Natural Proofs." … "Even though a collection of lower bound statements S might not be (P/poly)-recognizable, it is possible that there is a **variation S' of S that is (P/poly)-recognizable** and still captures all the 'interesting' lower bounds given by S. A situation like this occurs in Razborov and Rudich's paper where they show how to modify **Smolensky's** lower bound against AC⁰[p] circuits to fit into the natural proofs framework, even though it is unclear whether Smolensky's original method is constructive."

`[gate-1-residual-is-naturalization]` `[naturalization-open-question]`: **the residual Gate-1 risk is that the Lupanov/direct-sum LB *secretly naturalizes*** — i.e., although S (the Lupanov LB collection) is not *obviously* recognizable, there may exist a (P/poly)-recognizable *variation* S' that still certifies the tight-window hardness (Smolensky-style: the original method looks non-constructive, but a recognizable reformulation exists). If such an S' exists, then the tight-window LB *is* effectively recognizable ⇒ using it in a deterministic many-one reduction would (by Finding 1's contrapositive, i.e., Ilango's recognizable⇒SAT∈subexp) **collapse SAT**, re-tightening Gate 1. So the substantive open question is **(B): is the Lupanov/direct-sum tight-window LB *genuinely* non-natural, or does it secretly admit a recognizable certificate?** This is **Ilango's own stated open question** (line 615: "it would be interesting to explore whether one can put this result in the framework of Natural Proofs"). Resolving (B):
- **positively** (prove the Lupanov/direct-sum LB is genuinely non-naturalizing / has no recognizable variation) ⟹ confirms Gate 1 discharged for the Lupanov route; the wall is then purely (A).
- **negatively** (exhibit a recognizable variation S') ⟹ Gate 1 re-tightens; the Lupanov LB cannot be the reduction's witness without collapsing SAT; the AC⁰ face re-blocks at Gate 1.

(B) is a *single, named, primary-source-anchored* open question — sharper than the vague "empirical non-recognizable-method gap" of Cycle 6, because it is now pinned to *one specific method* (Ilango's own Lupanov/direct-sum) and *one specific test* (does it naturalize?).

2. **The two-gate wall collapses to (A) + (B).** The AC⁰ face's binding barrier is the **derandomization (A)** — the antiprox obstruction / fine-grained DNF LB for an explicit function in the tight window — plus the **naturalization confirmation (B)**. Together: Gate 1 is soft from *two independent directions* — (i) Williams' (⇒) cannot re-tighten (Cycle 6), and (ii) the Lupanov method itself is plausibly non-recognizable, with automaticity for a deterministic many-one reduction (Cycle 7). The residual in direction (ii) is the naturalization question (B).

## Net

The AC⁰ face of the `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` wall is **overcounted as two independent gates** (Cycle 1). Gate 1 (recognizability) is **automatic** for any successful deterministic many-one reduction (Ilango's theorem, lines 559-564) and **plausibly discharged** by the Lupanov/direct-sum method itself — the *same* method that achieves the tight window (Gate 2) — which Ilango himself flags as non-large (Lupanov: random functions have no depth-gap) and not-obviously-recognizable (lines 601-619). The wall therefore **collapses** to:
- **(A)** derandomize the Lupanov sampler = the antiprox obstruction = a fine-grained DNF LB for an explicit function in the tight window (the circuit-LB frontier), and
- **(B)** prove the Lupanov/direct-sum LB is *genuinely* non-naturalizing (Ilango's own open question, line 615; the residual Gate-1 risk is a Smolensky-style recognizable variation re-tightening).

The breakthrough shape is no longer "two independent gates crossed simultaneously"; it is "one known existential method (Lupanov) made explicit (A) and confirmed non-natural (B)." Gate 1 is downgraded from independent binding gate to automatic-plus-residual-naturalization-question. The wall stands; the map is sharper and one structural overcount is removed; the binding barrier is precisely (A) + (B). No breakthrough; `[honest-ceiling]` upheld — primary-source-grounded (Ilango §1.4 verbatim), the collapse is a structural interpretation (not a new theorem), non-recognizability is plausible-not-proven, (A) is the circuit-LB frontier, (B) is genuinely open.

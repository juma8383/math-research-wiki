# Cycle 15 — SYNTHESIS of the fourth 5-cycle loop: the two routes CONVERGE; the mining face's escape from natural proofs IS the S1.a route; the S1.a (A) is the single live thread

## What the fourth loop set out to do, and did

The third loop (`sources/2026-08-23-third-loop-synthesis.md`) compressed the S1.a (AC⁰/DNF) face's wall from a 2-D "cross two independent gates" barrier to a 1-D "hit one balanced point" barrier — non-compositional, Gate 1 soft from three directions, the wall an OPEN construction (A = an explicit function at the balanced point of {expensive ∧ small-gap} = the circuit-LB frontier). It ended with a forward pointer `[mining-face-forward-pointer]`: is the symmetric Williams/mining (MAJ∘MAJ) face's wall compressible to a one-linchpin structure? does its near-term relaxation seam (Tell's quantified derandomization = the CSS16 wire frontier) offer a lever the S1.a face lacks? The fourth loop did not re-survey; it **attacked the mining face directly** across four cycles (C11–C14), testing exactly that forward pointer.

The fourth loop's answer, synthesized here: **the mining face does NOT compress to an open construction the way the S1.a face did. It CLOSES.** The NEXP-LB route is natural-proofs-blocked for ALL NEXP-LB / algorithmic-method techniques (Cycle 14's CWY bridge + RR/FLY partition), and the mining face's ONLY escape from that natural-proofs cover IS the S1.a explicit-function route. So the fourth loop's net product is a **convergence**: both routes point to the SAME open construction (A), and the natural-proofs skin of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` is now fully mapped — two variants on two axes, routed by CWY's NEXP-specificity. The mining face, despite its Cycle-11 quantitative-lever optimism, ends FURTHER from a breakthrough than the S1.a face: its escape routes are natural-proofs-blocked, whereas the S1.a barrier is an open construction (not a proven impossibility).

## The four-cycle trajectory of the mining face

The mining face's recognizability/black-box gate (the mining analogue of the S1.a recognizability gate) traced a self-correcting arc across four cycles — **not monotone, ending MORE blocked than it began**:

BUT guarded by a NEW barrier — Fan-Li-Yang's black-box natural proof barrier (threshold at φ≈1.618). Both faces guarded by RR variants on DIFFERENT axes (S1.a = recognizability/constructivity of the witness object; mining = black-box-ness of the proof method). Optimistic opening.

- **Cycle 12** (`[two-barriers-two-axes-mining-face]`): the mining face has TWO stacked barriers on TWO axes — the n²-wall (algorithmic: does the class admit a fast SAT/CAPP algorithm via structure exploitation) + the black-box NP barrier (proof-theoretic: is the LB technique black-box). **The arc: optimistic opening (Cycle 11, a real quantitative lever) → two stacked barriers (Cycle 12) → partial walk-back + residual (Cycle 13) → residual resolved AGAINST optimism + the CWY-bridge two-face unification (Cycle 14).** The mining face ends the loop MORE blocked than any prior cycle suggested — the non-largeness "escape" was illusory, and the NEXP-LB route is natural-proofs-blocked for the entire known technique class.

## The synthesis — two routes CONVERGE `[two-routes-converge]` `[nexp-lb-route-naturalproofs-closed]` `[mining-redirects-to-s1a]`

## The natural-proofs skin fully mapped `[np-skin-two-variants-two-axes]`

The fourth loop completes the mapping of the natural-proofs skin of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`. The wall's natural-proofs barrier has **two variants on two axes**, and which variant bites is determined by the NEXP-vs-explicit-function distinction (CWY's NEXP-specificity, the routing mechanism):

- **Constructivity/recognizability axis (explicit-function LBs):** Ilango's (P/poly)-recognizability barrier (`[recognizable-stronger-than-natural]`, strictly stronger than RR — needs constructivity ∧ usefulness, NO largeness). Explicit-function LBs escape CWY's NEXP-specific black-box forcing, so they are NOT forced to be black-box-constructive — but Ilango's recognizability barrier bites instead (the witness object's constructivity). This is the axis the S1.a (explicit-function) face lives on, and the third loop already compressed it to the open construction (A) with Gate 1 soft.

So the wall's natural-proofs skin is now fully mapped: a NEXP LB is routed to the black-box/largeness axis (RR/FLY, blocked for all known NEXP-LB techniques); an explicit-function LB is routed to the constructivity/recognizability axis (Ilango, an open construction). CWY's NEXP-specificity is the routing mechanism — it is what gives `[two-faces-two-np-variants]` (Cycle 11) a mechanism rather than a co-occurrence, and what makes the two routes converge on (A).

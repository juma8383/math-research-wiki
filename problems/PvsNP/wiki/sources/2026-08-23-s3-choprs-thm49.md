## The verified negative result

### What Thm 49 actually is (formula world, not AC⁰)

> **Theorem 49 (CHOPRS).** There is a language L ∈ E such that:
> (i) L ∉ GapAND_O(N)-Formula[N^{3−ε}] for all constants ε > 0, but
> (ii) L ∈ O_{N^{o(1)}}-Formula[N²].

This is an **unconditional, uniform** result: L ∈ E (deterministic 2^{O(N)} time). The model is **formulas with oracle gates** (GapAND top gate of fan-in O(N); O_{N^{o(1)}} = small-fan-in oracle gates), i.e. **HM Frontier D (the formula world)** — *not* the AC⁰ circuit world Route A lives in. Its significance is that it is a **non-localizable** lower bound: the N^{3−ε} formula LB for L does *not* extend to formulas with small-fan-in oracle gates (it survives exactly where the localizable LBs of Frontiers A–E collapse). It is the proof-of-concept that the locality barrier [locality-barrier] [choprs-2020] is not absolute.

### How the non-localizability is purchased: an existential hard function

The construction of L is Andreev-like. On an input x of length N, it partitions x into m = log N blocks, takes the parity of each block to form an index i ∈ {0,1}^m, and outputs **f_hard(i)**, where f_hard is a function on m bits found by **exhaustive search in 2^{O(N)} time**: enumerate all functions on m bits and keep one that **cannot be 0.8-approximated by formulas of size N^{1−ε/2}** (an existence/counting argument guarantees one exists; the search finds it within the E time budget of L).

**This is the same non-uniformity/existential-witness pattern as S2's irritable T and S1.a's Lupanov g.** The "uniformity" of L ∈ E comes from allowing 2^{O(N)} time *to build the circuit for L itself* — but that is the time budget of the *language*, not of a *reduction*. For a **logtime-uniform AC⁰ many-one reduction** (Route A's requirement), the reduction circuit family must be constructible in O(log n) time per input length; a 2^{O(N)} exhaustive search cannot be embedded in a logtime-uniform reduction. To port Thm 49's escape into a *uniform-AC⁰ MCSP reduction*, one would need **f_hard given explicitly** (poly-time computable, not searched) — i.e., an **explicit function on m = O(log n) bits with a proven formula lower bound of N^{1−ε/2} = 2^{(1−ε/2)·m} = 2^{Θ(m)}** (an **exponential-in-its-input-length formula lower bound**, average-case/0.8-approx).

### The formula-LB frontier (verified) — same exponential gap as S2, formula model

The best **explicit** formula lower bound is **Ω̃(n³)** (polynomial, cubic): Håstad 1998 (Andreev function, shrinkage exponent O(p²)); held/improved by Dinur-Meir CCC 2016 (KW proof, n^{3−o(1)}), Gál-Tal-Trejo-Nuñez ITCS 2019 (n³/polylog via majority composition), Filmus-Meir-Tal ToC 2023 (Ω̃(n³) for an explicit AC⁰ function via random projections). The shrinkage exponent is known **tight**, so improving beyond cubic needs a fundamentally new technique (the "n³ barrier"). Thm 49 needs an explicit f_hard on m bits not approximable by formulas of size **2^{Θ(m)}** — i.e. a 2^{Θ(m)} LB where best explicit is **m³** (polynomial, with m the input length of f_hard). **Exponential gap — exactly the S2 structure, in the formula model instead of the general-circuit model.**

## The Cycle-1 finding (unifying diagnosis extended to a THIRD instance + a barrier-orthogonality clarification)

**[s3-locality-escapes-witness-barrier-does-not] — porting CHOPRS Thm 49 does NOT escape the [witness-needs-explicit-lb] obstruction; it *relocates* it.** Thm 49 escapes the **locality** barrier [locality-barrier] precisely by leaning on an **existential** hard function (the exhaustively-searched f_hard). The non-localizability is bought with non-uniformity. Uniformizing the escape — making f_hard explicit so it can live in a logtime-uniform AC⁰ reduction — requires an **explicit exponential (in m) formula lower bound**, which is open (best explicit m³, needed 2^{Θ(m)}). So:

- **Three instances of [witness-needs-explicit-lb], one per circuit model:** S1.a = **DNF** witness (explicit exponential DNF LBs *exist*, so the crux is the tight window + derandomization-of-the-union [s1a-antiprox-obstruction]); S2 = **general-circuit** witness (explicit LBs stuck at Θ(L) linear, needed 2^{Θ(L)} exponential [s2-irritable-witness-obstruction]); **S3 = formula witness** (explicit LBs stuck at m³ cubic, needed 2^{Θ(m)} exponential). All three require, at the core, an **explicit exponential (in L=O(log n)) lower bound for a function on O(log n) bits**, in their respective circuit models — and in all three the best explicit LB is *polynomial* (DNF: exponential exist; general circuits: linear; formulas: cubic), exponentially short of the exponential target. The three are the same natural-proofs-frontier obstruction in three circuit models.
- **Barrier orthogonality [locality-vs-witness-orthogonal]:** the **locality** barrier [locality-barrier] [choprs-2020] and the **witness-needs-explicit-LB** barrier [witness-needs-explicit-lb] are **distinct and orthogonal**. Thm 49 demonstrates that the locality barrier is *not absolute* (a non-localizable LB exists) — a genuine conceptual advance by CHOPRS — but its escape mechanism is the existential hard function, which *is* the witness barrier. **Escaping locality ≠ escaping the witness barrier.** This refines the wiki's prior framing of [route-a-dual-locality] (which lumped "the locality barrier blocks route B AND touches Route A's gap construction"): the locality barrier is one wall, the witness barrier is another, and Route A is pinned by the *witness* barrier (the explicit-exponential-LB need) even where a locality escape (Thm 49) is in principle available. The "one crack, CHOPRS Thm 49, proves such LBs can exist" note in mcsp-meta-complexity is now sharpened: it proves *non-localizable* LBs exist, but for an Andreev-like function via an existential witness — not for MCSP, not uniformly, not escaping the witness barrier.

### Honest scope

- **Rules out:** porting *this* Thm 49 escape into a *uniform* logtime-AC⁰ MCSP reduction via an explicit f_hard — the uniformization is exactly the explicit-exponential-formula-LB problem (open).
- **Does NOT rule out:** (a) a structurally different non-localizable LB whose hard function *is* explicit (no such technique is known; this would be the breakthrough); (b) a uniform-AC⁰ reduction escaping locality by a mechanism that does not lean on an existential witness (none known); (c) P ≠ NP regardless (S3 is a sub-target of Route A; even a full S3 would be one of the restricted→original / Turing→many-one / quasipoly→AC⁰ gaps still remaining).

### Status of the locality barrier after Thm 49

Thm 49 is a real, important CHOPRS result: it shows the locality barrier is not a logical impossibility — non-localizable LBs *can* be proved. But the only known way to make an LB non-localizable is to hardwire an existential hard function whose *explicit* construction is itself the open circuit-LB problem. So the locality barrier is, in practice, escaped only by paying the witness barrier. This is the same conclusion [witness-needs-explicit-lb] reaches on the Williams side and the S1.a/S2 side: the cheap probabilistic/existential input supplies the witness only insufficiently for a *uniform* reduction.

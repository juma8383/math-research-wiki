## Four web searches (all search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified)

1. **Karchmer-Wigderson games, depth lower bounds, current state & barriers.** Confirmed: the strongest formula depth LB is (3−o(1)) log n (Håstad, ~25 years, Tal 2014 lower-order terms). The KRW strategy: take a random function f on log n bits (depth ≈ log n − o(log n), explicit via its n-bit description), compose with itself O(log n) times ⟹ a P-computable function on n bits requiring depth ≈ log² n ⟹ P ⊄ NC¹. Gavinsky-Meir-Weinstein-Wigderson (SICOMP 2017) proved KRW for f ⋄ universal relation (information complexity); de Rezende-Meir-Nordström-Pitassi-Robere (FOCS 2020) extended via lifting to monotone/semi-monotone settings; Meir (FOCS 2023) proved a KRW-like theorem for STRONG composition, isolating the first obstacle (that g∘f must behave like a direct sum) as the crucial barrier; Chukhin-Kulikov-Mihajlin (2024) achieved (3−o(1)) log n for strong composition with XOR-inner via formal complexity measures.

2. **Communication-complexity lower bounds vs the three barriers.** Two genuinely new facts. (a) The KRW approach MAY CIRCUMVENT natural proofs — it targets a SPECIFIC artificially-constructed function rather than a random one, potentially violating the RR "largeness" property (search-summary reading, not an RR-framework theorem). (b) **Chen-Hu-Ren, ITCS 2026** ("New Algebrization Barriers to Circuit Lower Bounds via Communication Complexity of Missing-String", LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.37): NEW algebrization barriers via the communication complexity of XOR-Missing-String — proving PostBPE ⊄ i.o.-SIZE[O(n)] requires non-algebrizing techniques; BPE^{Ã₂} has linear-size A₂-oracle circuits (improving Aaronson-Wigderson's multiquadratic to MULTILINEAR extensions); and HALF-EXPONENTIAL might be the correct barrier for algebrizing techniques; communication complexity is "more useful than previously thought" for algebrization barriers.

3. **Transfer: does KRW separate P from NP or only NC¹ from P?** Confirmed: the KRW framework targets **P ⊄ NC¹** specifically (the composed function is in P, the goal is super-log depth ⟹ outside NC¹). This is a WEAKER separation than NC¹ vs NP — a "first milestone" / stepping stone toward super-poly SIZE LBs. The techniques are designed for the P-vs-NC¹ milestone, not P-vs-NP directly.

4. **Matrix rigidity for depth/linear circuits — why stuck (2025).** Alman-Liang (arXiv:2502.19580, Feb 2025, "Low Rank Matrix Rigidity: Tight Lower Bounds and Hardness Amplification"): a new spectral method over finite fields giving a near-TIGHT low-rank rigidity LB R_{H_n}(Θ(n)) ≥ 4^n·(1/2 − 2^{−O(n)}) (overcoming the old spectral caveats — ℂ-only, bounded-rigidity-only); and a HARDNESS AMPLIFICATION showing a slight improvement to the low-rank bound ⟹ Razborov rigidity ⟹ a PH^cc communication lower bound — explaining why rigidity is stuck at Ω(N²/r·log(N/r)) (the untouched-submatrix ceiling, Lokam 2000) with a "barrier just beyond reach." Alman-Williams (STOC 2017) showed Walsh-Hadamard is NOT Valiant-rigid (ruling out the rigidity route for O(n)-size O(log n)-depth arithmetic LBs); Alman (SICOMP 2025) depth-d linear circuits of size O(d·N^{1+0.96/d}). Chukhin-Kulikov-Mihajlin-Smirnova (STACS 2026, arXiv:2411.02936): a uniform-nondeterministic-LB assumption ⟹ small explicit rigid-matrix families, conditional depth-3 LBs.

## The six facet reports

### Facet 1 — Definitional foundations & the NP-completeness mystery (in-loop, from completed facets + verified attributions)
The definitional zoo and why NP-completeness is open:
- **MCSP[N,s]**: given the 2ⁿ-bit truth table T of a Boolean function and a size bound s, decide whether some circuit of size ≤ s computes T. In NP (witness = circuit). Exact complexity open.
- **MKTP / MKtP** (time-bounded Kolmogorov complexity, Levin): the Kolmogorov analogue. Often technically more tractable than MCSP (e.g., the AC⁰[p] lower bound and the Allender-Hirahara equivalence were first proved for MKTP, then lifted to MCSP in part).
- **Gap-MCSP / promise-MCSP**: a promise version with a gap between the YES threshold (small circuit) and NO threshold (large). Hirahara's worst-case/average-case machinery operates on a gap/MINKT variant.
- **Parameterized / threshold variants MCSP[2^{αn}]** and **partial-function / oracle / multi-output (MOCSP)** variants — relevant to hardness magnification and to the proliferating "variant" landscape (Huang-Ilango-Ren STOC 2023; Hirahara-Moshkovitz ISAAC 2023).
- **Why NP-completeness is open — the Goldilocks structure** `[murray-williams-2017]` (Murray & Williams, ToC 2017, "On the (Non) NP-Hardness of Computing Circuit Complexity"): the *consequence* of MCSP NP-hardness depends on the **reduction class**, and MCSP is **provably not NP-hard** under the weakest (local `TIME(n^{1/2−ε})`) reductions while the class that would yield P≠NP (uniform-AC⁰ reductions) sits right next to it. The reduction-class → consequence map: uniform-AC⁰-hard ⟹ NP ⊄ P/poly ⟹ P≠NP; polytime-hard ⟹ EXP≠ZPP (not P≠NP); local ⟹ provably impossible. This is the decisive structure for the honest verdict: **near-term de-conditioning (quasi-poly/polytime reductions) yields EXP≠ZPP-class separations — major progress, but NOT P≠NP.**
- **Two formal obstructions to the strong (P≠NP-yielding) milestone**, stronger/more numerous than the primer recorded: (i) the **OWF obstruction** `[mcsp-nphard-owf]` — MCSP NP-hard under natural/parsimonious reductions ⟹ no one-way functions (Liu-Pass 2020; Ren-Santhanam 2021; Allender-Das line), believed false; (ii) **Mazor-Pass CCC 2024** `[mazor-pass-2024]` — Gap-MCSP is **not** Levin-NP-complete under iO + subexponential OWFs (the primer recorded only the exact-MCSP OWF obstruction, missing this Gap-MCSP obstruction).

### Facet 2 — Hirahara distinguisher / de-conditioning machinery (in-loop, from thesis facet + barrier facet)
- **Mechanism (distinguisher framework).** Hirahara's program converts a *distinguisher* for a pseudorandom/distributional problem — or a worst-case-to-average-case reduction — into a circuit lower bound. The hallmark is **non-black-box**: the reduction exploits the *inner structure* of the problem (e.g., the Kolmogorov-information content in MINKT), not just an oracle's input-output behaviour. Non-black-box reasoning is structurally non-relativizing (the precedent is arithmetization in IP=PSPACE), giving the structural optimism on the relativization barrier — though, per Facet 5, no rigorous oracle-pair theorem yet proves MCSP lower bounds *must* be non-relativizing.
- **Hirahara FOCS 2018** `[hirahara-2018]` — "Non-black-box worst-case to average-case reductions within NP": if **Gap-MCSP is NP-hard**, then **Heuristica does not exist** (NP easy-on-average but hard-in-worst-case is impossible) — collapsing worst-case and average-case NP. *What is proven vs assumed:* the implication is proven; the premise (Gap-MCSP NP-hardness) is the open condition. The result is a **structural collapse**, not a circuit lower bound against P/poly.
- **Hirahara-Ilango FOCS 2025** `[hirahara-ilango-2025]` — "NP-hardness of the Minimum Circuit Size Problem from Well-Studied Assumptions" (doi 10.1109/focs63196.2025.00087): the **first conditional NP-hardness of MCSP**, conditional on (coNP has subexponential nondeterministic circuits) + (P^NP/poly circuits of size Ω(2ⁿ/n)) + (subexponential witness-indistinguishable proofs for SAT), under quasi-polynomial-time nonadaptive reductions. *Conditional vs proven:* the NP-hardness is conditional on those assumptions; the reductions are quasi-poly nonadaptive. De-conditioning = removing/weakening these assumptions toward unconditional hardness.
- **The de-conditioning trajectory as a concrete program.** Over the decade the community has moved from fully-conditional toward conditional-on-weaker assumptions; HI2025 is the current frontier. **The honest caveat (from Facet 6):** the active de-conditioning trajectory targets quasi-poly/polytime reductions, which under Murray-Williams yield **EXP≠ZPP-class** separations, **not P≠NP**. The reduction class that yields P≠NP (uniform AC⁰) is a different, harder target the trajectory is not currently aimed at.

### Facet 3 — The CIKK triangle (subagent-authored, attribution-verified)
- **CIKK 2016** `[cikk-2016]` (Carmosino-Impagliazzo-Kabanets-Kolokolova, CCC 2016, Art. 10; ECCC TR16-008; doi 10.4230/LIPIcs.CCC.2016.10). Theorem 5.1 (informally Thm 1.1): a **P-natural** (constructive) property with **largeness ≥ 1/5** that is **useful** against a circuit class Λ containing AC⁰[p] for some prime p yields a randomized **PAC-learning** algorithm for Λ. The run-time degrades with the strength of the lower bound (poly / quasipoly / subexponential depending on the size function u). The mechanism is a "play-to-lose" NW-generator reconstruction + hardness amplification; the AC⁰[p] containment is a genuine (load-bearing) hypothesis.
- **The double-edged reading the primer underplayed.** By contrapositive: if Λ is cryptographically hard to PAC-learn (the standard RR assumption: PRFs in P/poly), then **no** constructive+large+useful property against Λ can exist — because such a property would, by CIKK, yield a PAC learner. So CIKK is **both** a bridge (lower-bound ⟹ learner) **and** the **trap** that re-expresses the Razborov-Rudich natural-proofs barrier in learning-theoretic language. The primer (barriers.md) states both directions but softens the trap as "exposing the dual face"; the honest reading is that CIKK is as much an *explanation of why natural proofs cannot prove P/poly lower bounds* as a tool for proving them.
- **MCSP is adjacent, not identical, to the natural-proof object.** A polytime **MCSP decider** (complemented, for threshold s) *is itself* a large+constructive property useful against SIZE(s) — so an efficient MCSP algorithm would constitute a natural property (see the direct corollary in Facet 5). `[attribution-unverified for the explicit "complemented-MCSP-decider = natural property" phrasing — folklore, discussed in Allender's MCSP survey writings; not pinned to a single paper.]`
- **Routing a lower-bound program THROUGH MCSP inherits the natural-proofs barrier** to the *exact extent* the MCSP argument factors through a constructive+large+useful property. The meta-complexity escape attempts are precisely attempts to *drop one hypothesis*: drop **largeness** (hardness magnification, which deliberately destroys density), drop **constructivity-as-a-truth-table-predicate** (average-case/gap/relativized versions). `[verification_status: plausible-but-unverified at the theorem-detail level for the OS18 "magnification destroys largeness" density argument.]`
- **Follow-ups:** Oliveira-Santhanam CCC 2017 ("Conspiries…", LIPIcs.CCC.2018.7) — learning speedup lemma + PRF/learnability dichotomy + NC¹ reduces to MCSP via TC⁰ truth-table reductions (MCSP ∈ TC⁰ ⟹ NC¹ = TC⁰). Oliveira-Santhanam FOCS 2018 + Oliveira-Pich-Santhanam CCC 2019/ToC 2021 — **hardness magnification** for MCSP[2^{√n}], gap-MCSP, MKtP magnifying to strong separations (NP ⊄ Formula[poly], EXP ⊄ SIZE[poly], NP ⊄ NC¹, NEXP ≠ BPP). Pich (Comput. Complexity 2025, doi 10.1007/s00037-024-00261-4) — interactive-witnessing characterization of PAC learning, alternative NW-reconstruction proof of the OS speedup, locality-barrier discussion. `[theorem-level details of OS17/OS18/OPS19/Pich25 marked plausible-but-unverified; author/venue attributions correct.]`
- **Honest verdict:** CIKK + follow-ups **do not solve P vs NP**, yield **no unconditional P/poly lower bound**, and must not be overclaimed as such. Value: a three-way *equivalence* (natural-proof ⟺ PAC learner ⟺ meta-complexity distinguisher), a *no-go*, and a *roadmap* identifying where the barrier's hypotheses can be selectively dropped.

### Facet 4 — Does MCSP actually evade the three barriers? (subagent-authored; the honest-ceiling linchpin)
Adversarial test of the primer's "orthogonal to the three-barrier framework" claim. **Verdict: PARTIALLY TRUE / OVERSTATED.**
- **Natural proofs (the EASY-MCSP direction) — MCSP is directly CAUGHT.** `[mcsp-easy-naturalproof]` If MCSP ∈ P (decidable in poly(2ⁿ) time), then for any ε>0 the property "circuit-complexity(T) ≥ 2^{εn}" is (i) constructive [via MCSP], (ii) large [a random function has complexity ~2ⁿ/n ≫ 2^{εn} w.h.p.], (iii) useful against P/poly [every poly-size function has complexity poly(n) < 2^{εn}]. This is a **direct corollary of Razborov-Rudich 1997** that the primer **under-weights** by routing the natural-proofs link only through CIKK's learning restatement. MCSP ∈ P would contradict the existence of strong OWFs/PRGs.
- **Natural proofs (the HARD-MCSP / lower-bound direction) — evasive in principle, relocated in practice.** A lower bound for the *named* function MCSP need not satisfy largeness (RR 1997: largeness is what fails for named-function lower bounds — same kernel as Williams/GCT). So in principle an MCSP lower bound can evade natural proofs. **But** the live MCSP-to-LB mechanism is **hardness magnification**, which hits a *new, MCSP-specific* barrier: the **locality barrier** `[locality-barrier]` (Chen-Lyu-Potyagailo-Santhanam, `[attribution-unverified at exact-paper level]`), showing the weak-MCSP-lower-bound techniques "localize" and provably cannot cross the magnification threshold. So the natural-proofs difficulty is **relocated, not evaded** — and the wiki's own "barrier treadmill" (`[barrier-treadmill]`) says every breakthrough technique spawns a new barrier; the locality barrier IS that spawn for the MCSP route.
- **Relativization — structurally non-relativizing, but unproven rigorously.** Hirahara's non-black-box reductions are structurally non-relativizing (precedent: arithmetization), and oracle-sensitivity is *expected* (an oracle can encode circuit-complexity info, making MCSP^A easy; a random oracle plausibly leaves it hard), which would force any resolution to be non-relativizing. **But** no rigorous BGS-style oracle pair currently proves MCSP lower bounds *must* be non-relativizing. `[verification_status: plausible-but-unverified.]`
- **Algebrization — entirely unexamined.** No published analysis of MCSP-based lower bounds against the Aaronson-Wigderson 2008 algebraic-oracle barrier, in either direction. Only a weak structural hint: Hirahara's MINKT machinery is Kolmogorov/information-theoretic, plausibly beyond the low-degree-polynomial encodings algebrization was designed to defeat. `[speculative.]`
- **The core logical flaw.** The three barriers classify **proof techniques**, not **problem statements**. "MCSP is a reframing of the question" (true) does **not** entail "an MCSP-based *proof* evades the barriers" (unestablished). Williams' method and GCT are *specific techniques* each verified non-relativizing and non-natural; **MCSP has produced no concrete lower-bound *technique* that has been verified against any of the three barriers.** "Not caught by the barriers-union because it is a reframing" conflates *unclassified because no technique exists yet* with *barrier-immune*. Once a concrete MCSP-based proof exists it will have an access model and will be classifiable.
- **Accurate restatement:** MCSP is a **CANDIDATE route that may thread the barriers**, with structural reasons for optimism on relativization and the named-function natural-proofs escape, but **no rigorous evasion on any of the three**, and a new barrier already spawned for its magnification sub-route. The barrier treadmill (which the wiki itself documents) continues; MCSP is not exempt.

### Facet 5 — MCSP hardness ↔ OWF ↔ Impagliazzo worlds + recent developments (in-loop, from thesis facet)
- **The OWF obstruction (verified, with nuance).** `[mcsp-nphard-owf]` "MCSP NP-hard under natural (parsimonious / polytime many-one) reductions ⟹ one-way functions do not exist" — this is the **Liu-Pass 2020 / Ren-Santhanam 2021 / Allender-Das** line, more a **family of equivalence/obstruction results than one clean theorem**; the wiki's single-attribution framing is slightly imprecise. Crucially, the obstruction applies to a *specific reduction class* (natural/parsimonious/polytime many-one) and **does not rule out** NP-hardness under more general reductions — consistent with the Murray-Williams reduction-class dependence.
- **Impagliazzo worlds mapping.** MCSP NP-completeness (under the strong reduction class) would force us out of the worlds that need average-case hardness / OWFs and toward **Algorithmica**-adjacent collapse of worst/average case (Hirahara 2018's Heuristica-killing is the structural landmark); MCSP being genuinely NP-intermediate leaves Minicrypt/Cryptomania open. `[precise world-forcing is conditional and the Heuristica-killing result is itself conditional on Gap-MCSP NP-hardness.]`
- **Recent developments 2023–2026.** Beyond HI2025: **Huang-Ilango-Ren STOC 2023** (MOCSP / approx-Kolmogorov NP-hard, oracle/approx variants); **Hirahara-Moshkovitz ISAAC 2023** (variant NP-hardness); **Mazor-Pass CCC 2024** `[mazor-pass-2024]` (Gap-MCSP not Levin-NP-complete in obfustopia — a *negative* result tightening the obstruction landscape); **Pich 2025** (witnessing-based re-derivation of the OS speedup, locality-barrier discussion). **No unconditional NP-hardness of MCSP (or a Gap/promise variant) exists as of mid-2026.** `[recent-year attributions verified at author/venue level via the thesis facet's web search; theorem-level details marked plausible-but-unverified where not opened line-by-line.]`

### Facet 6 — Is the meta-complexity thesis real, and what is the sharpest milestone? (subagent-authored)
- **The thesis is neither pure slogan nor fully formal.** The closest formal core is the **Allender-Hirahara 2019** equivalence `[allender-hirahara-2019]` (ToCT, doi 10.1145/3349616): circuit-size lower bounds ⟺ hardness of **relativized** MKTP^A under uniform AC⁰ reductions — a genuine lower-bound↔meta-complexity-hardness equivalence, but **for the relativized problem**; **de-relativizing it is open** (and may be a relativization barrier in disguise). The synthesis "understanding meta-complexity is the key to P vs NP" is a **programmatic slogan** (Allender surveys; Hirahara talks), not a single formal conjecture, and is **not cleanly falsifiable in its strong form** because new MCSP variants can always be proposed.
- **The sharpest falsifiable milestone.** De-condition `[hirahara-ilango-2025]` toward **unconditional NP-hardness of MCSP (or a Gap/promise/Kolmogorov variant) under a consequential reduction class.** HI2025 is the actual frontier; the field organizes around the de-conditioning trajectory. **Decisive honest caveat:** near-term de-conditioning targets quasi-poly/polytime reductions, yielding **EXP≠ZPP-class separations (major progress), NOT P≠NP**. P≠NP requires the strictly harder **uniform-AC⁰-hardness** (route A) or crossing the hardness-magnification **locality barrier** (route B).
- **Exactly two formal MCSP→P≠NP bridges, both currently out of reach** `[route-a-route-b]`: **(A)** NP-hardness of MCSP under **uniform-AC⁰ reductions** ⟹ NP ⊄ P/poly ⟹ P≠NP (sits in the Murray-Williams Goldilocks zone next to the provably-impossible local reductions); **(B)** **hardness magnification**: MCSP[2^{δn}] requiring time > N^{1.01} on one-tape deterministic TMs ⟹ P≠NP — blocked by the **CLPS 2022 locality barrier** `[locality-barrier]`.
- **The honest gap (most prone to hype).** Most actual MCSP landmark results give circuit lower bounds against **restricted** classes or **structural collapses** (Heuristica non-existence), **not P≠NP**. The bridge from "MCSP is hard" to "P≠NP" is **formal but is itself the unproven, hardest part**, and the active de-conditioning trajectory is **not currently aimed at the reduction class that would yield P≠NP**. Hirahara 2018 yields an average-case collapse; CIKK 2016 rules out a barrier; only the Murray-Williams AC⁰-hardness chain reaches P≠NP, and that is the hardest variant.
- **A genuine falsifier of the strong thesis.** An (unconditional or widely-believed-conditional) result that **MCSP is NOT NP-hard under uniform-AC⁰ reductions** — extending the Murray-Williams local lower bound *upward* to AC⁰ — **combined** with the locality barrier closing route B, would close **both** formal P≠NP bridges and make MCSP a dead end for P vs NP via the known formal bridges. The *slogan* form remains un-falsifiable by variant proliferation, which is itself the sharpest methodological finding.

## Synthesis (researcher-applied, over the six facet reports)

- **Deepened vs the primer:** (a) the verified AC⁰[p]/SZK attributions and the Goldilocks reduction-class structure (Facets 1–2); (b) the *direct* "MCSP ∈ P ⟹ natural property" corollary of RR 1997 the primer under-weighted (Facet 4); (c) the **relocated-not-evaded** locality barrier for the magnification sub-route (Facets 4–6); (d) the **route A / route B** structure of the only two formal P≠NP bridges and the honest gap between "circuit lower bound / structural collapse" and "P≠NP" (Facets 2, 6); (e) the slogan-vs-formal asymmetry of the thesis (Facet 6).
- **Barrier assessment (the headline):** **OVERSTATED → corrected to "candidate route, partially evasive, not barrier-immune, with a descendant barrier already spawned."** Natural proofs: CAUGHT in the easy direction (direct RR corollary), evasive-in-principle but relocated-via-locality-barrier in the hard direction. Relativization: structurally optimistic but no rigorous oracle-pair theorem. Algebrization: entirely unexamined. The "orthogonal/reframing ⇒ barrier-immune" inference is a category error (barriers classify techniques, not problem statements).
- **Breakthrough requirement:** A genuine P≠NP-adjacent breakthrough via MCSP requires **route A** (unconditional or de-conditioned NP-hardness of MCSP under **uniform-AC⁰** reductions) **or route B** (crossing the locality barrier for hardness magnification). Both are currently out of reach and partially obstructed (Murray-Williams local lower bound on one side of route A; CLPS locality barrier on route B; OWF + Mazor-Pass obstructions on strong-reduction variants). Near-term de-conditioning of HI2025 under quasi-poly/polytime reductions would be major progress but yields **EXP≠ZPP-class, not P≠NP**.
- **Falsifiable milestones (sharpest first):**
  1. **De-condition HI2025** — remove/weaken its assumptions toward unconditional NP-hardness of MCSP (or a Gap/promise/Kolmogorov variant) under a consequential reduction class. *(Highest-leverage live target.)*
  2. **Construct the MCSP oracle pair** — an oracle A with MCSP^A ∈ P^A and an oracle B with MCSP^B hard/NP^B-complete — to rigorously settle whether resolving MCSP *requires* non-relativizing reasoning. *(Cleanest single test of the relativization facet; currently open.)*
  3. **Test Hirahara-style non-black-box MCSP/MINKT reductions against Aaronson-Wigderson algebraic-oracle extensions** — does an algebraic extension defeat the worst-case-to-average-case reduction? *(Zero published analysis; settles the algebrization facet.)*
  4. **Resolve the locality-barrier question** — is the CLPS locality barrier technique-level (escapable by a genuinely new technique, as arithmetization escaped relativization) or inherent to all MCSP-to-LB magnification routes? *(Determines whether route B is a temporary block or a genuine descendant barrier.)*
  5. **Extend Murray-Williams upward** — prove (or rule out) MCSP NP-hardness under uniform-AC⁰ reductions (route A). *(A negative result here, plus route B closed, falsifies the strong thesis.)*
- **Verdict (faithful):** MCSP/meta-complexity is the **most promising current reframing** — the one program where near/medium/long-term targets are all *circuit lower bounds or structural collapses* and where conditional results have been progressively de-conditioned — but it is **not a barrier-immune shortcut through to P≠NP**. The honest position, corrected from the primer: MCSP is a **candidate route that may thread the barriers**, with no rigorous evasion proven on any of the three, a descendant locality barrier already spawned, and a formal-but-unproven bridge to P≠NP that the active trajectory is not currently aimed at. The primer's "orthogonal to the three-barrier framework" is the overclaim this deep-dive corrects.

- `[allender-hirahara-2019]` — Allender-Hirahara, ToCT 2019 (doi 10.1145/3349616): circuit-size lower bounds ⟺ hardness of *relativized* MKTP^A under uniform-AC⁰ reductions; the closest formal core of the meta-complexity thesis; de-relativizing it is open.

- `[murray-williams-2017]` — Murray & Williams, ToC 2017 ("On the (Non) NP-Hardness of Computing Circuit Complexity"): the Goldilocks reduction-class structure — MCSP NP-hard under uniform-AC⁰ ⟹ NP ⊄ P/poly ⟹ P≠NP; polytime ⟹ EXP≠ZPP; local TIME(n^{1/2−ε}) ⟹ provably not NP-hard.
- `[mazor-pass-2024]` — Mazor-Pass, CCC 2024 ("Gap MCSP Is Not (Levin) NP-Complete in Obfustopia"): Gap-MCSP not Levin-NP-complete under iO + subexponential OWFs; a *negative* obstruction the primer missed.
- `[os-2017]` — Oliveira-Santhanam, CCC 2017: learning-speedup lemma + PRF/learnability dichotomy + NC¹ reduces to MCSP via TC⁰ truth-table reductions.
- `[os-2018]` — Oliveira-Santhanam, FOCS 2018: hardness magnification for MCSP[2^{√n}], gap-MCSP, MKtP to strong separations; argued magnification sidesteps natural proofs by destroying largeness.
- `[pich-2025]` — Pich, Comput. Complexity 2025: interactive-witnessing characterization of PAC learning; alternative NW-reconstruction proof of the OS speedup; locality-barrier discussion.
- `[locality-barrier]` — the locality barrier for hardness magnification (Chen-Lyu-Potyagailo-Santhanam, `[attribution-unverified at exact-paper level]`): known weak-MCSP-lower-bound techniques "localize" and provably cannot cross the magnification threshold — a *descendant* natural-proofs-style barrier specific to the MCSP route. Symbol of the barrier treadmill continuing under MCSP.
- `[goldilocks-structure]` — the reduction-class-dependent consequence map for MCSP NP-hardness (uniform-AC⁰ ⟹ P≠NP; polytime ⟹ EXP≠ZPP; local ⟹ impossible); the decisive structure for the honest verdict.
- `[mcsp-easy-naturalproof]` — MCSP ∈ P ⟹ a constructive+large+useful natural property against P/poly (property "circuit-complexity ≥ 2^{εn}"), a *direct corollary* of Razborov-Rudich 1997 the primer under-weighted by routing the link only through CIKK.
- `[route-a-route-b]` — the only two formal MCSP→P≠NP bridges: (A) NP-hardness under uniform-AC⁰ reductions; (B) hardness magnification MCSP[2^{δn}] > N^{1.01} one-tape. Both currently out of reach and partially obstructed.
- `[barrier-evasion-overstated]` — the corrected verdict: MCSP is a *candidate* route that may thread the barriers, not barrier-immune; "orthogonal/reframing ⇒ immune" is a category error (barriers classify techniques, not problem statements); a descendant locality barrier is already spawned.
- `[thesis-formal-vs-slogan]` — the meta-complexity thesis has a formal core (Allender-Hirahara 2019, relativized) but its strong form ("key to P vs NP") is an unfalsifiable-by-variant-proliferation slogan; the genuine falsifier closes both route A and route B.

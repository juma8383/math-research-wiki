# Descriptive Complexity

## The reformulation
Fagin: NP = ESO. Immerman-Vardi: P = FO+LFP over **ordered** finite structures. Hence **P=NP ⇔ ESO = FO+LFP over ordered structures**.

## Dead — the 0-1-law route collapses
The 0-1 law is the only model-theoretic tool producing a genuine logical asymmetry: on **unordered** dense random graphs G(n,½), LFP obeys the 0-1 law (Blass-Gurevich-Kozen) but ESO does not (Kaufmann-Shelah). Superficially LFP ⊊ ESO ⟹ P ⊊ NP.

But P vs NP lives in the **ordered** regime (Immerman-Vardi), and the order destroys the 0-1 law for **both** logics `[shelah-1996]` — the order lets LFP express universe parity, which oscillates.

## The sharpest rescue fails too
Re-pose on unordered structures as ESO vs **order-invariant** LFP (sentences using an auxiliary <, truth independent of the order chosen — captures P, lives in the 0-1-law regime). **Fails**: order-invariant LFP can still express "universe has even cardinality" (iterate even/odd along the order via a least fixed point; invariance removes the order-dependence). Universe parity has no limiting probability (1 on even n, 0 on odd). So order-invariant LFP fails the 0-1 law by the **same mechanism** as ESO. The gap collapses.

## Why it dies
A 0-1 law distinguishes behavior on a *distribution*, not expressive power on all structures; the distribution that separates the logics is not the one in which the problem is stated, and the one in which it is stated erases the separation. Compactness inapplicable (finite + non-compact). EF/pebble games reduce to the CFI/circuit-lower-bound difficulty. All three barriers apply via faithful translation.

## Residual value
A clean **negative result** explaining why the ESO/0-1-law descriptive tools cannot reach P vs NP. The orthogonal **Gurevich logic-for-P program** was flagged here as open and relativization-independent (Dahan-Dawar 2020) — that residual is now investigated (Cycle 25, below), and the independence from relativization turns out to be the surface's genuinely new structural fact.

## The logic-for-P lever (Cycle 25)

`[logic-for-p-implies-pneqnp]`: **Gurevich (1988)** asked whether a **logic captures P** on the class of *all* finite structures (isomorphism-closed, including **unordered** — the hard case; on ordered structures FO+LFP captures P by Immerman-Vardi). He conjectured the **negative**. The implication: **no logic for P ⟹ P ≠ NP**. Mechanism (per **Dahan-Dawar 2020**, who stress it is not a trivial corollary of Fagin): if P = NP then PH collapses and a **poly-time canonical labelling** of graphs exists; poly-time canonization ⟹ a logic for P (Gurevich 1997: PTIME complete invariants ⟹ PTIME canonization ⟹ a logic effectively capturing P). Contrapositive gives the lever. The **converse is open** (P ≠ NP yet a logic for P may exist). Equivalent formulation (**Dawar 1995**): a logic for P ⟺ a complete problem for P under first-order reductions. The conclusion is a **meta non-existence statement** ("no recursive presentation of the P-computable isomorphism-invariant classes"), NOT "exhibit a hard function" — so, like the measure pivot, it **sidesteps the `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` explicit-construction lock in its conclusion**.

`[descriptive-escapes-relativization]` — **the genuinely new structural fact (first in 24 cycles):** **Dahan-Dawar 2020** construct an oracle A with a logic for P^A but P^A ≠ NP^A — the implication "logic-for-P ⟹ P=NP" is **false in relativized worlds**, i.e. the lever "no-logic-for-P ⟹ P≠NP" is a **non-relativizing** theorem (its relativized analog fails). So **any P≠NP proof going through "no logic for P" is automatically non-relativizing** — the relativization barrier `[three-barriers-required]` is circumvented *for free*. **This is the first surface across all 24 cycles to escape the relativization barrier** — every prior surface was explicitly relativization-blocked ((A)/S1.a needs a non-relativizing proof as an open requirement; the mining face needs one; the measure surface inherits relativization `[measure-relativization-barrier]`; the disjoint-pairs target is relativization-blocked `[disjoint-pairs-relativize]`). The descriptive/logic-for-P surface is the sole exception. Caveat: it escapes **one** of the three barriers; natural-proofs and algebrization still apply to the *proof* (below).

### The catch — the active frontier targets a weaker question `[cpt-separator-not-pneqnp]` `[logic-for-p-harder-not-easier]`
The active descriptive-complexity frontier is the **separator program**: find a P-query not definable in the strongest candidate logic, separating that one candidate from P. State of play: **FP+C** ruled out (CFI 1992); **rank logic** ruled out (Lichter 2022); **CPT** (Choiceless Polynomial Time, Blass-Gurevich-Shelah 1999) the leading OPEN candidate — CPT = P is open, CFI on fully unordered graphs is open; **CPT+WSC** (Lichter-Schweitzer 2022) open (captures P on a class iff it defines isomorphism there); **Pago** (CSL 2021 / CSL 2023 / MFCS 2023, RWTH Aachen 2023) — CPT cannot define the needed fine preorders in hypercubes, reducing the separator to symmetric-circuit / proof-complexity lower bounds. **The honest point:** separating *one* candidate (CPT/CPT+WSC) from P is a major result but is **NOT** the Gurevich conjecture and **does NOT imply P ≠ NP** — Gurevich needs *every* logic to fail (a universal negative). The P≠NP-relevant lever (the full conjecture) is not what the field attacks head-on and is plausibly **harder** than (A), not easier. The surface sidesteps the construction lock in its *conclusion* but the P≠NP-relevant lever is, if anything, harder.

### A second independent bridge to proof complexity `[descriptive-bridges-to-proof-complexity]`
**Pago CSL 2023:** if CPT distinguishes all non-isomorphic graphs in a class K, then so does the **degree-3 extended polynomial calculus (EPC3)** with polynomial-size refutations. A **super-polynomial EPC3 lower bound for graph isomorphism** on CFI-graphs or multipedes (both **in P**, explicitly constructible) would **separate CPT from P.** This is a direct **bridge from the descriptive surface to propositional proof complexity** — the **second** independent bridge to that face, after the measure→disjoint-pairs→canonical-pairs (Razborov) bridge of Cycle 24. Two genuinely different surfaces now converge on the proof-complexity face as their lower-bound far end, confirming proof complexity as a **multi-surface convergence hub** — and re-confirming `[proof-complexity-face-barriered]` (EPC3 / Frege / EF lower bounds for GI are open).

### The re-collapse — weak-model LBs, in an instance-free form `[descriptive-re-collapse-weak-model-lb]`
The surface's *proof* re-collapses to lower bounds against **weak models**, not to (A)'s balanced-point construction: **symmetric XOR-circuit lower bounds** (Pago MFCS 2023 — a CFI-symmetric CPT algorithm ⟹ certain poly-size symmetric XOR-circuits exist; near-nonexistence proved for restricted symmetric circuits over hypercubes, gap remains) and **EPC3 lower bounds** for the explicitly-given CFI/multipede families. **The shape is genuinely different from `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`** in its standard form: the hard instance is **explicit and freely given** — CFI graphs and multipedes are in P, constructible by a known gadget procedure over Z₂ — so the **construction lock is DROPPED** (the first surface where the "exhibit a hard instance" half is satisfied for free); the open part is the **lower bound against the weak model** for an instance already in hand — closer to the *mining* face's shape (convert an existing structure into a LB) but for a much weaker, more structured model. **Whether natural-proofs / `[locality-barrier]` applies to symmetric-circuit LBs is itself open** — the natural-proofs barrier is formulated for *general* circuit classes; **symmetric circuits** (automorphism-invariant) are a restricted, structured class, and the barrier's extension to them is not established in the literature the search surfaced. A sliver of light, honestly flagged as unmapped, not as a known escape.

## Status
The ESO/0-1-law route is **dead** (the order destroys the 0-1 law for both logics `[shelah-1996]`); EF/pebble games reduce to CFI/circuit-LB difficulty. The orthogonal **logic-for-P lever is alive** and is the **first surface to escape the relativization barrier** (Dahan-Dawar 2020), sidestepping the explicit-construction lock in its *conclusion*; but its *proof* re-collapses to weak-model LBs (symmetric circuits / EPC3) that bridge to proof complexity, and the P≠NP-relevant version (full Gurevich conjecture) is a universal negative *harder* than the active CPT-separator frontier (which reaches only CPT≠P, not P≠NP). NO BREAKTHROUGH; `[honest-ceiling]` upheld; (A) remains the single live thread, the descriptive surface a genuinely-different-shape fifth route that escapes one barrier and bridges to the proof-complexity convergence point.

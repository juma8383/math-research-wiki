# Open Problems

Tracked, with status and the angle that raised each. The highest-leverage targets are flagged ⭐.

## Williams / algorithmic
- ⭐ **Chen 2018 Max-IP shave** `[chen-2018-maxip]` (pushed 2026-08-21 — see sources/2026-08-21-maxip-push.md): `n²·poly(d)/log^{ω(1)} n` for ℓ₂-Furthest-Pair / Z-Max-IP at **polylog d** ⟹ NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR. *Proven-correct sufficient condition.* **Deepened status:** not "only the algorithm missing" — the five natural routes are each blocked, and the approximate-to-exact route is **provably SETH-blocked** `[chen-2020-maxip-tight]` (additive o(d) approx in subquadratic time refutes SETH) `[maxip-shave-obstruction]`. — [[williams-algorithmic]]
- ⭐ **MAJ∘MAJ / THR∘THR CAPP derandomization** `[majmaj-capp-route]` (the reframed live target — **corrected 2026-08-21**, see sources/2026-08-21-majmaj-push.md): a non-trivial `1/poly`-error CAPP for poly-size MAJ∘MAJ ⟹ NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR `[chen-williams-2019]`, *and* NEXP ⊄ MAJ∘MAJ∘MAJ = TC⁰₃ (Chen-Ren Thm 1.13 `[chen-ren-2020]`). **The SAT→CAPP weakening escapes SETH.** But the *MAJ∘MAJ-vs-THR∘THR distinction is illusory* `[majmaj-symmetry-deflated]`: Chen-Williams prove a CAPP for MAJ∘MAJ **iff** for THR∘THR (CAPP-equivalent), so MAJ symmetry gives no PRG advantage — the live question is whether *any* non-trivial CAPP exists for the pair, not whether MAJ admits a special PRG. Both natural PRG mechanisms are bounded away `[dilv-2024]` (small-bias+noise fails threshold tests; k-wise+noise doesn't lift past a single symmetric gate). **Constructive path — the mining program** `[mining-program]`: convert the known HMPT exponential lower bound (IP2 ∉ 2^{(1/2−ε)n}-size depth-2 threshold `[hmpt-1993]`) into a CAPP via an NW reconstruction for threshold classes. **Within-reach lever** `[tell-2018-quantified]`: Tell's quantified-derandomization wire-count improvement (the `c>1` in `n^{1+c^{-d}}`, = the `[chen-tell-2019]` bootstrap) — a *promise* derandomization, not full CAPP. **Mining seam verified 2026-08-21** (sources/2026-08-21-mining-program-seam.md): the *direct* NW-from-HMPT route to the MAJ∘MAJ CAPP is blocked at a **worst-case vs average-case** gap `[mining-worst-vs-average]` — refined (second pass) to a **quantitative+regime gap**: average-case (correlation) LBs for depth-2 threshold **exist** (CSS16 `[css-2018]`: Parity correlation ≤ n^{−ε_d} with ≤ n^{1+ε_d} wires; Kane-Williams `[kane-williams-2016]`: superquadratic-wire average-case) but are **few-wire / polynomial-correlation**, not full-poly-size / exponential; the exponential HMPT/Forster bounds are **worst-case SIZE**, not average-case correlation (discrepancy bounds correlation with *rectangles*, and a size-s MAJ∘THR circuit is richer than s rectangles, so at s=2^{Ω(n)} the correlation is not small). NW/Chen-Ren for a full-poly-size CAPP needs **exponential average-case-correlation** (2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ — **open** (exponential bounds = worst-case-size; average-case bounds = few-wire/polynomial). Amplification (IW XOR lemma) costs overhead + class-drifts to XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ (MAJ∘MAJ not closed under top XOR) + needs a *well-structured* hard function (Chen-Rothblum-Tell-Yogev JACM 2023) HMPT doesn't give; Williams connections go CAPP→LB not LB→CAPP, so an average-case MAJ∘MAJ LB is itself the open problem `[mining-nw-seam]`. **Tell's lever = the CSS16 wire frontier** — CSS16's average-case LB lives at the few-wire regime = Tell's promise regime, and Tell's open wire improvement is the *same question* as extending CSS16's correlation LB to more wires. **Unification** `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`: this obstruction and the meta-complexity S1.a/S2 obstruction are the SAME wall from opposite sides — both need an explicit average-case / lower-bound-carrying witness for a restricted class, whose explicit construction IS the circuit-LB problem (natural-proofs frontier); the cheap inputs (worst-case LB / non-uniformity / randomness) supply it only insufficiently. The genuine open shape: an **exponential average-case-correlation LB for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ** (open; ⟹ mining ⟹ NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR + TC⁰₃), or pushing the CSS16/Tell wire frontier (few-wire n^{1+ε} → more wires) — the two missing ingredients coincide. **Cycle 2 sharpening (2026-08-23, sources/2026-08-23-wire-frontier-push.md):** the gap is **two-dimensional** `[mining-gap-two-dimensional]` — correlation-strength (known: polynomial/subexponential; target: exponential 2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) × wire-regime (known: few-wire ≤ n^{1+ε_d}; target: full poly-size); CSS16 Parity is **wire-tight at n^{1.5}** (wrong function to push past n^{1.5}); the unrestricted THR∘THR worst-case exponential for IP₂ is itself open (Amano `[amano-2020]` / Kane-Williams Open Q #2), so **MAJ∘MAJ (HMPT worst-case anchor) is the only viable target**; Chen-Tal-Wang 2025 `[chen-tal-wang-2025]` n^{2.5−ε} worst-case THR∘THR goes the CAPP→LB direction (opposite of mining). **Cycle 3 (2026-08-23, sources/2026-08-23-nonamplification-route.md): the non-amplification route is CLOSED** `[non-amplification-also-class-drifts]` — the second-pass's suggested "non-amplification" opening (bypass the IW XOR-lemma's drift to XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ) does not escape the drift: STV `[stv-2001]` (PRGs without XOR lemma) and CRTY `[crty-2023]` (arithmetization wc→ac) both use **arithmetic/parity decoder machinery exceeding MAJ∘MAJ** (which lacks parity, HMPT `[hmpt-1993]`); the drift is **not XOR-lemma-specific** but lives in the reconstruction decoder (Goldreich-Rothblum `[goldreich-rothblum-2019]`: known wc→ac works only for parity/arithmetic-containing classes like AC⁰[2], not MAJ∘MAJ). So no new lever; the genuine-open shape reduces to a **direct** (non-reconstructive) exponential average-case-correlation LB for MAJ∘MAJ, OR a MAJ∘MAJ-decodable locally-list-decodable code (equivalent; none known). **Cycle 4 (2026-08-23, sources/2026-08-23-aggregation-crux.md): the obstruction is one level BELOW the decoder drift — at the amplification/aggregation step** `[threshold-aggregation-obstruction]` `[avg-worst-2x2-cell]`. A rough calc (agreement of a threshold of s low-discrepancy functions ≤ O(s·disc), disc(IP₂)=2^{−n/2}) appeared to give exponential average-case correlation at *poly* size — the mining target, contradicting the field; verified **closed**. HMPT decomposed against the primary source: Lindsey (HMPT 3.4) is an avg-case correlation bound at **depth 1 only**; the Discriminator Lemma (HMPT 3.3) is **worst-case** (exact accept-A/reject-B), lifting avg(C)→worst(MAJ∘C), never avg(MAJ∘C). CLLO21 `[cllo-2021]` (Thm 1) proves a *converse* worst(MAJ∘C) ⟺ avg(C) (C⊆NC¹ closed under negation) but returns to **avg(C)**, not avg(MAJ∘C). The mining target — avg(MAJ∘C) with C=depth-1 — is the **uncovered fourth cell** of the (avg/worst)×(C/MAJ∘C) grid `[avg-worst-2x2-cell]`. The linear O(s·disc) bound cannot reach it because the **top threshold is a nonlinear aggregator**: boosting shows low per-gate correlation does NOT bound majority correlation (CLLO21 SUM]-gate gap; amplification fails for classes not closed under majority — depth-1 threshold C is not); Kumar CCC 2023 `[kumar-2023]` shows it concretely (small-bias threshold gates individually uncorrelated with parity yet aggregate to *compute* it once the bias parameter grows); GHR92 (weight ~1/correlation) shows low per-function correlation limits weight but does not prevent aggregation — so aggregate correlation can be high once s ≳ 1/disc ≈ 2^{n/2}, exactly where HMPT's worst-case bound also kicks in; the exponential average-case bound at *poly* size does not follow. This is the **fifth angle** confirming `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`; the genuine-open shape now has the obstruction's *mechanism* identified (defeat majority-aggregation / an anti-boosting correlation-structure bound for IP₂), not merely its location. No derandomization built; no PRG lower bound proved; `[honest-ceiling]`. **4th-loop Cycle 11 (2026-08-23, sources/2026-08-23-mining-face-lever-and-bbnp-barrier.md — tests the third-loop synthesis's `[mining-face-forward-pointer]`; web-verified primary literature — Goldreich-Wigderson STOC 2014, Tell STOC 2018, Chen-Tell STOC 2019, Chen-Jin-Williams STOC 2020, Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022 — search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified):** the mining face's near-term lever IS REAL and quantified — and guarded by a NEW barrier. `[goldreich-wigderson-dichotomy]`: quantified derandomization (badness B(n)) vs standard is a CLASS-DEPENDENT dichotomy — for STRONG classes (compute extractors + approximate majority for error reduction, e.g. AC⁰[2]) quantified derand at subexponential B is AS HARD AS standard (no LB bypass); for WEAK classes (AC⁰) strictly easier. `[tc0-extractor-enables-error-reduction]` `[tell-quantified-derand-is-a-real-lever]`: **TC⁰/MAJ∘MAJ is on the STRONG side** — Tell (STOC 2018) constructs the FIRST TC⁰-specific seeded extractor (n^{1+O(1/d)} wires), the error-reduction bridge; his own theorem: a wire improvement n^{1+exp(−d)} (his algorithm) → n^{1+O(1/d)} ⟹ standard derandomization of all of TC⁰ ⟹ **NEXP ⊄ TC⁰** (and via CAPP-equivalence, NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR). So the quantified derand DOES yield the LB — resolving the open question of whether the promise/quantified relaxation pays off (it does, at threshold). `[wire-frontier-quantitative-gap]`: the lever is **QUANTITATIVE** — a measurable incremental gap with a known algorithm below threshold (n^{1+exp(−d)}) and the LB above it (n^{1+O(1/d)}), fundamentally unlike the meta-complexity S1.a face's qualitative all-or-nothing (A) — a genuine structural asymmetry favoring the mining face. `[black-box-natural-proof-barrier]` `[sharp-threshold-at-phi]`: **BUT the crossing route is guarded by a NEW barrier** — Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022 (a fine-grained Razborov-Rudich variant specific to BOOTSTRAPPING) pin the exact TC⁰ PRF complexity to n^{1+O(φ^{−d})} wires (φ ≈ 1.618, matching Chen-Tell's threshold) and show that a black-box LB technique reaching the threshold + bootstrapping would **DISPROVE PRFs** (believed impossible) — so NO black-box (natural-proof-style) LB technique can cross the wire threshold; crossing needs a **NON-black-box** LB method (the mining analogue of S1.a's non-(P/poly)-recognizable-method requirement). `[mining-face-structurally-different]`: the S1.a compression lens (2-D balanced point with tension) transfers only coarsely — the mining wall is a 2-D gap blocked by ONE mechanism (boosting/aggregation, §Cycle-4), not a balanced point of two tensioned conditions; the mining "tension" (more-wires⟹easier) is a banal monotonicity + the non-trivial boosting mechanism, vs S1.a's non-trivial lifting-intrinsic tension; the two faces are NOT mirror images. `[two-faces-two-np-variants]` (the genuinely novel insight): **BOTH faces of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` are guarded by a natural-proofs-VARIANT, but on DIFFERENT RR axes** — S1.a = Ilango's recognizability barrier (the witness object's constructivity); mining = Fan-Li-Yang's black-box barrier (the proof method's black-box-ness) — the two RR axes (constructivity vs black-box) as the guarding barriers on the two faces; a single idea defeating BOTH variants (a non-recognizable AND non-black-box method) would advance both routes, but the variants are distinct, so defeating one does not defeat the other. **Reframed genuine-open shape:** the mining payoff (NEXP ⊄ TC⁰ / THR∘THR) is now precisely framed as **"cross the wire threshold (n^{1+exp(−d)} → n^{1+φ^{−d}}) with a non-black-box LB method"** — more optimistic than S1.a (a quantitative lever with an algorithm in hand) AND guarded by a new barrier (black-box natural proofs) the S1.a analysis lacked. No breakthrough; `[honest-ceiling]` upheld (web-confirmed not PDF-line-verified; the load-bearing "real lever" fact is Tell's own stated implication; the structural comparison and two-axes unification are readings; no breakthrough). **4th-loop Cycle 12 (2026-08-23, sources/2026-08-23-mining-face-two-barriers-two-axes.md — pursues Cycle 11's directive; web-verified — Williams 2011/2014, Fan-Li-Yang STOC 2022, Chen-Tal-Wang STOC 2026 / ECCC TR26-039 — search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified):** Cycle 11 framed the mining face as ONE barrier (black-box NP); Cycle 12 ADDS the SECOND stacked barrier (the n²-wall) and reveals a TWO-BARRIER/TWO-AXES structure. `[williams-method-is-non-black-box]`: the Williams algorithmic method (NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰) IS non-black-box — it exploits internal ACC structure (SYM+ representation + Evaluation Lemma), circumvents natural proofs via diagonalization (SUCCINCT 3SAT), and is non-relativizing; black-box/oracle methods cannot see this structure. So Williams escapes the Fan-Li-Yang black-box barrier — the mining-axis analogue of how, on the S1.a recognizability axis (Cycles 6-8), Williams' (⇒) is non-targetable; the two RR axes (recognizability of the witness object vs black-box-ness of the proof method) are independent dimensions of "natural-ness," confirmed by Williams' differing status across them. `[n2-wall-and-bbnp-are-two-axes]` `[two-barriers-two-axes-mining-face]`: BUT Williams needs a fast SAT algorithm for the class, and the n²-wall kills the SYM+ route for TC⁰/THR∘THR at threshold wires (threshold approximate degree Θ√t forces ≥2ⁿ monomials at n² wires) — so the mining face has TWO ORTHOGONAL barriers: **#1 the n²-wall** (ALGORITHMIC axis — does the class admit a fast SAT/CAPP algorithm via structure exploitation) and **#2 the black-box NP barrier** (PROOF-THEORETIC axis — is the LB technique black-box). The known methods each escape ONE and fall to the OTHER: Williams = non-black-box (escapes #2) but n²-wall-blocked (falls to #1); the CSS16/Tell CAPP route = escapes #1 (SAT→CAPP bypasses the fast-SAT requirement) but black-box `[capp-route-is-black-box]` (CSS16's random-restriction LB is, per Fan-Li-Yang, "a black-box natural property extracted from a white-box restriction lemma") → blocked by #2. `[williams-sym-plus-coupling]`: for the Williams method the two barriers COUPLE through SYM+ — its non-black-box-ness IS its SYM+ structure-exploitation, exactly what the n²-wall denies for thresholds; it escapes Fan-Li-Yang precisely where the n²-wall does not apply (ACC⁰), and for TC⁰ both fire through the same mechanism (the mining analogue of how the S1.a two gates coupled through one linchpin, the tight-window upper bound). `[chen-tal-wang-inhabits-target-quadrant]` `[target-quadrant-not-at-mining-regime]`: the target quadrant (non-black-box ∧ escapes-n²-wall) is NOT empty — Chen-Tal-Wang (STOC 2026, ECCC TR26-039; previously recorded as "STOC 2025" — minor dating correction, additive) apply the Williams method (non-black-box) with a NEW 2^{n−n^{Ω(ε)}} CAPP algorithm for an XOR-of-two n^{2.5−ε}-size THR∘THR circuits (a DIFFERENT route than the single-polynomial SYM+ SAT the n²-wall kills) ⟹ a superquadratic worst-case LB (f ∈ E^NP requires n^{2.5−ε}-size THR∘THR, breaking the decade-old sub-quadratic gate barrier).

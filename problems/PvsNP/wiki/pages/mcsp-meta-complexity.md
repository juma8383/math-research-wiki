# Meta-complexity / MCSP

## Core
The **Minimum Circuit Size Problem** `[mcsp-def]`: given the truth table T of a Boolean function (length 2ⁿ) and a size parameter s, decide whether there exists a Boolean circuit of size ≤ s computing T. MCSP is in NP (the circuit is the witness). Its exact complexity is **open** — neither known in P nor NP-complete; believed NP-intermediate. Meta-complexity is the study of the complexity of computing complexity-theoretic quantities, and MCSP is its canonical problem: "how hard is it to compute the circuit complexity of a given function?"

The **meta-complexity thesis** `[meta-complexity-thesis]`: understanding the complexity of computing complexity is the key to resolving circuit lower bounds; the difficulty of P vs NP is bound up with the difficulty of MCSP itself. Developed across work by Allender, Hirahara, Carmosino-Impagliazzo-Kabanets-Kolokolova, Oliveira-Santhanam, Ilango, and others.

This is the angle the 8-agent fan-out **missed** entirely `[mcsp-gap]`, and the highest-upside long-term direction in the field (see [[missed-angles]]). Unlike the 8 surveyed angles — which refine existing invariant families (combinatorial, algebraic, dynamical, proof-theoretic, model-theoretic) — meta-complexity **reframes** the lower-bound question as the hardness of a single NP problem.

## Landmarks (attributions verified by the deep-dive — see sources/2026-08-21-mcsp-deep-dive.md)
- **Not easy, not NP-complete (open).** The **unconditional** AC⁰[p] lower bound for MCSP is `[gikkt-2019]` (Golovnev-Ilango-Impagliazzo-Kabanets-Kolokolova-Tal, ICALP 2019, "via the Coin Problem"): MCSP needs depth-d AC⁰[p] circuits ≥ exp(N^{0.49/d}). The **MKTP** (Kolmogorov-variant) precursor MKTP ∉ AC⁰[p] is `[allender-hirahara-2017]`; improved AC⁰ bounds via local PRGs are `[cklmz-2021]`. (The primer's "Akhmedov-Carlson-Cook; Murray-Williams-style" attribution was imprecise — corrected here.) MCSP is **hard for SZK** `[ad-2014]` (Allender & Das, MFCS 2014 / Inf. & Comp. 2017, "Zero Knowledge and Circuit Minimization"): every problem in SZK reduces to MCSP in BPP-time; hence Graph Isomorphism ∈ RP^MCSP.
- `[mcsp-nphard-owf]` — if MCSP were NP-hard under "natural" reductions (parsimonious / polytime many-one), one-way functions would not exist (Liu-Pass 2020 / Ren-Santhanam 2021 / Allender-Das — a *family* of results, not one clean theorem). So MCSP's NP-hardness is believed false under cryptographic assumptions. The obstruction applies to a *specific* reduction class and does **not** rule out NP-hardness under more general reductions. Framing: "MCSP is to P vs NP what 3SUM is to fine-grained."
- `[mazor-pass-2024]` — Mazor-Pass CCC 2024: **Gap-MCSP is not Levin-NP-complete in Obfustopia** (under iO + subexponential OWFs). A *negative* obstruction the primer missed, tightening the no-NP-hardness evidence on the Gap/promise variant.
- `[cikk-2016]` — Carmosino-Impagliazzo-Kabanets-Kolokolova 2016: natural proofs constructive against P/poly imply PAC learning algorithms for P/poly. A "natural" circuit lower bound yields a learning algorithm, and (contrapositive) hardness-of-learning conversely blocks natural proofs — **CIKK is both a bridge and the trap that re-expresses the RR barrier in learning-theoretic language.** Connects the natural-proofs barrier, PAC learnability, and MCSP — three facets of one phenomenon. See [[barriers]].
- `[hirahara-2018]` — Hirahara FOCS 2018: non-black-box worst-case-to-average-case reductions within NP via MCSP/MINKT. **If GapMCSP (a promise version) is NP-hard, then "Heuristica" does not exist** — collapsing worst-case and average-case NP hardness. The implication is proven; the premise is the open condition. This is a **structural collapse, not a circuit lower bound against P/poly.**
- `[hirahara-ilango-2025]` — Hirahara-Ilango FOCS 2025: the **first conditional NP-hardness of MCSP**, conditional on (coNP subexp nondeterministic circuits) + (P^NP/poly circuits of size Ω(2ⁿ/n)) + (subexp witness-indistinguishable proofs for SAT), under quasi-polynomial-time nonadaptive reductions. A landmark toward de-conditioning MCSP hardness.
- `[impagliazzo-worlds]` — Impagliazzo's five worlds (Algorithmica, Heuristica, Pessiland, Minicrypt, Cryptomania) frame where MCSP hardness and P vs NP sit; MCSP's hardness is tied to which world we are in.

## The de-conditioning trajectory `[mcsp-deconditioning]`
The empirical signature that makes this the most promising direction: MCSP hardness results have been **progressively de-conditioned** over the last decade — from fully conditional, toward conditional-on-weaker-assumptions (`[hirahara-ilango-2025]`), with the community hope being eventual unconditional hardness. This is the one current program where near-, medium-, and long-term targets are **all circuit lower bounds directly** (not proof-system or geometric proxies), and where conditional results of the form "if MCSP (or a variant) is hard, then circuit lower bounds follow" actually exist.

## Barrier position — CORRECTED `[barrier-evasion-overstated]`
The primer claimed MCSP is "orthogonal to the three-barrier framework… not caught by the barriers-union… a reframing, not a technique class." **Adversarial examination (deep-dive Facet 4) downgrades this to PARTIALLY TRUE / OVERSTATED.** Corrected statement: MCSP is a **candidate route that may thread the barriers**, with **no rigorous evasion proven on any of the three**, and a **descendant barrier already spawned** for its magnification sub-route.

- **Natural proofs — CAUGHT in the easy direction.** `[mcsp-easy-naturalproof]` MCSP ∈ P would *directly* yield a constructive+large+useful property against P/poly ("circuit-complexity ≥ 2^{εn}") — a **direct corollary of Razborov-Rudich 1997** the primer under-weighted by routing the natural-proofs link only through CIKK's learning restatement. MCSP ∈ P would contradict strong OWFs/PRGs.
- **Natural proofs — evasive-in-principle, RELOCATED in practice.** A lower bound for the *named* function MCSP need not satisfy largeness (same kernel as Williams/GCT), so an MCSP lower bound can in principle be non-natural. **But** the live MCSP-to-LB mechanism is **hardness magnification** `[os-2018]`, which hits a new MCSP-specific barrier: the **locality barrier** `[locality-barrier]` (Chen-Lyu-Potyagailo-Santhanam, attribution unverified at exact-paper level) — weak-MCSP-lower-bound techniques "localize" and provably cannot cross the magnification threshold. So the natural-proofs difficulty is **relocated, not evaded**: the barrier treadmill (`[barrier-treadmill]`, [[barriers]]) continues and MCSP is not exempt.
- **Relativization — structurally optimistic, unproven.** Hirahara's non-black-box reductions are structurally non-relativizing (precedent: arithmetization), and oracle-sensitivity is *expected* (an oracle can encode circuit-complexity info, making MCSP^A easy). **But** no rigorous BGS-style oracle pair currently proves MCSP lower bounds *must* be non-relativizing. The **MCSP oracle pair** (an A with MCSP^A ∈ P^A, a B with MCSP^B hard) is the cleanest open test.
- **Algebrization — entirely unexamined.** No published analysis of MCSP-based lower bounds against the Aaronson-Wigderson 2008 algebraic-oracle barrier, in either direction. Only a weak structural hint (MINKT's Kolmogorov/information-theoretic character plausibly beyond low-degree encodings).
- **The category error.** The three barriers classify **proof techniques**, not problem statements. "MCSP is a reframing" (true) does **not** entail "an MCSP-based *proof* evades the barriers" (unestablished). Williams and GCT are *specific techniques* verified non-relativizing/non-natural; **MCSP has produced no concrete lower-bound technique verified against any of the three.** "Not caught by the barriers-union because it is a reframing" conflates *unclassified because no technique exists yet* with *barrier-immune.* See [[barriers-union]].

## Why it is the highest-upside direction (with the honest gap)
A resolution of MCSP's exact complexity — or even strong conditional hardness — would directly advance circuit lower bounds. The 8-angle fan-out refined existing invariant families; meta-complexity alone reframes the lower-bound question as the hardness of a single NP problem. That makes it the clear second wave and the critic's top-rated long-term target. **But the honest gap (most prone to hype):** most actual MCSP landmarks give circuit lower bounds against **restricted** classes or **structural collapses** (Heuristica non-existence), **not P≠NP**. The bridge from "MCSP is hard" to "P≠NP" is **formal but itself the unproven, hardest part**, and the active de-conditioning trajectory is **not currently aimed at the reduction class that would yield P≠NP.**

## The only two formal MCSP→P≠NP bridges `[route-a-route-b]` `[goldilocks-structure]`
The `[murray-williams-2017]` Goldilocks structure makes the *consequence* of MCSP NP-hardness depend on the **reduction class**: uniform-AC⁰-hard ⟹ NP ⊄ P/poly ⟹ **P≠NP** (Murray-Williams Thm 1.8); polytime-hard ⟹ **EXP≠ZPP** (Thm 1.6, not P≠NP); logspace ⟹ PSPACE≠ZPP (Thm 1.7); local `TIME(n^{1/2−ε})` ⟹ **provably not NP-hard** (Thm 1.3/1.5). Two formal bridges to P≠NP, both out of reach:
- **Route A** — NP-hardness of MCSP under **logtime-uniform AC⁰ many-one reductions** ⟹ NP ⊄ P/poly ⟹ P≠NP. Sits in the Goldilocks zone next to the provably-impossible local reductions; no proof either way. **Sharpened 2026-08-21** (see sources/2026-08-21-routeA-push.md):
  - **Compression crux** `[route-a-compression-crux]`: a uniform-AC⁰ reduction must encode the `n`-bit satisfiability of a SAT instance into the circuit complexity of a function on only `O(log n)` bits (the output truth table has length `poly(n)` ⟹ `m=log N=O(log n)` bits). A full construction is *at least as hard as* NP ⊄ P/poly — i.e. the construction IS the breakthrough, not a step toward it. Not constructed.
  - **Variant gap** `[route-a-variant-gap]` (sharpened 2026-08-21, see sources/2026-08-21-s2-primary-source.md): the precise open question is the **non-uniform → uniform (logtime-uniform) AC⁰ many-one** gap. Ilango (TR19-021, ITCS 2020) proved **MOCSP is NP-hard under *non-uniform* AC⁰ many-one reductions** (Corollary 4.9) — so a meta-complexity variant *does* reach AC⁰ many-one, just non-uniformly. **No** MCSP variant is known NP-hard under *uniform* AC⁰ many-one. Every other achieved NP-hardness (DNF-MCSP Masek 1979; DNF∘XOR-MCSP HOS 2018; multi-output Ilango-Loff-Oliveira 2020; `(AC⁰_d)-MCSP` Ilango FOCS 2020; MFSP Ilango 2020) is for a restricted/oracle/partial/formula variant and uses *randomized* / *Turing* / *quasipoly-or-polytime* reductions. The closest *uniform* target, Ilango's `(AC⁰_d)-MCSP`, is still randomized + quasipoly-time + Turing + restricted-class. The exact Route A reduction class is open even for the restricted variants.
  - **S2 — the non-uniformity is load-bearing** `[s2-irritable-witness-obstruction]` (analyzed 2026-08-21, see sources/2026-08-21-s2-primary-source.md): the MOCSP non-uniform reduction (TR19-021 §4) hardwires an **(rn)-irritable** truth table T (length m=O(n³), a function on L=O(log n) bits) as *advice depending only on input length*; the rest of the map is genuinely AC⁰. A random T is (rn)-irritable w.h.p. (counting argument, failure 2^{−Ω(n²)}), so the non-uniformity *supplies* a high-complexity witness explicit construction cannot. **Uniformizing requires an explicit T with general-circuit complexity > rn = Θ(n) = 2^{Θ(L)}** — an *explicit exponential (in L) general-circuit lower bound*. The best known explicit general-circuit LB is **Θ(L)** (Blum 1984 ~3L; Find et al. ~5L) — *linear in L*, i.e. Θ(log n) — falling **exponentially short** of the required Θ(n)=2^{Θ(L)}. The threshold 2^{Θ(L)} is structural (the partition needs m ≥ n; r is a constant), so no parameter tuning makes it sub-exponential. The non-uniform→uniform gap **is exactly** an explicit exponential circuit lower bound — the open natural-proofs / circuit-LB frontier. **Honest scope:** this rules out uniformizing *this* reduction via explicit-T construction; it does not rule out a structurally different uniform-AC⁰ reduction, and is not P≠NP regardless (MOCSP is a restricted/oracle variant; the restricted→original gap remains).
  - **Unifying diagnosis** `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` (see sources/2026-08-21-s2-primary-source.md): S1.a and S2 are two faces of **one** obstruction — across the meta-complexity AC⁰ reductions, the load-bearing non-uniformity/randomness supplies a *witness object* (irritable T / Lupanov g) whose existence is proved *probabilistically* and which *carries a provable circuit lower bound*. Derandomizing/uniformizing requires **explicitly constructing** such a witness — i.e. an explicit function with a proven circuit lower bound of the required magnitude — which *is* the circuit-lower-bound problem. The non-uniformity/randomness is not a removable technicality: it is the only known way to obtain a high-complexity (lower-bound-carrying) witness. The two instances differ in *which* lower bound bottlenecks: **DNF** (S1.a — explicit exponential DNF LBs *exist*, so the tight-window + derandomization-of-the-exponential-union is the crux `[s1a-antiprox-obstruction]`) vs **general circuits** (S2 — explicit LBs are stuck at linear, so the *exponential LB itself* is the crux). S2 is the harder of the two: the non-uniform→uniform gap is already at the explicit-exponential-circuit-LB frontier, *before* the full Route A (restricted→original) gap. **This unification was confirmed (2026-08-21) to span the *Williams/mining* route too** (see sources/2026-08-21-mining-program-seam.md): the mining program `[mining-program]` needs an explicit **average-case-hard** function for the restricted class MAJ∘MAJ; HMPT's worst-case LB doesn't suffice (amplification drifts the class to XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ `[mining-worst-vs-average]` `[mining-nw-seam]`), and the Williams connections are one-way (CAPP→LB). **Refined (second pass):** the gap is a *quantitative+regime* one — average-case correlation LBs for depth-2 threshold **exist** but are few-wire (n^{1+ε}) / polynomial-correlation (CSS16 `[css-2018]`, Kane-Williams `[kane-williams-2016]`); the exponential HMPT/Forster bounds are **worst-case SIZE**, not average-case correlation (discrepancy bounds correlation with *rectangles*, and a size-s MAJ∘THR circuit is richer than s rectangles). NW needs **exponential average-case-correlation** (2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ — open. Tell's promise/wire-count lever `[tell-2018-quantified]` **is the CSS16 wire frontier** (the same question as extending CSS16's correlation LB to more wires). So the same `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` wall governs both live routes from opposite sides — meta-complexity needs the witness to uniformize a reduction; Williams needs the average-case witness to build a derandomization.
  - **Dual locality** `[route-a-dual-locality]`: the `[locality-barrier]` `[choprs-2020]` (CHOPRS ITCS 2020 / JACM 2022) blocks route B (LB-proving side) AND touches Route A — the natural satisfiability-conditional gap constructions are magnification-style and unconditionally produce the small-fan-in-oracle circuits that defeat the LB techniques. The one crack, CHOPRS Thm 49 (a non-localizable LB for an Andreev-like function, *not* MCSP), proves such LBs can exist. **Refined 2026-08-23 (see sources/2026-08-23-s3-choprs-thm49.md):** the locality barrier and the `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` barrier are **distinct and orthogonal** `[locality-vs-witness-orthogonal]` — Thm 49 escapes locality precisely by hardwiring an *existential* hard function (exhaustive-search f_hard); the non-localizability is bought with non-uniformity. So a locality escape is available in principle, but it lands on the witness barrier; Route A is pinned by the *witness* barrier even where a locality escape exists.
  - **OWF-escape (a point in favor)** `[route-a-owf-escape]`: the obstructions blocking the *strong* rungs do **not** obviously block the weak uniform-AC⁰ rung. The OWF obstruction `[mcsp-nphard-owf]` targets *polytime* reductions; the Mazor-Pass negative `[mazor-pass-2024]` targets *Levin/gap* reductions. Uniform-AC⁰ many-one on exact MCSP sits *below* both — Route A's resistance is **structural (locality/compression), not cryptographic**. (Plausible escape, not proven; flagged.)
  - **Concrete falsifiable sub-targets:** (S1) derandomize Ilango's `(AC⁰_d)-MCSP` reduction to *deterministic*; (S2) analyze the actual depth/uniformity of the Masek DNF-MCSP reduction toward a weak class; (S3) port CHOPRS Thm 49's non-localizable construction to a meta-computational problem (cracks route B's locality wall; informs Route A's gap by duality). `[ilango-2020]`

    - **S3 analyzed against the primary source 2026-08-23** (see sources/2026-08-23-s3-choprs-thm49.md — verified negative): CHOPRS Thm 49 gives a *non-localizable* LB for an Andreev-like function L ∈ E (formula world, *not* AC⁰; not MCSP) — proving the `[locality-barrier]` is not absolute. **But the escape is purchased with an existential hard function**: f_hard is found by exhaustive search in 2^{O(N)} time and cannot be 0.8-approximated by formulas of size 2^{(1−ε/2)m} (m=log N bits) — an **explicit exponential (in m) formula LB**, open (best explicit Ω̃(m³) cubic, Håstad 1998 / Filmus-Meir-Tal 2023; shrinkage exponent tight — the "n³ barrier").

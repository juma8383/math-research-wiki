# The Three Barriers

Every prior attempt to prove P≠NP was killed by one of three barriers. Any genuine attack must be **all three** of non-relativizing, non-natural, non-algebrizing `[three-barriers-required]`.

## The three

- **Relativization** `[bgs-1975]` — Oracles A (P^A=NP^A) and B (P^B≠NP^B) exist. Any technique that treats machines the same with/without an oracle (diagonalization, simulation, hierarchy theorems) gives the same answer in both worlds → contradiction. Killed pure diagonalization.
- **Natural proofs** `[rr-1997]` — A "natural" circuit-LB property is constructive (recognizable from the truth table in poly time in 2ⁿ) and large (holds for a non-negligible fraction of functions). If strong OWFs exist, no natural property can prove super-poly circuit LBs — it would double as a PRF distinguisher. Killed the combinatorial circuit-LB program.
- **Algebrization** `[aw-2008]` — Arithmetization (low-degree polynomial encodings) was the non-relativizing trick behind IP=PSPACE, the one big non-relativizing success. But algebraic-oracle worlds with P=NP and P≠NP exist, so even arithmetization-based proofs fail. Killed the IP=PSPACE/PCP family as a route to P vs NP.

## The barrier treadmill `[barrier-treadmill]`
Each breakthrough technique spawns a new barrier: diagonalization→relativization; arithmetization→algebrization; combinatorial bounds→natural proofs. Pattern: *every sufficiently generic/constructive technique provably fails.* This reframes "prove P≠NP" as "characterize the boundary of constructive proof" — see [[barriers-union]] for the attempted unified fourth barrier.

## What escapes
- Williams' algorithmic method — non-relativizing, non-natural; see [[williams-algorithmic]].
- **GCT — escapes all THREE local barriers** `[gct-escapes-three-barriers]` (Cycle 26 upgrade): non-natural (obstructions not constructible as a truth-table predicate; characterization-by-symmetries ⟹ extreme rigidity ⟹ bypasses RR largeness), non-algebrizing (no oracle extensions; representation-theoretic structure beyond degree), AND non-relativizing (works on the algebrized form of computation, IP=PSPACE spirit). Mulmuley Thm 8.3 bypasses all three simultaneously with strongly explicit obstructions — the **first surface in the 26-cycle map with a clean barrier profile**. (Mulmuley's claim, search/arXiv-summary-level, NOT PDF-line-verified; the rigidity↔natural-proofs part is accepted in the GCT literature but not an RR-framework theorem.) See [[gct]].
- **The logic-for-P / descriptive surface** `[descriptive-escapes-relativization]` — escapes **relativization for free**: Dahan-Dawar 2020 construct an oracle with a logic for P^A but P^A ≠ NP^A, so the lever "no-logic-for-P ⟹ P≠NP" is a non-relativizing theorem — any proof through it is automatically non-relativizing. The **first surface in 24 cycles to circumvent the relativization barrier** (every prior surface was relativization-blocked). It does NOT escape natural-proofs/algebrization on its *proof* (which re-collapses to weak-model LBs). See [[descriptive-complexity]] § The logic-for-P lever.
- They escape for **orthogonal reasons** (algorithmic structure vs symmetry-rigidity vs descriptive non-relativizing) — this is load-bearing for whether a single 4th barrier can capture all three (probably not). See [[barriers-union]].

## The local/universal distinction — barriers are local, the construction lock is universal `[barriers-and-construction-are-complementary]` (Cycle 26 — the sharpened meta-finding)
The three (four) barriers are **"local and mathematical"** (Mulmuley): each rules out only a restricted technique family — diagonalization→relativization, arithmetization→algebrization, combinatorial bounds→natural proofs, degree reasoning→degree barrier. **GCT is the existence proof that escaping all three local barriers does NOT remove the wall — it ISOLATES it.** GCT pays to escape all three (rigidity, algebrized form, representation-theoretic structure) and is left *exactly* with the **flip** `[gct-flip-is-construction-lock]` — the explicit short-witness construction = `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` in algebraic guise. Mulmuley names this residual the **universal complexity barrier** (self-referential: "discovery is hard" ⟹ "discovering this proof is hard"); its formalization IS the short-witness problem `[mulmuley-complexity-barrier-equals-witness-barrier]`. Triangulated with the descriptive surface (escapes *one* barrier, drops the construction lock on the *instance* side because CFI/multipedes are explicit + in P, but retains the weak-model-LB construction), the two surfaces pin the wall on two orthogonal axes: **barrier-clean yet construction-locked (GCT) vs instance-free yet LB-locked (descriptive)** — no surface is clean on both. **The real obstruction is the construction lock, and no barrier-escape removes it.** The wall is *not* the three barriers (local symptoms); the wall *is* the universal short-witness construction problem. The construction lock even **bites twice** in GCT `[flip-construction-objects-are-nphard]`: the flip's Kronecker/plethysm coefficients are NP-hard/#P-hard (IMW 2017, FI 2020), so the poly-time constructibility the flip requires collides with the very hardness it proves. This is the strongest, externally-grounded form of `[no-missed-connection-moved-wall]` and the sharpest `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`, confirmed from a FIFTH sub-surface (structural→measure→disjoint-pairs/proof-complexity→descriptive→GCT/algebraic).

## The natural-proofs / learning / MCSP triangle `[cikk-2016]`
Carmosino-Impagliazzo-Kabanets-Kolokolova 2016 (CCC, Art. 10): a **P-natural** (constructive) property with largeness ≥ 1/5 that is **useful** against a circuit class Λ containing AC⁰[p] yields a randomized **PAC-learning** algorithm for Λ. So the natural-proofs barrier `[rr-1997]`, PAC learnability, and MCSP are three facets of one phenomenon. CIKK is **double-edged**: by contrapositive, if Λ is cryptographically hard to PAC-learn (the standard RR assumption: PRFs in P/poly), then **no** constructive+large+useful property against Λ can exist — because such a property would, by CIKK, yield a learner. So CIKK is **both** the bridge (lower-bound ⟹ learner) **and** the trap that re-expresses the RR barrier in learning-theoretic language. It does not evade the barrier so much as expose its dual face: hardness-of-learning is the positive obverse of the barrier that killed the combinatorial program. The meta-complexity program ([[mcsp-meta-complexity]]) makes this triangle a first-class angle rather than a barrier footnote.

## The measure facet `[rsc-martingale-naturalproof-equivalence]`
Regan-Sivakumar-Cai 1995 (FOCS / ECCC TR95-006) add a **measure facet** to the same phenomenon: a martingale (Lutz betting strategy) succeeding on a class C ⟺ (Thm 18, partial converse with density conditions) a natural property diagonalizing against C; P/poly measure-0 in EXP ⟹ a natural proof against P/poly ⟹ no strong PSRGs; strong PSRGs ⟹ P/poly not measurable in EXP. Honest martingales (Thm 17) **strengthen** RR against uniform adversaries. So **martingales / measure / natural proofs** are facets of one barrier — the wiki's recurring "one barrier, several facets" structure (learning `[cikk-2016]`, search/decision/property `[apepp-mcsp-np-one-triangle]`, measure) — see [[resource-bounded-measure]]. The measure pivot (Cycle 23) re-discovers the natural-proofs barrier from the measure side `[measure-pivot-rediscovers-natural-proofs]`: proving μ_p(NP)≠0 ≡ proving NP contains a p-random (hard) set `[positive-measure-means-np-pseudorandom]` — re-collapsing to `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`; the measure surface also inherits the relativization barrier (oracles both ways) `[measure-relativization-barrier]`, so it does not escape `[three-barriers-required]`. **The one new target's barrier profile is mapped (Cycle 24)** `[disjoint-pairs-stronger-not-easier]` `[disjoint-pairs-relativize]` `[disjoint-pairs-re-collapse-to-inseparable-pair]` `[disjoint-pairs-inherit-rsc]`: the 2022 disjoint-pairs condition `dim(disjNP|disjEXP)=1` is *stronger* than (implies) the direct dimension hypothesis (not an easier shortcut), is relativization-blocked (oracles both ways — Fortnow-Rogers, Glaßer et al., Fortnow-Lutz-Mayordomo), and re-collapses via RSC to exhibiting a P-inseparable disjoint NP pair — a differently-shaped witness that nonetheless re-collapses to `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`. Disjoint NP pairs are the **bridge** `[disjoint-pairs-bridge-to-proof-complexity]` from the measure facet to propositional proof complexity (Razborov's canonical pairs have identical degree structure to DisjNP), and the proof-complexity far end is itself barriered `[proof-complexity-face-barriered]` — see [[disjoint-np-pairs]].

## MCSP is directly caught by natural proofs in the easy direction `[mcsp-easy-naturalproof]`
The triangle's learning framing (CIKK) can obscure a **more direct** link the deep-dive surfaced. If **MCSP ∈ P** (decidable in poly(2ⁿ) time), then for any ε>0 the property "circuit-complexity(T) ≥ 2^{εn}" is (i) constructive [via MCSP], (ii) large [a random function has complexity ~2ⁿ/n ≫ 2^{εn} w.h.p.], (iii) useful against P/poly — a **direct corollary of Razborov-Rudich 1997**, independent of the CIKK learning detour. So MCSP ∈ P would contradict strong OWFs/PRGs. The meta-complexity route inherits the natural-proofs barrier to the *exact extent* its argument factors through a constructive+large+useful property; the escape attempts (hardness magnification `[os-2018]` destroying largeness; average-case/gap/relativized variants dropping constructivity-as-truth-table-predicate) are attempts to **drop a barrier hypothesis**, not refute its conclusion — and the magnification escape itself hits a **descendant** barrier, the `[locality-barrier]` (Chen-Lyu-Potyagailo-Santhanam, attribution unverified). The barrier treadmill (`[barrier-treadmill]`, above) continues: MCSP is not exempt. See [[mcsp-meta-complexity]] § Barrier position for the full corrected assessment that downgrades the primer's "orthogonal to the barriers" claim.

## The depth/KW surface: evades natural-proofs (plausibly) but is algebrization-blocked — a second axis for the local/universal distinction `[depth-axis-barrier-profiled-not-untouched]` `[krw-may-evade-natural-proofs]` `[chen-hu-ren-2026-algebrization-via-communication]` (Cycle 28 — the depth axis barrier-profiled)

The wiki's central wall-diagnosis was built entirely on the **size** lower-bound axis. Cycle 28 profiles the orthogonal **depth** axis (Karchmer-Wigderson D(f)=C(R_f); the KRW conjecture D(g∘f)≈D(g)+D(f) ⟹ P ⊄ NC¹) — the FIRST non-size axis barrier-profiled against `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` (the page `communication-kw` already mapped the machinery; the cycle profiles it against the wall). Two barrier facts:
- **Natural-proofs (plausibly) evaded** `[krw-may-evade-natural-proofs]`: KRW targets a SPECIFIC constructed function (random log n-bit function composed with itself O(log n) times, explicit via its n-bit description), not a random function — so it may violate RR "largeness." Same escape-shape as GCT's rigidity (`[gct-escapes-three-barriers]`): escape RR by a specific non-generic target, not a generic large property. Second surface (after GCT) with a plausible RR escape, here on the depth axis. (Search-summary reading, NOT an RR-framework theorem; the `communication-kw` page's randomized-protocol barrier caps discrepancy/rank/information methods at O(log n) regardless.)
- **Algebrization NOT evaded** `[chen-hu-ren-2026-algebrization-via-communication]`: Chen-Hu-Ren (ITCS 2026, LIPIcs.ITCS.2026.37) build NEW algebrization barriers via communication complexity of XOR-Missing-String — PostBPE ⊄ i.o.-SIZE[O(n)] needs non-algebrizing techniques; Aaronson-Wigderson improved to MULTILINEAR extensions (BPE^{Ã₂} has linear-size A₂-oracle circuits); half-exponential the correct algebrizing barrier. The very toolbox the depth surface uses (communication complexity) is the one 2026 sharpens algebrization with. So the depth surface trades ONE local barrier (natural-proofs) for ANOTHER (algebrization) — unlike GCT (which claimed to escape all three), the depth surface escapes at most one. This re-confirms `[barriers-and-construction-are-complementary]` (Cycle 26): barriers are LOCAL; escaping one does not remove the construction lock or the other local barriers.

**The depth-face of the construction lock** `[direct-sum-is-depth-face-of-noncompositionality]`: the KRW direct-sum obstacle (`[meir-2023]`, Meir FOCS 2023 — R_{g∘f} must require solving both R_g and R_f; strong-composition proved, the first obstacle isolated as crucial) IS the depth-face of the non-compositionality construction lock `[balanced-point-non-compositional]`. The composed object does not decompose into its parts — on the SIZE axis, the lifting witness g must be simultaneously expensive ∧ small-gap NON-COMPOSITIONALLY (S1.a lifting); on the DEPTH axis, g∘f must behave as a direct sum but does not (KRW). The genuinely new structural product: the non-compositionality lock is AXIS-INDEPENDENT — it blocks the size axis (S1.a lifting) and the depth axis (KRW direct-sum) alike. The wall's lock is specifically a NON-COMPOSITIONAL construction, a sharpening of `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` holding across both classical LB axes. Transfer gap `[depth-transfer-gap-p-vs-nc1]`: KRW ⟹ P ≠ NC¹, NOT P ≠ NP (same necessary-not-sufficient shape as descriptive CPT≠P, GCT VP≠VNP). Rigidity sub-route `[alman-liang-2025-rigidity-stuck-barrier]`: a precise "barrier just beyond reach" (Alman-Liang 2025 near-tight low-rank LB + hardness amplification), the depth/linear-circuit analogue of the S1.a tight window. NO BREAKTHROUGH; `[honest-ceiling]` upheld; re-confirms `[no-missed-connection-moved-wall]` from the SEVENTH sub-surface (structural → measure → disjoint-pairs/proof-complexity → descriptive → GCT/algebraic → direct-attack-on-(A) → depth/KW-communication).

# Communication Complexity & KW

## Core
Karchmer-Wigderson: depth(f) = CC(KW_f); formula_size(f) = Θ(leaves of the optimal KW protocol). Strongest explicit De Morgan formula LB = n^{3-o(1)} (Håstad 1998), unchanged in exponent for ~27 years.

## Ceiling
**Full success (the KRW conjecture) yields P≠NC¹** — super-polynomial formula size/depth — **NOT P≠NP**, because formula lower bounds do not imply general circuit lower bounds (formulas ⊊ circuits; P/poly ⊋ NC¹ not even known). As a *direct* P vs NP attack, KW is weak-to-medium.

## Two technique-level barriers (not the big three)
- **Randomized-protocol barrier** — every KW_f has an O(log n)-bit randomized protocol, so all discrepancy/rank/information-complexity methods are capped at O(log n) and cannot prove super-log deterministic KW LBs.
- **Direct-sum obstacle** `[meir-2023]` — KW handles the tensor direct-product of relations, but KRW needs **nested composition** f∘g, a different object. The Iyer-Rao entropy port `[iyer-rao-2024]` to output-valid rectangles reproduces a known-flavor direct-sum and does **not** cross the composition wall.

## The P≠NP-adjacent thread (KW-descended, outside the comm toolbox)
`[cavalar-oliveira-2025]`: an **explicit** family of graphs with super-logarithmic (ideally linear) **graph-cover complexity** ⟹ general circuit-SIZE lower bounds. Random graphs achieve linear cover complexity but are non-explicit. This is the most concrete known reduction from general circuit-SIZE LBs to a combinatorial object, and the genuine P≠NP-adjacent target in this lineage.

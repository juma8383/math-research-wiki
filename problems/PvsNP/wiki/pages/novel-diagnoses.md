# Novel Diagnoses

The workflow's genuinely novel contributions (flagged as not-stated-as-such in the surveyed literature; the most valuable outputs per the critic).

## 1. GCT coarsening barrier + fixed-degree escape
- `[coarsening-gap]` — both GCT no-gos (`[bip-2019]` occurrence, `[elsw-2017]` shifted-partials/sums) rule out only **coarse** invariants. Fine **individual** multiplicity differences are open: a single multiplicity can exceed the determinant's even when the sum does not.
- `[fixed-degree-escape]` — `[fi-2020-asymmetry]` makes the permanent-side plethysm P-time for fixed outer degree d, concentrating ALL difficulty on the determinant orbit-closure boundary (unknown n≥9).
- **Falsifiable experiment**: classify holes of S(Det_n) for n=4,5; check for positive plethysm. Either outcome is publishable. See [[gct]].

## 2. Meta-duality thesis
`[meta-duality]` — for a witnessing theory T, "T does not prove LB" is dual to a weak upper bound. Independence is the **same difficulty** as the direct lower bound, dualized. Three-layer trichotomy (Layer A unconditional but PH-internal; Layer B conditional on derandomization; Layer C near-collapse). Krajíček Problem 3.2 is a fixed point (LB xor independence). See [[meta-duality]].

## 3. Heterogeneity argument against a unified fourth barrier
Williams and GCT evade the three barriers for **orthogonal** reasons; no single access model bounds both. A clean broad fourth barrier is probably not formulable, and establishing one is plausibly P-vs-NP-hard (the meta-barrier). See [[barriers-union]].

## 4. MCSP barrier-evasion overclaim corrected `[barrier-evasion-overstated]` (deep-dive diagnosis)
The MCSP primer claimed meta-complexity is "orthogonal to the three-barrier framework… not caught by the barriers-union… a reframing, not a technique class." The deep-dive (sources/2026-08-21-mcsp-deep-dive.md, Facet 4) adversarially tested this and **downgraded it**: the three barriers classify **proof techniques**, not problem statements, so "MCSP is a reframing" does **not** entail "an MCSP-based proof evades the barriers." Concretely: MCSP is **caught** by natural proofs in the easy direction (MCSP ∈ P ⟹ a constructive+large+useful property — a direct `[rr-1997]` corollary, `[mcsp-easy-naturalproof]`); evasive-in-principle but **relocated** in the hard direction via the `[locality-barrier]` descendant; structurally-but-unprovenly non-relativizing; **algebrization entirely unexamined**. Corrected statement: MCSP is a *candidate* route that *may* thread the barriers, with **no rigorous evasion proven on any of the three** and a descendant barrier already spawned. This is a genuine diagnostic contribution of the deep-dive — converting a hopeful "orthogonal/immune" framing into a falsifiable, weaker, sharper one.

## 5. Meta-complexity thesis: formal core vs unfalsifiable slogan `[thesis-formal-vs-slogan]`
The "meta-complexity thesis" has a real formal core — `[allender-hirahara-2019]` (circuit-size LBs ⟺ hardness of *relativized* MKTP^A under uniform-AC⁰ reductions; de-relativizing it is open) + the `[murray-williams-2017]` Goldilocks reduction-class chains. But the strong form ("the key to P vs NP") is a **programmatic slogan, not a single formal conjecture**, and is **not cleanly falsifiable** because new MCSP variants can always be proposed. The genuine falsifier is structural: close **both** formal P≠NP bridges — `[route-a-route-b]` route A (extend Murray-Williams' provable non-NP-hardness upward to uniform-AC⁰) **and** route B (the `[locality-barrier]`) — and MCSP is a dead end for P vs NP via the known formal bridges. The slogan-vs-formal asymmetry is itself a finding: the field would benefit from one canonical falsifiable conjecture rather than an open variant family.

## 6. Route A: resistance located as structural, not cryptographic `[route-a-dual-locality]` `[route-a-owf-escape]` (Route A push diagnosis)
The only direct P≠NP bridge in the portfolio is Route A `[route-a-route-b]`: NP-hardness of MCSP under **logtime-uniform AC⁰ many-one reductions** ⟹ NP ⊄ P/poly ⟹ P≠NP (Murray-Williams Thm 1.8 `[murray-williams-2017]`). The 2026-08-21 Route A push (sources/2026-08-21-routeA-push.md) located *where* this bridge's resistance lives, and the answer is diagnostic:
- **It is NOT cryptographic.** The obstructions that block the *strong* reduction rungs — the OWF obstruction `[mcsp-nphard-owf]` (targets *polytime* reductions) and the Mazor-Pass Levin negative `[mazor-pass-2024]` (targets *Levin/gap* reductions under iO) — sit *above* the uniform-AC⁰ many-one-on-exact-MCSP rung. Route A is plausibly below both crypto nets `[route-a-owf-escape]` (plausible, not proven — flagged). So the standard story "MCSP NP-hardness is blocked by one-way functions" does **not** obviously apply to Route A's weak reduction class.
- **It IS structural — a dual locality wall.** `[route-a-dual-locality]` The `[locality-barrier]` `[choprs-2020]` is recorded as blocking route B (the LB-proving/magnification side). The push surfaced that it is **dual**: the natural satisfiability-conditional gap constructions Route A needs are magnification-style, and CHOPRS show such constructions unconditionally produce the small-fan-in-oracle circuits that defeat the very LB techniques one would use to certify the gap. The single crack — CHOPRS Thm 49 (a non-localizable LB for an Andreev-like function, *not* MCSP) — proves such LBs *can* exist but not yet for any meta-computational problem.
- **The compression crux** `[route-a-compression-crux]`: the concrete structural obstruction is that a uniform-AC⁰ reduction must encode `n`-bit SAT-satisfiability into the circuit complexity of a function on only `O(log n)` bits — and a full construction is at least as hard as NP ⊄ P/poly (the breakthrough itself).
- **The variant gap** `[route-a-variant-gap]`: no MCSP variant is known NP-hard under uniform-AC⁰ many-one reductions; every achieved NP-hardness (Ilango `[ilango-2020]`, Masek, HOS) uses randomized/Turing/quasipoly-or-polytime reductions on restricted variants. The exact Route A class is open even for the restricted variants.
The diagnostic value: for the single direct P≠NP bridge, **do not spend effort defeating the OWF obstruction or Mazor-Pass** (they don't reach this rung); **do** attack the compression crux and the dual locality wall, with the Thm 49 non-localizable construction as the one proven proof-of-concept and three named falsifiable sub-targets (S1 derandomize Ilango's restricted-class reduction; S2 analyze the Masek reduction's depth/uniformity; S3 port Thm 49 to a meta-computational problem).

## 7. S1.a: a phantom derandomization mechanism, caught and re-located `[s1a-chebyshev-phantom]` `[s1a-lupanov-only-rng]` `[s1a-antiprox-obstruction]` (primary-source verification diagnosis)

## 8. S2 + the unifying diagnosis: the witness-needs-explicit-lb obstruction `[s2-irritable-witness-obstruction]` `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` (primary-source analysis diagnosis)

But its escape is purchased with an **existential** hard function f_hard (found by exhaustive search in 2^{O(N)} time; on m=log N bits, not 0.8-approximable by formulas of size 2^{(1−ε/2)m}). Porting the escape into a *logtime-uniform AC⁰* MCSP reduction needs f_hard **explicit** — an **explicit exponential (in m) formula LB**, open (best explicit **Ω̃(m³)** cubic: Håstad 1998 / Filmus-Meir-Tal 2023 / Dinur-Meir 2016 / Gál-Tal-Trejo-Nuñez 2019; the shrinkage exponent is tight, the "n³ barrier"), needed 2^{Θ(m)}. So the three S-sub-targets are the **same obstruction in three circuit models**: S1.a = **DNF** (explicit exponential DNF LBs *exist* → crux is the tight window + derandomization-of-the-union `[s1a-antiprox-obstruction]`); S2 = **general circuits** (best explicit Θ(L) linear, needed 2^{Θ(L)} `[s2-irritable-witness-obstruction]`); **S3 = formulas** (best explicit m³ cubic, needed 2^{Θ(m)}). All three require an **explicit exponential (in L=O(log n)) lower bound for a function on O(log n) bits** in their model; in all three the best explicit is *polynomial*, exponentially short of the exponential target. The S2/S3 symmetry is exact (linear-general ↔ cubic-formula, both polynomial, both vs exponential 2^{Θ(L)}).
- **The locality barrier and the witness barrier are distinct and orthogonal** `[locality-vs-witness-orthogonal]`. Thm 49 demonstrates the locality barrier is *not a logical impossibility* (a non-localizable LB exists) — a genuine CHOPRS advance — but the only known way to make an LB non-localizable is to hardwire an existential hard function whose *explicit* construction is itself the open circuit-LB problem. **Escaping locality ≠ escaping the witness barrier.** This refines §8's lumping of `[route-a-dual-locality]`: the locality barrier (LB *techniques* extending to small-fan-in oracle circuits) is one wall; the witness barrier (the explicit-exponential-LB need) is another; Route A is pinned by the *witness* barrier even where a locality escape (Thm 49) is in principle available. The locality escape is, in practice, paid for in exactly the witness barrier's coin.
The diagnostic value: a third verified negative result completing the S1.a/S2/S3 trio, plus a **barrier-orthogonality separation** — the survey had conflated "dual locality" with the witness obstruction; S3 shows they are different walls, and the locality wall (unlike the witness wall) has a known (existential) escape, which the witness wall precisely blocks from being made uniform. The methodological lesson, complementing §7–§8: **distinguish barriers** — an escape from one barrier (here, locality via Thm 49) can be real without helping, if its escape mechanism is a second barrier's load-bearing coin.

## 10. The non-amplification route is closed: the class drift lives in the decoder, not the XOR lemma `[non-amplification-also-class-drifts]` (Williams-side diagnosis, Cycle 3)
The second-pass [mining-worst-vs-average] named its "genuine open shape" as a *non-amplification* route to an average-case-hard MAJ∘MAJ function — bypassing the IW XOR-lemma amplification that drifts the predictor to XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ. Cycle 3 (sources/2026-08-23-nonamplification-route.md, verified against STV / CRTY / Goldreich-Rothblum) **closes that opening**: bypassing the XOR lemma does **not** bypass the class drift.
- **The drift is in the reconstruction's decoder, not the amplification.** STV `[stv-2001]` (PRGs without the XOR lemma) uses EITHER a pseudoentropy+extractor pipeline (pairwise-independent XOR + Trevisan extractor) OR algebraic list-decoding (multivariate polynomial encoding + Sudan univariate list-decoding + polynomial self-correction) — both **arithmetic/parity machinery**. CRTY `[crty-2023]`'s well-structured wc→ac is **arithmetization-based**. Every known worst-case→average-case conversion's reconstruction circuit lives in **(arithmetic/parity) ∘ (predictor's class)** — which **exceeds MAJ∘MAJ**, since MAJ∘MAJ provably lacks parity (IP₂ ∉ MAJ∘MAJ, HMPT `[hmpt-1993]`). So the XOR-lemma route drifts to XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ; the XOR-lemma-free routes drift to (arithmetic)∘MAJ∘MAJ. Either way the NW class-relativity is defeated.
- **Corroboration via the parity-containing-class pattern.** Goldreich-Rothblum `[goldreich-rothblum-2019]` achieve wc→ac precisely for classes that **already contain parity/arithmetic** (uniform AC⁰[2], counting-in-P) — and *not* for MAJ∘MAJ. The wc→ac machinery *needs* parity/arithmetic, so it works for classes that have it and drifts classes that lack it. This makes the drift a **robust, decoder-level** feature, not an amplification-specific lossiness one might engineer around.
- **Reduction.** The non-amplification route therefore offers no new lever; it reduces to the **same** Cycle-2 open shape: a **direct (non-reconstructive) exponential average-case-correlation LB for MAJ∘MAJ**, or equivalently a **locally-list-decodable code whose decoder is in MAJ∘MAJ** (known decoders need parity/polynomial ops — none in MAJ∘MAJ; such a code would itself be the breakthrough).
The diagnostic value: a verified **closure** of the survey's own suggested open shape — the second pass had optimistically proposed "avoid amplification" as the way out; Cycle 3 shows the obstruction is one level deeper (the decoder, not the amplifier), so the framing "amplification drifts the class" was if anything too weak. The methodological lesson, complementing §7–§9: when a route is blocked by a *mechanism* (here amplification), check whether the blockage is in the specific mechanism or in the *function the mechanism serves* (here, worst-case→average-case conversion's reconstruction) — if the latter, alternative mechanisms (XOR lemma → list decoding → arithmetization) fail for the same structural reason. This re-confirms `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` from a fourth angle: the only escape is a directly-constructed average-case-hard MAJ∘MAJ function, which is the circuit-LB problem.

## 11. The aggregation crux: the worst-case→average-case wall is one level below the decoder drift — the top threshold is a nonlinear aggregator `[threshold-aggregation-obstruction]` `[avg-worst-2x2-cell]` (Williams-side diagnosis, Cycle 4)
Cycle 4 (sources/2026-08-23-aggregation-crux.md, verified against HMPT / CLLO21 / Kumar CCC 2023) sharpens §10 one level deeper. A rough first-principles calculation (mine, initially unverified) appeared to **contradict** the Cycle-2 conclusion: applying the bound "agreement of f with a threshold of s low-discrepancy functions ≤ O(s·disc(f))" with disc(IP₂) = 2^{−n/2} gave, at *polynomial* size, agreement ≤ poly(n)·2^{−n/2} = 2^{−Ω(n)} — i.e. an **exponential** average-case-correlation bound at **full poly-size** MAJ∘MAJ, *exactly the mining target* (Cycle 2's open shape, which would already yield NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR + TC⁰₃). Under `[honest-ceiling]` a result of that magnitude demanded verification against the primary source. **Verified closed — and the reason is one level below §10's decoder drift:**
- **HMPT decomposed into two lemmas of different character.** Lindsey's Lemma (HMPT 3.4) is an *average-case (correlation)* bound — but at **depth 1 only** (any single threshold gate correlates with IP₂ ≤ O(weight·2^{−n/2})). The **Discriminator Lemma (HMPT 3.3) is worst-case** (a circuit that *exactly* accepts A / rejects B has a (1/a)-discriminator subcircuit). HMPT's exponential is avg(depth-1) **lifted by a worst-case lemma** to a **worst-case** depth-2 size LB. **There is no worst-case→average-case lifting step in the toolchain.**
- **The 2×2 grid — the mining target is the uncovered cell** `[avg-worst-2x2-cell]`. The Discriminator Lemma goes avg(C)→worst(MAJ∘C) (left→right, top row). CLLO21 `[cllo-2021]` (Thm 1) proves a *converse*: worst(MAJ∘C) ⟺ **avg(C)** (for C ⊆ NC¹ closed under negation, bottom O(1)-juntas) — but it returns to **avg(C)**, not avg(MAJ∘C); with C = depth-1 threshold it recovers Lindsey (circular). **Neither lemma lands in avg(MAJ∘C)** — the mining target (avg-case against MAJ∘MAJ = avg(MAJ∘C), C = depth-1) is the uncovered fourth cell of (avg/worst) × (C/MAJ∘C).
- **The reason the cell is uncovered: the top threshold is a nonlinear aggregator** `[threshold-aggregation-obstruction]`. Low per-gate (C) correlation does **not** bound MAJ∘C correlation — this is **boosting** (a majority of weak learners yields a strong learner). CLLO21 formalizes the MAJ∘C-vs-C gap via **SUM] (Approximate-Linear-Sum) gates** (MAJ simulates SUM], not conversely; standard black-box amplification fails for classes not closed under majority, and depth-1 threshold C is not). Kumar CCC 2023 `[kumar-2023]` demonstrates the obstruction concretely: biased threshold gates GC⁰(k) individually uncorrelated with parity yet, once the bias parameter k is large enough, a *sublinear-size* aggregation **computes parity exactly**. GHR92's correlation lemma (weight ~ 1/correlation) shows low per-function correlation *limits weight but does not prevent* aggregation — so aggregate correlation can be high once s ≳ 1/disc ≈ 2^{n/2}, exactly where HMPT's worst-case bound also kicks in. The exponential average-case bound at *poly* size does not follow; my rough calc's linear O(s·disc) bound is the wrong (linear) bound for a nonlinear top gate.
- **Diagnosis, sharpened one level below §10.** §10 located the obstruction at the **NW reconstruction's decoder** (arithmetic/parity machinery exceeding MAJ∘MAJ). §11 locates it one level **earlier**: at the **amplification / aggregation step itself**, independent of any NW reconstruction. Even before a worst-case LB is fed into an NW reconstruction, the step that would turn it into an average-case LB against the *same composed class* is blocked — by the circuit class's *own* aggregation power. The only escape remains a **direct** exponential average-case-correlation LB for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ — now with the obstruction's **mechanism** identified (defeat majority-aggregation / an anti-boosting correlation-structure bound for IP₂), not merely its location. This is the **fifth angle** confirming `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`.
The diagnostic value: a primary-source-verified **closure of a contradiction-flagged opening** (a rough calc that appeared to be the breakthrough, verified not to be), and a deeper placement of the obstruction (aggregation level, below the decoder level), with the mechanism (boosting/threshold nonlinearity) connected to a positive known phenomenon. The methodological lesson, complementing §7–§10: **a linear "threshold-of-low-discrepancy ≤ O(s·disc)" bound assumes the top gate aggregates linearly; a threshold is a nonlinear aggregator, and boosting is precisely the proof that linear aggregation bounds fail** — so any worst-case→average-case conversion that routes through a top majority must defeat the class's own amplification power, which is the circuit-LB problem.

## 12. The AC⁰ face is the thinnest, and the wall's blocking gate is the natural-proofs barrier located exactly — a correction to the first-loop synthesis `[ac0-lb-existence-passed]` `[ppoly-recognizable]` `[ac0-natural-proofs-wall]` `[ac0-tight-window]` `[parameterization-correction]` (meta-complexity diagnosis, 2nd-loop Cycle 1)

The diagnostic value: a *correction under `[honest-ceiling]`* — the first-loop synthesis's over-broad "polynomial best-explicit" claim was checked against the primary source and found inaccurate for the AC⁰ model — plus the natural-proofs frontier is no longer a vague backdrop but located **exactly**: the very tool that supplies the explicit exponential AC⁰ LB (the switching lemma) is (P/poly)-recognizable and therefore ruled out as the reduction's witness. Unification preserved + sharpened: `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` still governs all three faces, but the blocking gate differs — LB-existence (S2/S3) vs {non-naturality, tight-window} (S1.a). No breakthrough; the wall stands; the map is sharper.

## 13. Ilango's (P/poly)-recognizable barrier is strictly stronger than Razborov-Rudich — Williams' non-natural method is the wrong escape `[recognizable-stronger-than-natural]` `[largeness-vs-constructivity]` `[williams-constructive-theorem]` `[triple-conjunction-wall]` (meta-complexity diagnosis, 2nd-loop Cycle 2)
Cycle 1 (§12) located the AC⁰ face's blocking gate as the natural-proofs/(P/poly)-recognizability gate. Cycle 2 (sources/2026-08-23-recognizable-stronger-than-natural.md) asks the obvious breakthrough question: **is there any known *non-natural* LB method that crosses it?** The canonical exemplar is Williams' algorithmic method (NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰) — the LB widely held to *evade* the natural-proofs barrier. The answer is **no**, and the reason is itself a diagnostic:
- **Largeness vs constructivity — the two natural-proofs axes are different, and Ilango's barrier uses the wrong one to escape** `[largeness-vs-constructivity]` `[recognizable-stronger-than-natural]`. Razborov-Rudich needs constructivity ∧ largeness ∧ usefulness; you evade it by dropping **any one**, and the standard escape (Williams') drops **largeness**. Ilango's (P/poly)-recognizable condition (TR20-183 §1.4) needs only **constructivity ∧ usefulness — no largeness requirement**. So Ilango's barrier is **strictly stronger** than RR for the MCSP-reduction-witness setting: it has no largeness condition to drop. The standard escape from natural proofs (drop largeness) is the **wrong escape** — it leaves constructivity intact, and constructivity is exactly what Ilango's barrier forbids.
- **Williams' method is non-natural by dropping largeness, but is still (P/poly)-recognizable** `[williams-constructive-theorem]`. Verified (Williams, "Natural Proofs vs. Derandomization," SIAM J. Comput. 2016, Theorem 1.1): for typical C (incl. ACC⁰), NEXP ⊄ C ⟺ a P-time useful property against C **with O(log n) advice**; constructivity is **unavoidable** for NEXP LBs, and NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰ is non-natural by lacking **largeness**, not constructivity. The "O(log n) advice" = P/poly-computable ⇒ Williams' LB **is (P/poly)-recognizable**. So if Williams' method certified the reduction's witness g, the LB collection would be recognizable ⇒ algorithmizing it yields SAT ∈ subexp-circuits (a *collapse*, the opposite of separation) ⇒ **ruled out**, exactly like the switching-lemma LBs (§12). The one celebrated natural-proofs-evading LB does not cross Ilango's gate, because it evades the wrong condition. To cross the AC⁰ face's gate one must drop *constructivity* — i.e. produce a genuinely non-constructive LB.
- **The wall as a triple conjunction; non-recognizable ↔ non-constructive in tension** `[triple-conjunction-wall]`. The S1.a witness must satisfy **three** conditions simultaneously: {non-(P/poly)-recognizable ∧ AC⁰-constructible explicit g ∧ tight-window}. No known method achieves even the first two — switching lemma {✗-recognizable, ✓-constructible, ✓-tight-ish}, Williams {✗-recognizable (Thm 1.1), ✗-NEXP-not-constructible, ✗-coarse}, counting {✓-non-recognizable, ✗-no-explicit-f, ✗-coarse}. The central tension: Gate 1 (non-recognizable) and constructibility are in **near-definitional conflict** — a non-recognizable method is non-constructive (counting) ⇒ no explicit witness; a constructive method (switching lemma, Williams, per Thm 1.1) ⇒ recognizable ⇒ ruled out. The witness demands a method that is simultaneously non-constructive (to pass Gate 1) *and* constructive-of-an-explicit-g (to be reduction-usable).
The diagnostic value: the wall's gate (§12) is shown to be **harder than natural proofs** — even the canonical escape from Razborov-Rudich (Williams' largeness-drop) misses it, because the gate is the *constructivity* axis. The genuine-open shape is now maximally precise: **an explicit, AC⁰-constructible function g with a non-(P/poly)-recognizable tight-window AC⁰ LB**, where whether "non-recognizable" and "explicit-constructible" are even simultaneously achievable is itself **open** (Thm 1.1 makes them equivalent for NEXP, hinting at tension, but the explicit-f regime is uncovered). The methodological lesson, complementing §7–§12: **distinguish a barrier's axes** — "non-natural" (drops largeness, Williams) and "non-constructive / non-recognizable" (drops constructivity, what Ilango's gate actually forbids) are different escapes, and the escape that defeats Razborov-Rudich can be impotent against a barrier that never imposed the axis it drops. **Honest scope:** no breakthrough; Thm 1.1 is web-confirmed (search-summary-level, not PDF-line-verified); the non-recognizable↔non-constructive tension is a heuristic (sound for NEXP per Thm 1.1, unproven for the explicit-f regime the S1.a witness lives in), not a theorem.

## 14. The non-recognizable↔constructible tension is empirical, not definitional — the constructivity-forcing is NEXP-specific and absent for explicit f `[tension-not-definitional]` `[constructivity-forcing-nexp-specific]` `[easy-witness-mechanism]` `[recognizer-needs-mcsp]` `[wall-loosened-not-fallen]` (meta-complexity diagnosis, 2nd-loop Cycle 3)
Cycle 2 (§13) characterized the S1.a wall as the triple conjunction {non-(P/poly)-recognizable ∧ AC⁰-constructible explicit g ∧ tight-window} and called Gate 1 (non-recognizable) and constructibility a **"near-definitional"** tension. Cycle 3 (sources/2026-08-23-tension-not-definitional.md) tests that claim — Cycle 2's own next-step directive — against the literature, and **corrects it downward**:
- **The constructivity-forcing is NEXP-specific** `[constructivity-forcing-nexp-specific]` `[easy-witness-mechanism]`. Williams Theorem 1.1 (NEXP ⊄ C ⟺ a P/log n useful property — constructivity unavoidable) runs through the **Easy Witness Lemma** (Thm 3.1), built on IKW 2002 (NEXP ⊆ P/poly ⟹ NEXP has easy witnesses). The mechanism is the **nondeterministic-witness structure**: a nondeterministic class with small circuits HAS its witnesses as small circuits, which a diagonalizer detects. **Deterministic / explicit functions have no such witness structure**, so the easy-witness forcing has no lever. The S1.a witness is an explicit (AC⁰-constructible) function, **not** a nondeterministic class — so Williams' theorem does **not** force its LB to be recognizable. Cycle 2 applied an NEXP theorem to an explicit-function setting it does not cover; the inference "explicit-f LB ⇒ recognizable" is an empirical pattern, not a theorem.
- **Corroboration that the forcing stays at the nondeterministic level (and is delicate for AC⁰).** CWY ITCS 2023 `[cwy-2023-blackbox]`: black-box constructivity unavoidable for **NEXP**; **Theorem 1.6 — no deterministic polylog(N)-constructive property useful against AC⁰** (randomness essential; the AC⁰ constructivity axis is delicate). Dhayal-Impagliazzo 2020 `[dhayal-impagliazzo-2020-unique]`: UEXP forcing via **unique** (anti-largeness) properties — retains constructivity, stays at the nondeterministic-class level. No analog forces constructivity for an explicit function.
- **"Explicit g ⇒ recognizable" is false** `[recognizer-needs-mcsp]`. A natural worry: if g_n is explicit, is the LB collection S = {(g_n, T_n)} trivially (P/poly)-recognizable (compute g_n, compare)? No. Ilango's recognizer must **accept all of S AND reject ALL YES-instances of (C)-MCSP** (all easy functions). Accepting g_n is easy; **rejecting all easy functions requires solving MCSP-complement** — the hard part, *independent* of g's explicitness. So an explicit g does NOT automatically trip the barrier; the barrier is on the LB *method* yielding a P/poly separator from easy functions, a separate object from g. A non-recognizable LB is compatible with explicit g.
- **The wall is reclassified, not removed** `[wall-loosened-not-fallen]`. From Cycle 2's "near-paradox (non-constructive yet explicit)" to "requires a genuinely new LB method certifying a specific explicit function hard *without* a P/poly-checkable separator from the easy functions." Still hard — no such method is known — but **not ruled out by the constructivity-forcing theorem** (its mechanism is absent in the explicit-f regime). The tension is EMPIRICAL: every known explicit-f LB method happens to be constructive (switching lemma, diagonalization); every known non-recognizable method (counting) happens to give no explicit f.
The diagnostic value: a *self-correction under `[honest-ceiling]`* — Cycle 2's word "near-definitional" was checked against the primary mechanism (easy-witness lemma) and found too strong; the correction is *downward* (a more accurate negative, not a positive). The methodological lesson, complementing §12–§13: **a "tension" backed by a theorem that is specific to a higher complexity class does not transfer to a lower one** — Williams' constructivity-forcing is an NEXP statement via nondeterministic easy-witnesses; the S1.a witness lives in the explicit-f regime where that forcing is absent, so "the wall is a near-paradox" is a framing overreach. The genuine breakthrough shape (a non-recognizable explicit-f tight-window AC⁰ LB) is now certified *possible in principle* (not blocked by the constructivity-forcing theorem), while remaining *not achieved* (blocked by the empirical absence of a method and by Gate 2). **Honest scope:** no breakthrough; Williams/CWY/Dhayal-Impagliazzo web-confirmed (search/arXiv-summary-level, not PDF-line-verified); the "loosening" is a more-accurate *framing* correction, NOT a loosening of the wall itself; the boundary case (an explicit g also in a nondeterministic class, e.g. NP∩coNP) is unaddressed.

## 15. The boundary case: g ∈ NEXP, so the single-function LB implies the class LB — the wall pins to Williams' (=>) targetability `[boundary-case-resolved]` `[single-lb-implies-class-lb]` `[williams-iff-vicinity]` `[targetability-circularity]` `[wall-pinned-to-targetability]` (meta-complexity diagnosis, 2nd-loop Cycle 4)

Cycle 4 (sources/2026-08-23-boundary-case-targetability.md) observes that the S1.a witness **is** such a function — g is AC⁰-constructible ⊆ P ⊆ NP ⊆ NEXP — and tests whether this re-tightens the wall:
- **The single-function LB entails the class LB** `[single-lb-implies-class-lb]`. The S1.a LB is therefore **not disjoint** from the class-LB regime Williams' theorem governs — it sits inside it, even though (Cycle 3) its *proof mechanism* (explicit-f) is outside the easy-witness forcing.
- **A recognizable property is guaranteed in the vicinity** `[williams-iff-vicinality]`. Williams Thm 1.1 (⇒) is an IFF: class LB ⟺ a (P/poly)-recognizable useful property exists.

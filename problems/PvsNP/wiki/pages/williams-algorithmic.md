# Williams Algorithmic Method

## Core
Meta-theorem `[williams-2011]`: if a circuit class C has a SAT algorithm running in 2ⁿ / n^{ω(1)} (barely faster than brute force), then some function in NEXP is not in C. Williams built such an algorithm for ACC⁰ and proved **NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰** — non-relativizing (oracles hide the hard language) and non-natural (uses diagonalization + a SAT algorithm, not a truth-table property).

## Why ACC⁰ works
ACC⁰ → SYM⁺ via Yao-Beigel-Tarui depth reduction → a low-degree probabilistic polynomial over a finite ring (AND/OR are "nearly F-easy", polylog degree; MOD gates are constant degree) → SAT testable in 2^{n−n^{Ω(1)}}. The crux: **AND/OR have polylog approximate degree**.

## The n² wall (dead for the literal trick)
Extending to THR∘THR / TC⁰ breaks `[n2-wall]`: threshold gates have F_p-approximate degree Θ√t `[alman-williams-2015]` (tight), not polylog; Beigel-Tarui modulus amplification is useless for real-valued thresholds. Composed degree Θ√(mt) is **tight** for THR∘THR (contains OR∘AND), forcing ≥2ⁿ monomials at n² wires. **This is a theorem about the class, not the construction** — refining the single-polynomial representation cannot yield NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR. Two barrier-breaking attempts (Littlewood-Offord boundary decomposition; sign-pattern enumeration) collapsed to the same wall. The ACC⁰ trick is provably the wrong tool past n² wires.

## Live frontiers (the program is not the trick)
- **Probabilistic-rank axis** `[lss-2025]`: extend ACC0∘3-PTF → ACC0∘k-PTF for k=4,5… climbing PTF degree toward TC⁰.
- **Geometric axis** `[chen-2018-maxip]` (deepened — see sources/2026-08-21-maxip-push.md): a polylog shaving — `n²·poly(d)/log^{ω(1)} n` for ℓ₂-Furthest-Pair / Z-Max-IP / Hopcroft at **polylog d** (or `n²/log^{ω(1)} n` for Max-IP at d=n^ε) — ⟹ NEXP ⊄ poly-size THR∘THR, via Chen's THR∘THR structure lemmas + Williams easy-witness. **Proven-correct sufficient condition; structurally the highest-leverage target** because it needs only a polylog shave while SETH rules out only polynomial (`n^{2−ε}`) improvements. **But the shave is harder than "missing":** the five natural routes are each blocked — the polynomial method's `n^{2−1/Õ(√c)}` exponent `[acw-maxip]` collapses at polylog d and is too small to drive the LB reduction; word-RAM gives only O(1) logs; rectangular matrix mult lands on the wrong side; CRT powers the lower bound, not an algorithm; and the **approximate-then-round-to-exact route is provably blocked by SETH** (`[chen-2020-maxip-tight]`: additive o(d) approx in subquadratic time refutes SETH) `[maxip-shave-obstruction]`. **Reframed live target** `[majmaj-capp-route]` — **corrected 2026-08-21** (see sources/2026-08-21-majmaj-push.md): Chen-Williams `[chen-williams-2019]` tightened the bridge to **derandomization** — a non-trivial CAPP for poly-size **THR∘MAJ**, or `1/poly`-error CAPP for **MAJ∘MAJ**, suffices (not a SAT algorithm). **The SAT→CAPP weakening (and the PCPP `AND2∘C/OR2∘C/⊕2∘C` drop) is genuine and escapes SETH.** But the *MAJ∘MAJ vs THR∘THR* distinction is **illusory on the derandomization axis** `[majmaj-symmetry-deflated]`: Chen-Williams prove a `1/poly`-error CAPP for MAJ∘MAJ **iff** for THR∘THR — the classes are **CAPP-equivalent**, so MAJ's symmetry yields no PRG advantage (a special MAJ-only PRG would *violate* the iff). MAJ∘MAJ does **not** escape the `[n2-wall]` on either axis (approximate degree shared with THR; CAPP-equivalent). Both natural PRG mechanisms are bounded away `[dilv-2024]`: small-bias+noise fails threshold tests (Derksen-Ivanov-Lee-Viola 2024, Thm 9); k-wise+noise fools only *single* symmetric functions and does not lift to the non-symmetric MAJ∘MAJ composition (Thm 7). **The genuine constructive opening is the mining program** `[mining-program]` `[hmpt-1993]` `[chen-ren-2020]`: convert the known HMPT exponential lower bound (IP2 ∉ 2^{(1/2−ε)n}-size depth-2 threshold) into a non-trivial CAPP via an NW reconstruction for threshold classes. Payoff (Chen-Ren Thm 1.13): a `2^n/n^{ω(1)}` CAPP for MAJ∘MAJ ⟹ **NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR *and* NEXP ⊄ TC⁰₃**. **Within-reach quantitative lever** `[tell-2018-quantified]`: Tell's quantified derandomization already gives a q.d. PRG for depth-2 threshold at `n^{3/2−Ω(1)}` wires, seed `Õ(log n)`, for the *promise* setting; the open improvement is the wire-count constant (the `c>1` in `n^{1+c^{-d}}`, = the `[chen-tell-2019]` bootstrap) — a *promise* derandomization, not full CAPP. **Mining seam VERIFIED 2026-08-21** (see sources/2026-08-21-mining-program-seam.md): the *direct* NW-from-HMPT route to a MAJ∘MAJ CAPP is blocked at **worst-case vs average-case** `[mining-worst-vs-average]` — HMPT is a worst-case LB; NW/IW needs average-case hardness; converting via the IW XOR-lemma amplification incurs (a) overhead, (b) **class drift** (the amplified XOR-of-copies predictor lives in a *deeper* class XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ, and MAJ∘MAJ is not closed under the top XOR, so the hardness drifts away from the class one fools), and (c) a need for a *well-structured* (downward-self-reducible, sample-aided-wc-to-ac reducible) hard function (Chen-Rothblum-Tell-Yogev JACM 2023) that HMPT does not provide. The known Williams connections go **CAPP→LB, not LB→CAPP** (an average-case MAJ∘MAJ LB is itself the open problem). This is why Tell's *promise*/wire-count relaxation is the tractable seam — it lowers the witness requirement rather than meeting it. **Refinement (2026-08-21, second pass, verified against primary sources — see sources/2026-08-21-mining-program-seam.md):** "worst-case vs average-case" is *too blunt*. Known average-case (correlation) LBs for depth-2 threshold **do** exist — CSS16 `[css-2018]` (Parity correlation ≤ n^{−ε_d} with depth-d threshold of ≤ n^{1+ε_d} wires; depth-2 tight) and Kane-Williams `[kane-williams-2016]` (Andreev average-case, superlinear-gate/superquadratic-wire) — but they are **few-wire (n^{1+ε}) / superquadratic** and **polynomial-correlation (n^{−ε})**, not full-poly-size / exponential. Conversely the **exponential** bounds (HMPT, Forster) are **worst-case SIZE**, not average-case **correlation**: HMPT uses discrepancy (bounds correlation with *rectangles*; a size-s MAJ∘THR circuit is *richer than s rectangles*, so discrepancy does NOT bound its correlation — at s=2^{Ω(n)} the correlation ~s·2^{−n/2} is not small); Forster uses sign-rank (depth-1 / unbounded-error, doesn't lift to depth-2). So the precise obstruction is a **quantitative+regime gap**: NW/Chen-Ren for a full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ CAPP needs average-case hardness at full poly-size with ≤1/poly → after NW predictor loss, **exponential average-case-correlation** (2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ, which is **open** (exponential bounds are worst-case-size; average-case bounds are few-wire/polynomial). **This confirms Tell's lever IS the CSS16 wire frontier** — CSS16's average-case LB lives exactly at the few-wire (n^{1+ε}) regime = Tell's promise regime, and Tell's open wire improvement (n^{1+exp(−d)} → n^{1+O(1/d)}) is the *same question* as extending CSS16's correlation LB to more wires. The genuine-open shape for a breakthrough: an exponential average-case-correlation LB for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ (open; ⟹ mining ⟹ NEXP ⊄ THR∘THR + TC⁰₃), or pushing the CSS16/Tell wire frontier. **Third pass (2026-08-23, Cycle 2 — verified, see sources/2026-08-23-wire-frontier-push.md): the gap is TWO-DIMENSIONAL** `[mining-gap-two-dimensional]` — correlation-strength × wire-regime. The two known average-case data points both fall short on BOTH dimensions: CSS16 Parity (depth-2) is **wire-tight at n^{1.5}** (depth-2 with O(√n) gates/O(n^{1.5}) wires approximates Parity well — Parity cannot give an average-case LB past n^{1.5}); CSS16 Generalized Andreev (depth-d) is **subexponential** correlation (exp(−n^{ε_d}), not 2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) at **n^{1+ε_d} wires** (few-wire). The NW target needs **exponential (2^{−Ω(ℓ)}) correlation at full poly-size** — short on both axes from every known point. Parity is the wrong function to push past n^{1.5} (wire-tight); one needs Gen-Andreev or a new function crossing BOTH dimensions. **Anchoring sharpened:** the worst-case exponential for IP₂ exists only for *restricted-weight* depth-2 (MAJ∘THR: HMPT Ω(2^{(1/3−ε)n}) `[hmpt-1993]`; THR∘MAJ: Forster Ω(2^{n/2}/poly); THR∘SYM: Amano `[amano-2020]` Ω((1.5−ε)ⁿ)) — for *unrestricted* THR∘THR the worst-case exponential is itself OPEN (Ω(n) trivial; Amano/Kane-Williams Open Q #2). So **MAJ∘MAJ (HMPT worst-case anchor present) is the only viable mining target**; the CAPP-equivalence means a MAJ∘MAJ CAPP automatically serves THR∘THR, but THR∘THR direct targeting would first need the (open) worst-case anchor. Recent progress (Chen-Tal-Wang STOC 2025 `[chen-tal-wang-2025]`: f ∈ E^NP needs n^{2.5−ε}-size THR∘THR, worst-case) uses the Williams algorithmic method in the **CAPP→LB direction** (opposite of mining; XOR-of-two specialized estimator, not a general CAPP; f ∈ E^NP not NW-usable). **Fourth pass (2026-08-23, Cycle 3 — verified, see sources/2026-08-23-nonamplification-route.md): the non-amplification route is CLOSED.** `[non-amplification-also-class-drifts]` The second pass's suggested "genuine open shape — a non-amplification route" (avoiding the IW XOR-lemma that drifts to XOR∘MAJ∘MAJ) does NOT escape the class drift: STV `[stv-2001]` (PRGs without the XOR lemma, via pseudoentropy+extractor OR algebraic list-decoding) and CRTY `[crty-2023]` (well-structured arithmetization-based wc→ac) both use **arithmetic/parity decoder machinery** (polynomial encoding + Sudan list-decoding + self-correction; pairwise-independent XOR + extractors) that **exceeds MAJ∘MAJ** — which provably lacks parity (HMPT `[hmpt-1993]`). The drift is **not XOR-lemma-specific**; it lives in the reconstruction's decoder, present in *both* amplified and amplification-free pipelines. Corroborated by Goldreich-Rothblum `[goldreich-rothblum-2019]`: known wc→ac reductions work only for classes that **already contain parity/arithmetic** (uniform AC⁰[2], counting-in-P), not MAJ∘MAJ. So the non-amplification route offers no new lever; the genuine-open shape reduces to a **direct** (non-reconstructive) exponential average-case-correlation LB for MAJ∘MAJ, OR a locally-list-decodable code whose decoder is in MAJ∘MAJ (equivalent; none known — known decoders need parity/polynomial ops). **Fifth pass (2026-08-23, Cycle 4 — verified, see sources/2026-08-23-aggregation-crux.md): the obstruction is one level BELOW the decoder drift — at the amplification/aggregation step itself.** `[threshold-aggregation-obstruction]` `[avg-worst-2x2-cell]` A rough first-principles calc (agreement of a threshold of s low-discrepancy functions ≤ O(s·disc), disc(IP₂)=2^{−n/2}) appeared to give exponential average-case correlation at *poly* size — the mining target, contradicting the field; verified **closed**. Decomposing HMPT against the primary source: Lindsey's Lemma (HMPT 3.4) is an **average-case (correlation) bound at depth 1 only**; the **Discriminator Lemma (HMPT 3.3) is worst-case** (exact accept-A/reject-B), lifting avg(C)→worst(MAJ∘C) — never avg(MAJ∘C). CLLO21 `[cllo-2021]` proves a *converse* (Thm 1: worst(MAJ∘C) ⟺ avg(C), for C⊆NC¹ closed under negation) but it returns to **avg(C)**, not avg(MAJ∘C). The mining target — avg-case against MAJ∘MAJ = avg(MAJ∘C) with C=depth-1 — is the **uncovered fourth cell** of the (avg/worst) × (C/MAJ∘C) grid `[avg-worst-2x2-cell]`. The reason the linear O(s·disc) bound cannot reach it: the **top threshold is a nonlinear aggregator** — boosting shows low per-gate correlation does NOT bound majority correlation (CLLO21 SUM]-gate gap; standard amplification fails for classes not closed under majority, and depth-1 threshold C is not); Kumar CCC 2023 `[kumar-2023]` demonstrates it concretely (small-bias threshold gates individually uncorrelated with parity yet aggregate to *compute* it once the bias parameter is large). GHR92's correlation lemma (weight ~1/correlation) shows low per-function correlation *limits weight but does not prevent* aggregation — so the aggregate correlation can be high once s ≳ 1/disc ≈ 2^{n/2}, exactly where HMPT's worst-case bound also kicks in; the exponential average-case bound at *poly* size does not follow. This is the **fifth angle** confirming `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]`: the only escape remains a **direct** exponential average-case-correlation LB for full-poly-size MAJ∘MAJ — now with the obstruction's *mechanism* identified (defeat majority-aggregation / an anti-boosting correlation-structure bound for IP₂), not merely its location. **Unification** `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` (now spanning both live routes): the mining-program obstruction and the meta-complexity S1.a/S2 obstruction are the SAME wall from opposite sides — both need an **explicit average-case / lower-bound-carrying witness for a restricted class**, which a cheap probabilistic/worst-case input supplies only insufficiently and whose explicit construction IS the circuit-lower-bound problem (natural-proofs frontier).

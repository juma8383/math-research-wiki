## K1  p^2Y_q = 2Y_pR_q
  v_p: p^2 | R_q (i.e. v_p(R_q) = 2+v_p(Y_q) >= 2).
  v_q: v_q(Y_p) = 0 (since v_q(Y_q)=0=v_q(R_q)).
  Mod-8 GATE (Lemma A below): p|R_q requires p==1 mod 8 AND chi_p(q)=chi_p((2+sqrt2)/4).
  So K1 vacuous for p==5 mod 8 (half of pairs). p^2|R_q DOES occur (99 hits q<=1e5).
  No size kill: R_q < q^2 is compatible with p^2 | R_q.

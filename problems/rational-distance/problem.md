# Rational Distance Problem

> facts flagged `[to-verify]`. Source: unsolvedproblems.org/index_files/RationalDistance.htm.

## Statement
Is there a point in the plane at rational distance from all four corners of
the unit square? (A nontrivial such point, off the obvious symmetry locus.)

## Status
**OPEN.** (Erdős–Moser formulation.)

## Frontier (one line)
No example known; partial results rule out large families (Rav 2014, Kreisel–
Kurpisz); the existence is open. Trivial points at half-distance on the
diagonal exist for degenerate variants.

## Control-step framing (one line)
Resolution on a slice (points ruled out / degenerate solutions) → control =
existence-or-nonexistence for the full problem — a Diophantine-geometry
control step; the obstruction is the simultaneous rationality of four
distances (a rationality control, sibling to [[perfect_cuboid]]).

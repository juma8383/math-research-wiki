### The load-bearing honesty caveat (publication ≠ solution-acceptance)

This upgrade is **weaker** than the BSD/NS upgrades, and the honesty
framing must be sharper, for three reasons:

1. **Venue tier.** IJGMMP is a legitimate, indexed, peer-reviewed
   physics journal, but it is a **mid-tier** venue — NOT a top
   mathematical-physics journal (CMP / Inventiones / JAMS / Annals),
   which is where a Millennium-Prize-level construction would be
   expected to land. Trans. AMS (Kim) and FoCM (Hou) are stronger
   venues for *much* weaker claims (a structure theorem / a
   generalized-model blowup). Here the **claim is far stronger** (full
   4D YM construction + proven continuum mass gap = the Millennium
   problem) and the **venue is far weaker** — the evidence-strength /
   claim-strength ratio is inverted relative to BSD/NS.
2. **Clay non-acceptance + no independent verification.** The Clay
   Mathematics Institute has **not** formally accepted or verified any
   proof; the broader mathematical-physics community has **not**
   independently verified the claims through the standard process a
   Millennium solution demands. "Peer-reviewed in IJGMMP" is an
   *editorial* bar, not a *community-acceptance* bar.
3. **Substantive caveats unaddressed by publication.** The
   attempt-04 flags — the **admissible-class framework** and the
   **RG-interlacing defect summability** — are *technical* gaps that
   mere peer review at a mid-tier venue does not resolve; they await
   independent expert scrutiny.

So the flag is **renamed, not removed**: the *publication-status*
flag is resolved (peer-reviewed IJGMMP 2026), but a new
*substantive-acceptance* flag is raised — Clay non-acceptance + no
independent verification + mid-tier venue + unresolved technical
caveats. **Crossing the editorial bar ≠ crossing the
community-acceptance bar**, especially for a Millennium claim.

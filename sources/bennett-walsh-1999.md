# Bennett–Walsh 1999 — at most one solution to b²X⁴−dY²=1

VERIFIED VERBATIM 2026-09-03 (abstract + Thm 1.2 + intro from the AMS PDF).

- **[bw1999-at-most-one]** If $b,d>1$ are given positive integers ($b>1$),
  $b^2X^4-dY^2=1$ possesses **at most one** solution in positive integers
  $X,Y$; when it exists, it is explicitly characterized via fundamental
  units of associated quadratic fields. Thm 1.2 form: for squarefree
  $b,d>1$, at most one index $k$ with $T_k=bx^2$ ($T_k$ = Pell sequence),
  $k=\alpha(b)$ the divisibility index.
- **[ljunggren-quartic-lineage]** Ljunggren 1942: $X^4-dY^2=1$ has at most
  two positive solutions (sharp: $d=1785$, $(13,4)$ and $(239,1352)$);
  Cohn's sharpening (only $X^2=a$, $X^2=2a^2-1$ candidates) recorded.
- **[bw1999-scope-one-param]** SCOPE: one-parameter Pell-type quartics.
  NOT applicable to two-variable leaf quartics
  $u^2$ (or $9u^2$) $=c_1r^4+32r^2s^2+c_2s^4$; the wiki's K34 leaf
  insolvability needs Tzanakis-type elliptic-logarithm machinery or
  2-descent instead (notes.md §2i). A 2026 arXiv preprint
  (2603.05809, Walsh) surveys the modern elementary method of Lin–Luo;
  preprint status flagged.

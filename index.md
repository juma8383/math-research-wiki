## Problems
- [Beal's Conjecture](problems/beals-conjecture/problem.md) — no pairwise-coprime $A^x+B^y=C^z$ with $x,y,z\geq3$; famous open, $1M prize; in-progress. **Frontier: signature (3,5,7).**
- [Beal — State of the attack (synthesis)](problems/beals-conjecture/synthesis.md) — the five-thread obstruction map distilled from attempts 01–08; cross-session reference.
- [Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture](problems/birch-swinnerton-dyer/problem.md) — $r_{\text{alg}}=r_{\text{an}}$ + refined leading-coefficient for $E/\mathbb Q$; Clay Millennium; in-progress. **Frontier: analytic rank $\ge2$ + refined formula.**
- [Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness](problems/navier-stokes/problem.md) — global smooth 3D incompressible solutions OR a finite-time blowup counterexample; Clay Millennium; in-progress. **Frontier: global regularity for large 3D data.**
- [Yang-Mills existence and mass gap](problems/yang-mills/problem.md) — rigorous 4D quantum YM (Wightman/OS axioms) with mass gap Δ>0; Clay Millennium; in-progress. **Frontier: continuum-limit + IR-gap control (dimensional transmutation).**
- [Hodge Conjecture](problems/hodge-conjecture/problem.md) — every Hodge class a Q-linear combination of algebraic-cycle classes; Clay Millennium; in-progress. **Frontier: codimension-2 Hodge classes on a 4-fold.**
- [Collatz Conjecture](problems/collatz-conjecture/problem.md) — every $n$ under $n/2$ (even) or $3n+1$ (odd) reaches 1; famous open; in-progress. **Frontier: pointwise convergence (no divergent trajectory + no nontrivial cycle).**
- [P vs NP](problems/PvsNP/problem.md) — $\mathrm P\overset{?}{=}\mathrm{NP}$; Clay Millennium; complexity theory (the one non-number-theory/PDE problem in the wiki). Self-contained nested `wiki/` with 28 cycles / 7 loops. **Frontier: $\mathrm{NP}\not\subseteq\mathrm{P/poly}$; the obstruction `[witness-needs-explicit-lb]` = an open non-compositional construction (A).** See [progress.md](problems/PvsNP/progress.md) (read-first).
- [Poincaré Conjecture](problems/poincare-conjecture/problem.md) — every simply connected closed 3-manifold is $S^3$; Clay Millennium. **SOLVED — Perelman 2002–03 (Ricci flow with surgery; the only solved Millennium problem).** The wiki's *control-step contrast case*: the one problem where the 7-for-7 obstruction (control, not resolution) was actually discharged. See [progress.md](problems/poincare-conjecture/progress.md) (read-first).
- [Riemann Hypothesis](problems/riemann-hypothesis/problem.md) — all non-trivial zeros of $\zeta(s)$ have $\Re(s)=\tfrac12$; Clay Millennium; **OPEN** (the one Millennium problem that was missing from the wiki). **Frontier: every zero on the line — verified to $T=3\cdot10^{12}$ (Platt–Trudgian 2021); Guth–Maynard 2024 broke Ingham's 1940 zero-density record; the obstruction is the control step (slice/average → every zero).** Two-avatars structure = the [[birch_swinnerton_dyer]] twin (function-field RH proven Weil/Deligne / number-field RH open). 2026-08-31 hunt: proportion record $\ge0.6725$ (Alpöge–Furman arXiv:2608.13637, Lean-4-formalized, non-journal) + a **proved bandwidth-one certificate ceiling $p_0\le0.6818287$** (`[rh-bandwidth-ceiling-verified]`, §7.2 verbatim-checked) — the certificate class cannot certify above $\approx0.682$: the ceiling is the control step made quantitative. See [progress.md](problems/riemann-hypothesis/progress.md) (read-first).

- [Gross-Zagier-Kolyvagin theorem (BSD for analytic rank ≤ 1)](theory/theorems/kolyvagin-gross-zagier.md) — Heegner-point height ↔ $L'(E/K,1)$ (GZ) + Euler-system Selmer bound (Kolyvagin); BSD rank + Sha-finiteness proven for $r_{\text{an}}\le1$; one-point shape stops it at rank 1.

- [Collatz density / almost-all results (Terras, Allouche, Korec, Krasikov–Lagarias)](theory/theorems/collatz-density-results.md) — a.a. $\mathrm{Col}_{\min}<N$ (Terras, natural density), $<N^\theta$ ($\theta\downarrow0.79$, Allouche/Korec), count $\gg x^{0.84}$ (Krasikov–Lagarias); the average-case / density resolution layer.

## Theory — Methods
- [Pairwise-coprime reduction](theory/methods/pairwise-coprime-reduction.md) — $\gcd(A,B,C)=1 \iff$ pairwise coprime for any $A^x+B^y=C^z$; Beal's clean form.
- [Exponent reduction to {odd primes}∪{4}](theory/methods/exponent-reduction.md) — suffices to rule out prime/4 exponents only.
- [Frey curve / modularity / level lowering](theory/methods/frey-modularity.md) — the FLT engine; the mixed-exponent barrier is the Beal crux.
- [abc-conjecture finiteness argument](theory/methods/abc-finiteness.md) — abc gives only finiteness (= Darmon–Granville); does NOT prove Beal.
- [Level-lowering obstruction for pairwise-distinct odd-prime signatures](theory/methods/frey-level-lowering-obstruction.md) — provable block: only $\ell=2$ strips all bases; why (3,5,7) and the whole distinct-prime class resist the classical method.
- [Darmon's program (Frey abelian varieties of GL2-type)](theory/methods/darmon-program.md) — abelian-variety successor to Frey curves; repeated-exponent only; gated on the wide-open generalized-Mazur irreducibility conjecture.
- [Mordell-curve lens (cubic sum of two cubes)](theory/methods/mordell-curve-lens.md) — verified $x^3+y^3=N \leftrightarrow Y^2=X^3-432N^2$; reframes gap-1 vs gap-0 as integral points; cubic-only (genus 1), dies at genus ≥2.
- [Infinite descent (FLT n=3,n=4) and why mixed exponents break it](theory/methods/infinite-descent.md) — 3 requirements (factorization, power-match, UFD); each fails for mixed/distinct exponents; (3,5,7) fails at step 1.
- [Even-exponent reduction to spherical signatures (the (3,4,5) route)](theory/methods/spherical-reduction.md) — even exponent $2k\to2$ exposes a spherical $(2,\cdot,\cdot)$ signature that is parametrized; $(3,4,5)\to(2,3,5)$; doubly gated, (3,5,7) has no even exponent so the reduction cannot be written.
- [Triangle-group / nonabelian descent (the (2,3,7) route and its scope limit)](theory/methods/triangle-group-descent.md) — PSS effective descent via finite quotient $\mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb F_7)$ of the *infinite* $\Delta(2,3,7)$ needs near-spherical position ($\chi\approx0$) + an exponent $2$ ($X(7)$ modular interpretation); (3,5,7) deeply hyperbolic + no $2$ → unavailable; Darmon–Granville covering descent exists but is ineffective.
- [Counting heuristic for generalized Fermat equations](theory/methods/counting-heuristic.md) — *heuristic, not a theorem*: expected solution count $\sim H^{r\chi}$; predicts finiteness for $\chi<0$ (parallels Darmon–Granville), monotone sparsity (matches the $29\to77$ gap growth), and why $(3,3,3)$ ($\chi=0$, borderline) forced the modular engine; gives finiteness, not zero.
- [Heegner-point Euler system (Kolyvagin) and its rank-1 shape limit](theory/methods/heegner-point-euler-system.md) — the BSD rank-≤1 engine: construct Heegner points, GZ-bridge to $L'$, Euler system bounds Selmer; **one-dimensional shape** bounds a rank-≤1 Selmer group only — the central obstruction to BSD at rank ≥2; candidate higher-rank systems (Beilinson-Flach, Kato derivatives, higher Heegner points).
- [Energy estimates, scaling, and the supercriticality obstruction](theory/methods/energy-supercriticality.md) — the NS unifying lens: energy (subcritical $L^2$) is the only global bound; regularity needs the critical $L^3$; Serrin index $S_{\text{nonlin}}=d+1$ vs $S_{\text{lin}}=d/2+2$ equal in 2D (solved) and $4>3.5$ in 3D (supercritical, open); Tao triple-log rate quantifies the gap.
- [Constructive QFT continuum limit, OS reconstruction, gap transport](theory/methods/constructive-continuum-limit.md) — the YM reusable engine: lattice definition → RG control (Balaban + finite-range decomposition) → cluster/polymer expansion → OS reconstruction → gap transport uniform in $a$; the open control steps (convergence + $O(4)$ covariance, uniform gap transport, Gribov) ARE the obstruction; dimensional-transmutation lens.
- [The analytic→algebraic bridge (Hodge obstruction lens)](theory/methods/analytic-algebraic-bridge.md) — the Hodge unifying lens: exponential-sequence + GAGA + Picard variety bridge Hodge classes→algebraic divisors for $p=1$ (works); for codim ≥2 the Griffiths intermediate Jacobian is transcendental and does not control algebraicity — the one-codimension engine stops; analytic-vs-algebraic instance of the control-step obstruction.
- [Average-vs-pointwise control (the Collatz obstruction lens)](theory/methods/average-vs-pointwise-control.md) — the Collatz unifying lens: average-contraction heuristic ($3/4<1$, $\mathbb E[k]=2>\log_2 3$) is distributional over parity sequences; density results (Terras→Tao) control a.a. but not the measure-zero exceptional set; "almost all → every $N$" is the gap; the canonical exemplar of the control-step obstruction.
- [Cycle exclusion via linear forms in logarithms (Collatz, Beal-flavored)](theory/methods/cycle-exclusion-linear-forms.md) — a Collatz cycle forces the linear form $\Lambda=(K+L)\log2-K\log3$ exponentially small; transcendence lower bounds (Laurent–Mignotte–Nesterenko, Rhin) rule out $m\le75$ but degrade for large $m$; the Diophantine/transcendence sub-problem echoing Beal.
- [Two-avatars control step (function-field proven / number-field open)](theory/methods/two-avatars-control-step.md) — the sharpest cross-problem lens: RH and BSD are each *the same statement* proven over $\mathbb F_q$ (Weil/Deligne; function-field BSD) and open over $\mathbb Q$, because the discharging tool (Frobenius + Rosati positivity; Euler systems + étale cohomology) has no known char-0 translation — the parallelism upgraded to an exact avatar question; Hodge's standard conjectures are the shared Rosati-type positivity.
- [RH positivity equivalences (Weil / Li / Bombieri–Lagarias / Suzuki)](theory/methods/rh-positivity-equivalences.md) — four *exact* sign-control compressions of RH (Weil distribution positivity; Li $\lambda_n\ge0$; B–L Selberg-class form; Suzuki 2023 screw-function/Kreĭn kernels); the isolated control step — none dischargeable, and the bandwidth-one ceiling $p_0\le0.6818287$ is the proved limit of the finite-compression engine on the Weil form.
- [Necessary-window heuristics](theory/methods/necessary-window-heuristics.md) — reusable sharpening of probabilistic null-models by algebraically forced narrow windows: MSS partner window ($H_2$ 13× sharpened; $E_{A3}\approx3\cdot10^{-6}$) and Brocard root-of-unity window ($E(n)=2^{\pi(n)+1}\sqrt{n!}/n!\to0$ superexponentially) — two independent attacks, one method shape; window part is theorem, equidistribution part is model.

## Conjectures
- [Fermat–Catalan conjecture](theory/conjectures/fermat-catalan.md) — finiteness across signatures (may include a 2); distinct from Beal; 10 known solutions all have a 2.
- [Generalized Hodge Conjecture (Grothendieck, coniveau)](theory/conjectures/generalized-hodge.md) — coniveau-$r$ Hodge substructures come from cohomology supported on codim-$\ge r$ algebraic subsets; usual HC = GHC at $k=2r$; Hodge's original stronger form false; coniveau 1 known, coniveau ≥2 open.
- [Odd–odd Pillai-2 conjecture](theory/conjectures/odd-odd-pillai-2.md) — $X^u{-}Y^v{=}2$ has no solutions with $u,v$ both odd primes; a named restriction of the open $k{=}2$ Pillai case (Waldschmidt survey); verified to $Y^v\le10^{18}$, everywhere locally soluble (Catalan-like); exactly the −1 unit-base gap-1 channel of every Beal-open signature (attempt-25/T2).
- [Corner Principle](theory/conjectures/corner-principle.md) — the genuine min gap $G(p,q,r)$ of every all-distinct-odd-prime signature is attained at $C\le3$; verified 56/56 signatures (primes ≤23), failure boundary at granularity exponent $\gamma\le5/3$ (open class $\gamma\ge3.267$); mechanism = super-cubic spacing of attainable sums (attempt-25).
